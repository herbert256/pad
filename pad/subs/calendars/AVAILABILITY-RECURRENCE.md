# Availability and working days

`padCalendarAvailability(windows, busy, timezone, minimumMinutes)` subtracts occupied half-open intervals from the union of working windows, independently by resource. Intervals use explicit ISO timestamps and offsets, so DST folds and cross-midnight windows remain unambiguous. Adjacent windows merge; adjacent bookings do not remove extra time. At most 2000 windows/bookings per input, a minimum from 1 to 10080 whole minutes, and an IANA display timezone are accepted. The view uses `windows=`, occupied `data=`, `timezone=` and `minimum=` and renders free intervals as a readable time list. It does not reserve a resource. Unknown busy resources affect no other resource. Empty working windows mean closed.

`businessday` recurrence accepts `weekend=['SA','SU']` and `holidays=['2026-12-25']` supplied by the application. Holidays are explicit civil dates, not a jurisdiction inferred from timezone. `interval` counts accepted business days, `count` counts selected occurrences after business-date exclusions; ordinary recurrence COUNT behavior remains unchanged. The start may fall on a nonworking day and the first accepted business day is selected. Holiday data with a partial-day closure must be represented as availability intervals; only whole-day holidays should enter the exclusions here.

Monthly rules accept `byday=['2MO','-1FR']` or weekday names with `setpos=[-1]`. Yearly rules support these within explicitly selected `month` values. The RRULE subset adds ordinal BYDAY and BYSETPOS for monthly/yearly rules. BYDAY combined with BYMONTHDAY, yearly BYDAY without BYMONTH, BYSETPOS without BYDAY, yearly BYSETPOS with multiple BYMONTH values, and ordinal weekly BYDAY are rejected. DTSTART must match monthly/yearly filters. Existing scan/output limits, DST skipped-time omission, first-fold occurrence policy, COUNT/UNTIL exclusivity and exclusions remain in force.

Run `php tests/subsystems/application-calendars.php`; `application-calendars-example.php` prints example occurrences. The calendar availability and independent fixed-time clock examples are in `apps/tags/examples/`.
