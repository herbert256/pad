<p><code>{barcode}</code> encodes its first parameter as a barcode and writes it as inline SVG -
the bars of a product, a ticket, a carton or a parcel label, or the squares of a Data Matrix,
drawn on the server, no font or image needed. It is the codes subsystem of PAD
(<code>pad/codes/</code>): a symbology is a file of <code>pad/codes/types/</code>, and
<code>type</code> names it.</p>

<h3>The symbologies</h3>
<ul>
  <li><b>Products</b> - <code>ean13</code>, <code>ean8</code>, <code>upca</code> and
  <code>upce</code>: digits with a check digit of weights 3 and 1, which may be left off - it
  is then computed and added - and is checked when given. The guard bars are drawn longer and
  the digits set under the bars in their groups. A UPC-E stands for a UPC-A with its zeros
  left out; given the 11 or 12 digits of a UPC-A, <code>upce</code> writes its short form
  when it has one.</li>
  <li><b>Text</b> - <code>code128</code> takes printable ASCII and packs a run of four digits
  or more two to a symbol; <code>code93</code> takes printable ASCII too, the characters
  Code 39 lacks as pairs; <code>code39</code> digits, capital letters, space and
  <code>- . $ / + %</code>, with <code>check</code> for its check symbol.</li>
  <li><b>Digits</b> - <code>itf</code> (Interleaved 2 of 5, <code>check</code> for its check
  digit), <code>itf14</code> (the GS1 number of a carton, between bearer bars),
  <code>codabar</code> (digits and <code>- $ : / . +</code> between the start and stop letters
  A to D, A at both ends when the text has none) and <code>msi</code> (a Luhn check digit, or
  <code>check='mod1010'</code> or <code>'none'</code>).</li>
  <li><b>Two-dimensional</b> - <code>qr</code>, the code of <a href="?tag/qr">{qr}</a>, and
  <code>datamatrix</code>, Data Matrix ECC 200 in the smallest of its 24 square sizes: any
  text - one beyond ASCII marked as UTF-8 (ECI 26) - <code>size</code> in pixels in place of
  <code>height</code>, <code>scale</code> and <code>plain</code>.</li>
</ul>

<p>Without <code>type</code> the tag decides by the text: twelve or thirteen digits are an
EAN-13, anything else Code 128. The barcode type writes a symbology by its name,
<code>{barcode:upce '0425261'/}</code>, and so does the name alone,
<code>{itf14 '1540014128876'/}</code>, when no tag, store, field or sequence has it.</p>

<p><code>scale</code> is the width of the thinnest bar; keep it at 2 or more for a printed
label a scanner has to read. <code>plain</code> leaves the human-readable text out. The
colours are fixed - black on white unless <code>color</code> and <code>background</code> say
otherwise - since a scanner needs the contrast.</p>

<p>A wrong check digit, a wrong length for the type, a character the symbology cannot hold, a
text too long for it or an unknown type is an error under the strict check, and the tag writes
nothing.</p>
