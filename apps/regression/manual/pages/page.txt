<p>A nested pass is the engine run inside itself: the content of a <code>{pad:code}</code>
pair, or the page a <code>{pad:page}</code> tag names, is rendered as a complete PAD run
of its own, and what it produced lands in the surrounding page. The pass sees the request it
runs in - its variables, its stores - unless it asks for isolation.</p>
