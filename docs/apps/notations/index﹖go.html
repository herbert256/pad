Go

A go position from a game record in SGF, the Smart Game Format: the board of 9, 13 or 19 lines with its star points and coordinates, the stones set up, then the moves played out - a group without liberties is taken off the board, as the rules take it - the last move ringed or every move numbered. Labels and marks of a diagram are drawn too.

Written {notation:go '(;SZ[9]AB[cc];W[gg])'/} or with the record between the tags; numbers, move=, plain, size=.

Stones taken off

Stones set up, then five moves: Black fills the last liberty of the white stone at the top edge, which comes off the board; the last move is ringed.

Template 
{notation:go size=300}
  (;SZ[9]AB[cc][gc]AW[ea][cg]
  ;B[da];W[gg];B[fa];W[ge];B[eb])
{/notation:go}
Result

A game, numbered

The opening moves of a game on the small board, every stone with the number of the move that put it there; move= shows the position after an earlier move.

Template 
{notation:go numbers, size=280}
  (;SZ[13]GN[A game on thirteen lines]
  ;B[dj];W[jd];B[jj];W[dd];B[gd];W[gj];B[kh];W[ck])
{/notation:go}
{notation:go move=4, size=280}
  (;SZ[13];B[dj];W[jd];B[jj];W[dd];B[gd];W[gj];B[kh];W[ck])
{/notation:go}
Result

A diagram with marks

A corner of the full board set up as a diagram: the labels A and B on two points, a triangle on a stone; plain leaves the coordinates out.

Template 
{notation:go plain, size=320}
  (;SZ[19]AB[pc][qc][rd][re][qf]AW[qd][qe][pd][oc][nd][pf][qg][rf]
  LB[sd:A][sc:B]TR[re])
{/notation:go}
