{tab} is the item of the five widgets that take a list of labelled parts: {tabs} (a tab and its panel), {accordion} (a <details> with the label as its summary), {carousel} (a slide with the label as its caption), {wizard} (a step) and {lightbox} (a picture with its caption). It prints nothing where it stands: its content is rendered, trimmed, and handed to the nearest of those owners above it, which builds its markup from the items once its own content has rendered.

Because the hand-over goes to the nearest owner and not to the direct parent, a tab may stand inside an {if}, or inside a loop that makes a tab per row of data: {tabs}{plans}{tab $name}...{/tab}{/plans}{/tabs}. The owners are widgets, reached by their bare names after every other kind of tag; {tab} is a built-in tag, so a row with a field named tab still makes tabs.

Under the strict check a {tab} outside any owner is an error, as is one that never closes, and a tab of a {tabs}, an {accordion} or a {wizard} without a label. A carousel slide and a lightbox picture may go without a label; they then have no caption.
