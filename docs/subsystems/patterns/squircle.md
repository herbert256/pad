# squircle

Repeated superellipses. `exponent=2` gives a circle, 4 a squircle, up to 10 a nearly square tile. `density` is the occupied fraction of the repeat (0.1–1). Each tile has one 96-vertex closed path; cardinal points land exactly at the bounds. Touching tiles at density 1 meet without spilling outside the repeat.

`{pattern:squircle size=48, width=480, height=240/}`

Standard colors, opacity, title and SVG/data-URI options apply. Geometry is deterministic and seed-independent. No per-pixel or per-area shape growth.
