Clocks

The time three ways at once: a clock face with a sweeping second hand, a binary clock whose columns of lights are the digits of the hours, minutes and seconds, and a word clock that lights up the words - it is twenty past four. Drag the minute hand to set the clocks to another time, run them fast to watch a day go by, Now puts them right. show= which of them, 'analog, binary, words' unless given; zone= the time zone they tell, the visitor's own unless given - Europe/Amsterdam, America/New_York ...
