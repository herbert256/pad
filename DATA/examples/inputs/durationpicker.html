A length of time, an hour and a half, a lap of 3 minutes 12: a native number field that posts the duration as a whole number of seconds - 5400 for 1 h 30 min, whatever is shown - and where the script runs a box per part, hours, minutes and seconds, each a spin button: typed into, ArrowUp and ArrowDown a unit more or less of the whole duration - 59 minutes and one more is an hour - Page Up and Page Down ten, Home the part to 0, End to its most. parts= the parts shown, a run of d h m s - 'hms' unless given, 'hm', 'dhm', 'ms' ... - the largest without a bound, so a duration runs past 24 hours, the smallest the step: parts='hm' posts whole minutes, in seconds. min= and max= are seconds, and so are value= and the rules: rules='integer|between:60,7200'. Without the script the field takes the seconds typed, its step the smallest part's.
