
<p><code>{nocache}</code> ... <code>{/nocache}</code> inside a <code>{cache}</code> section -
or on a page the page cache keeps - renders on every request: the section is stored with the
part as its source, and on a hit the stored rendering comes back with the part rendered again.
A part inside a loop of the section gets the fields of its row back as they were when it was
stored. Where nothing caches, the content renders as any content does.</p>

<p>In a section the part renders where the section stands, with the page's fields. A
page-cache hit runs no PHP - not the page's, nor an <code>_inits.php</code> - so there a part
sees the request and session values, the <code>_lib</code> functions and the application's
tags. The CSRF token or the <code>{nonce}</code> in a part no longer keeps a section out of the
fragment cache. A page-cache entry with parts is never answered with a 304 from the entry
alone, nor renewed by a hit. <code>{nocache}</code> takes no parameter.</p>
