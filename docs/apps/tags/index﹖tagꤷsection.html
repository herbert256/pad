Tags/Layouts and components/section:*

{section:*}

A block of a landing page - 24 of them, from a hero, the features and the prices to the questions, a newsletter form and the footer - in pure CSS.

Layouts and components Either form

Syntax 

{section:kind 'heading', ...} ... {/section:kind}
{section:kind title='heading', ...} ... {/section:kind}
{section:hero title='heading', button='text', link='?page', image='picture.jpg', alt='text'} text {/section:hero}
{section:features title='heading', columns=3} {section:item 'name', icon='zap'} text {/section:item} ... {/section:features}
{section:pricing data='plans'/}
{section:pricing} {section:item 'name', price='€12', period='month', featured} a feature a line {/section:item} ... {/section:pricing}
{section:faq open=1, single} {section:item 'question'} answer {/section:item} ... {/section:faq}
{section:cta 'heading', button='text', link='?page'/}
{section:newsletter 'heading', button='Subscribe'/}
{section:contact 'heading', mail='...', phone='...', address='...', form/}
{section:footer brand='name', note='small print'} {section:item 'column'} Label | ?page {/section:item} ... {/section:footer}
{section:comparison plans='name, name', featured='name'} {section:item 'feature', values='yes; no'/} ... {/section:comparison}
{section:changelog} {section:item 'version', date='2026-10-30', added='a; b', fixed='c'} text {/section:item} ... {/section:changelog}
{section:downloads} {section:item 'file', link='?file', size=2457600, checksum='sha256:...'/} ... {/section:downloads}
{section:agenda timezone='CET'} {section:item 'title', from='09:00', to='10:00', track='name'/} ... {/section:agenda}
{section:blog data='posts'/}
{section:highlights} {section:item 'title', image='picture.jpg', alt='text'} text {/section:item} ... {/section:highlights}
{section:hero} {pad:slot 'eyebrow'} ... {/pad:slot} {pad:slot 'actions'} ... {/pad:slot} {/section:hero}

Parameters

kind after colon
The section, a file of pad/subs/sections/types/: agenda, blog, careers, casestudy, changelog, comparison, contact, cta, downloads, faq, features, footer, gallery, hero, highlights, item, logos, newsletter, pricing, roadmap, stats, steps, team, testimonials. The section: prefix selects it - {section:hero} - and that prefix is always written: a section is never reached bare, as hero, team and footer are the names of fields and pages.
heading
The section's own first value, used after {section:hero}: its heading, as title=; for {section:item} the item's name - a plan, a question, a person.

Options

title
The heading; also the first value. A {pad:slot 'title'} of markup wins over it.
eyebrow
The small line above the heading.
text
The line under the heading - the content between the tags wins over it, and a {pad:slot 'text'} over both.
button
hero, cta: the text of the main button, with link the page it goes to; newsletter, contact: the text of the form's button.
second
hero, cta: a quieter second button, with secondLink.
image
hero: a picture beside the words, alt its text - a {pad:slot 'image'} of markup in its place.
note
hero, cta, pricing, newsletter: the small print; footer: the line under it all.
data
A list section - features, pricing, testimonials, faq, stats, steps, team, logos, gallery, footer, comparison, changelog, downloads, agenda, blog, highlights: its items from the rows of a store, the page's array or a _data file, by their fields.
columns
The cards side by side at the widest, 1 to 6 - fewer as the section narrows.
open
faq: the question open at first, by its number; bare, every question. single keeps one open at a time.
form
newsletter: the form's name (newsletter); contact: bare for a message form, or its name. thanks is what shows once it came back right.
mail
contact: the mail address, a link; phone, address and hours the rest of the details.
brand
footer: the name at its side.
plans
comparison: the plans, a column each - else the fields of the rows of data; featured the plan in the accent.
timezone
agenda: the clock the times are on, said under the heading; tracks the tracks in their order.
level
The heading's level, 1 to 6 (2); the items' headings one more.
tone
plain, soft (the accent's light shade) or accent (the accent itself) - the background.
align
center or left.
id
The section's id, to link to it - ?page#pricing.

Description

{section:hero} puts a block of a landing page on the page - a hero, the features, the prices, what customers say, the questions, a call to action, the numbers, the steps, the team, the logos, a newsletter form, the contact details, a gallery or the footer. Every section is a file of pad/subs/sections/types/, its style in www/assets/sections/; the files of types/ are the sections there are. It is HTML and CSS only - the questions open and close by themselves, the forms post back without a script.

The section is selected by its prefix and name: {section:hero title='...'}. The heading is its first parameter, or title=.

Its parts come three ways, the first that has one winning: a {pad:slot 'name'} pair directly in its content, an option of the part's name, and - for the text under the heading - the rest of the content. A slot is template: it renders where the section stands, a field of the loop around it, a tag. A section that lists things - features, plans, quotes, questions, numbers, steps, people, logos, pictures, the footer's columns - takes its items from the rows of data=, by their fields, and from {section:item} pairs inside it: the item's first value is its name, its options its fields, its content its text - a plan's features a line each, a question's answer.

The newsletter and the contact form post back to the page as {input:form} does - the session's token and the form's name, so the page's PHP asks padPosted ( 'newsletter' ) and checks the post with padValidate; the fields refill and show the messages, and once the post came back right the section says thanks in the form's place.

Each section is a size container: its layout follows the room it has, not the window. The colours are --pad-section-bg, -panel, -text, -soft, -dim, -line, -accent, -accent-text and -accent-soft, falling back to the frame's palette, so a theme colours them; --pad-section-hero, -radius, -max, -font and -heading-font the rest. The style is written once per page with the CSP nonce.

A hero

The heading, the text between the tags, two buttons - and the eyebrow from a slot.

{section:hero 'Pages that drive themselves', button='Start building', link='?index', second='Read more', secondLink='?tag/section'}
  {pad:slot 'eyebrow'}New in PAD{/pad:slot}
  Write the page, and the page fetches its data, runs its logic and renders itself.
{/section:hero}

Questions from rows

data= names the rows - a question and an answer each - and the first stands open.

Templateexamples/section/3-faq.pad 
{format:auto 'questions'}
[ { "question": "Is it free?", "answer": "Yes, under the GPL." },
  { "question": "Does it need JavaScript?", "answer": "No - the questions open by themselves." } ]
{/format:auto}
{section:faq title='Questions', data='questions', open=1/}
