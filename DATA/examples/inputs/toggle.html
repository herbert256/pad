A switch, on or off: a native checkbox with role="switch", drawn as a switch by its style alone, so it needs no script. It posts a value either way - on= when it is on, 1 unless given, and off= when it is off, 0 unless given, from a hidden field before it that the checkbox's own value replaces when it is ticked - so the page's PHP finds the field in every post, and accepted holds the switch to on when on= is 1, on, yes or true - for another on= the rule is in:, rules='in:dark'. off='' posts nothing when off, as a checkbox does. checked turns it on before a post; after one it is as it was posted.
