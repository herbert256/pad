Dates

Invoices and their due dates: days added to a date, the weekday of the due date with TEXT and WEEKDAY, a due date in the weekend moved to the Monday, the end of the month with EOMONTH, a reminder a month later with EDATE and the days late with DAYS.

3 2024-002 2024-02-01 14 2024-02-15 Thursday 2024-02-15 2024-02-29 2024-03-15 2024-02-20 5
4 2024-003 2024-03-04 30 2024-04-03 Wednesday 2024-04-03 2024-04-30 2024-05-03 2024-04-08 5

6 2024-005 2024-04-15 30 2024-05-15 Wednesday 2024-05-15 2024-05-31 2024-06-15 open

{sheet:grid 'invoices',
  formulas='D2:D6=B2+C2, E2:E6=TEXT(D2,"dddd"), F2:F6=IF(WEEKDAY(D2,2)>5,D2+8-WEEKDAY(D2,2),D2),
            G2:G6=EOMONTH(F2,0), H2:H6=EDATE(F2,1), J2:J6=IF(I2="","open",MAX(0,DAYS(I2,F2)))',
