Tags/Holidays/holidays:jp

{holidays:jp}

Japan: the national holidays of the Act on National Holidays.

Holidays Either form

Syntax 

{holidays:jp year=2026/}

Description

One of the 32 holidays of holidays:*, written {holidays:jp year=2026/}.

Japan: the national holidays of the Act on National Holidays (国民の祝日に関する法律) and the one-off holidays of their own acts, with its two rules of days off: a holiday on a Sunday is made up for on the next day that is no holiday yet (振替休日, since 12 April 1973 - the next day until 2006, when no two holidays followed each other, so the same days), and a day between two holidays is one too (国民の休日, since 1986: 4 May from 1988 to 2006, and 22 September when Respect for the Aged Day falls on the 21st). The Happy Monday moves of 2000 and 2003, the holidays of the emperors - the birthday on 29 April, 23 December, 23 February, 2019 without one - and the Olympic moves of 2020 and 2021 are rules of their years. The equinox days are fixed each February for the next year by the National Astronomical Observatory's almanac; here they are the common formula of the Japan Coast Guard's calendar reckoning (1900 to 2099), which gives every equinox day the Cabinet Office lists, so the file knows the years from 1955 to 2099 - a date after 2027 is the formula's, which the almanac has yet to confirm. Checked by a script against the Cabinet Office's list of every national holiday and day off since 1955 (syukujitsu.csv): all 1067 dates from 1955 to 2027 agree, and every equinox day from 1955 to 2099 is the day of the equinox in Japan Standard Time by Meeus' algorithm - the closest call the autumn of 2074, three minutes after midnight.

Example

Public holidays

Use region= for regional rules or countries= for several calendars with provenance.

Template 
{holidays:jp year=2026/}
