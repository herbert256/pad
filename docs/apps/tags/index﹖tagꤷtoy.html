Tags/Games/pad:toy

{pad:toy}

A small live simulation or visual toy in the browser - 19 of them, from the Game of Life and a flock of birds to the Mandelbrot set and falling sand.

Games Either form

Syntax 

{toy 'name'/}
{toy 'life', pattern='gun', wrap/}
{toy:mandelbrot iterations=500/}
{boids birds=200/}
{sorting race/}
{life}
  .O.
  ..O
  OOO
{/life}
{lsystem}
  axiom: X
  X: F[+X]-X
  angle: 25
{/lsystem}

Parameters

name
The toy, a file of pad/toys/types/: ant, automaton, boids, clocks, fireworks, flowfield, galton, harmonograph, kaleidoscope, life, lsystem, mandelbrot, orbits, pendulum, ripples, sand, sorting, spirograph, starfield. The toy: type names it as well - {toy:life/}, or bare, {life/}, when nothing else has the name.

Options

seed
A whole number: what the toy picks at random is the same on every load. Without it the browser picks.
title
The name the toy is shown under, else its own.
paused
Bare option: the toy waits for its Play button, as every toy does when the visitor asks for reduced motion.
width
life (64), sand (160): the cells across; height the cells down.
speed
How fast it runs: generations, steps or rows a second (life, ant, automaton, sorting), or a level of 1 to 10 (boids, flowfield, orbits; starfield 1 to 20).
pattern
life: glider, spaceship, pulsar, gun, pentadecathlon, rpentomino, acorn, diehard, random or empty - or a pattern of your own between the tags.
rule
life: B3/S23 and its like; ant: the turns, RL, LLRR ...; automaton: Wolfram's number, 0 to 255.
wrap
Bare option, life: the edges of the grid join.
algorithm
sorting: bubble, cocktail, insertion, selection, gnome, shell, comb, quick, merge, heap or radix; race runs them all side by side.
iterations
mandelbrot: how long a point is followed (300); lsystem: how often the word is rewritten.
palette
mandelbrot: ocean, fire, rainbow or mono.
birds
boids: how many (150); separation, alignment and cohesion weigh the three rules, 0 to 10.
pendulums
pendulum: how many swing side by side, each started a hundredth of a degree apart (1); angles the start.
system
orbits: solar, binary, eight or swarm. An lsystem names its system as its first value: plant, dragon, koch, hilbert ...
setup
ripples: two, slits or pond.
show
clocks: analog, binary and words - which of them; zone a time zone.
mirrors
kaleidoscope: how many wedges (8).
levels
galton: the rows of pegs (12); balls how many, bias the chance of going right in percent.

Description

{toy} puts a small live simulation or visual toy on the page, run in the browser: the server checks the options it is given and writes its frame, and the toy's script - written once per page, with the CSP nonce - animates it. Every toy is a file of pad/toys/types/, its script and its style in pad/toys/assets/; the files of types/ are the toys there are.

A toy is written three ways, the same toy each time: the tag with the toy's name, {toy 'life'/}; the toy: type, {toy:life/}; and the bare name, {life/}, when no tag, store, field or sequence has that name. The pattern of a Game of Life and the rules of an L-system stand between the tags, one a line - taken as written, before the level walks them, so a field or a tag there is not filled in.

Every toy can be touched - draw cells, drag a bob, throw a planet, stir the current - and has labelled controls and a status line; its canvas has role="img" and a label that says what it shows. A toy holds while the page is hidden or it is scrolled out of view, and waits for its Play button when the visitor asks for reduced motion or the tag says paused. Its root carries data-state: playing, paused or held.

seed= makes what a toy picks at random the same on every load. The colours are --pad-toy-ink, -canvas, -accent, -night and their like on .pad-toy, light and dark; the script resolves them for the canvas and again when the scheme changes.
