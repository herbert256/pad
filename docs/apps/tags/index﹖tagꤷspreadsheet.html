Tags/Widgets/spreadsheet:*

{spreadsheet:*}

A grid of cells whose spreadsheet formulas are computed on the server, as a table - formats, totals and cells to edit.

Widgets Either form

Syntax 

{pad:spreadsheet 'name'} Item,Price ... Tea,=B2*2 {/pad:spreadsheet}
{pad:spreadsheet 'name', data='rows', formulas='total=B2*C2', totals='total'/}
{pad:spreadsheet 'name', formats='B:0.00, D:€ #,##0.00', headers=FALSE, caption='Caption'/}
{pad:spreadsheet 'name', editable='B2:C9', seed=7} ... {/pad:spreadsheet}

Parameters

name
The sheet's name: its edited cells post as padSheet[name][B2]. Unnamed, a sheet is sheet1, sheet2 ... in the order of the page.

Options

data
The cells: records under a first row of their field names - their values are never formulas - a list of rows of values, or cells by name. As a pair the content is the cells, a line a row, a comma between cells.
formulas
Formulas put in: total=B2*C2 fills a column down every row below the first, D2:D9=B2*C2 a range, F1=SUM(D:D) one cell.
totals
A row below the last with the SUM of each column named - its letters or the name in its first row.
formats
A format code per column, cell or range: B:0.00, total:€ #,##0.00, B5:B8:0.0%, as TEXT writes them.
headers
The column letters and the row numbers; headers=FALSE leaves them out.
caption
The table's <caption>.
editable
Cells to edit: bare, every number, date and logical value; or the cells and ranges named. An edit posts back and the sheet is computed again.
seed
The seed of RAND, 0 when not given: the same numbers on every request.

Description

{pad:spreadsheet} computes a grid of cells on the server and writes it as a table, the column letters and row numbers around it. A cell starting with = is a formula in spreadsheet syntax - =B2*C2, =SUM(D2:D9), =VLOOKUP(E2,$I$2:$J$6,2) - read by a parser of its own and computed with the functions of pad/subs/tables/formulas/types/, which answer what Excel answers; the cells are worked through in the order the formulas need them, and a circle of references answers #REF! in each of its cells. The cells come from the content - a line a row, a comma between cells - or from data=.

Formulas, totals and formats

formulas= puts formulas in: a name fills a column down the rows, the formula being the one of row 2 and its references moving with each row, as a spreadsheet's fill moves them; a range is filled from its first cell; a cell takes one formula. totals= adds a row of sums. formats= gives a column, a cell or a range a format code - 0.00, #,##0, 0%, yyyy-mm-dd; a cell typed as 12% or 2024-01-15 keeps that look without one, and a formula takes the look of its first cell. Numbers stand right, errors are marked and explained in their title, and every formula is in its cell's title and data-formula.

Cells to edit

editable makes cells fields. In a {input:form} they post with it; in a {pad:live} region a change posts the region and it comes back computed, without a script of the page's own; anywhere else the sheet is a form of its own with a Recalculate button. Under the strict check a formula of the template that cannot be read is an error naming its cell and the place in it.

Examples

Cells as content

A line a row, a comma between cells; a cell starting with = is a formula, computed on the server, its column formatted.

Templateexamples/spreadsheet/1-shop.pad 
{pad:spreadsheet 'shop', formats='B:€ #,##0.00, D:€ #,##0.00', totals='D'}
  Item,Price,Qty,Total
  Apples,1.20,3,=B2*C2
  Pears,0.80,5,=B3*C3
  Plums,2.25,2,=B4*C4
{/pad:spreadsheet}
Resultopen alone
A B C D
1 Item Price Qty Total
2 Apples € 1.20 3 € 3.60
3 Pears € 0.80 5 € 4.00
4 Plums € 2.25 2 € 4.50
5 Total € 12.10

Rows and a computed column

The rows of data= under their field names; formulas= fills a new column down the rows, as a spreadsheet fills one.

Templateexamples/spreadsheet/2-rows.pad 
{pad:data 'scores'}
  name,test1,test2
  Anna,92,88
  Bram,61,70
  Chen,78,82
{/pad:data}
{pad:spreadsheet 'scores', data='scores', formulas='mean=AVERAGE(B2:C2), grade=IF(D2>=75,"pass","retry")', formats='mean:0.0'/}
Resultopen alone
A B C D E
1 name test1 test2 mean grade
2 Anna 92 88 90.0 pass
3 Bram 61 70 65.5 retry
4 Chen 78 82 80.0 pass

Cells to edit

editable makes the numbers fields; outside a form or a live region the sheet is a form of its own and Recalculate computes it again on the server.

Templateexamples/spreadsheet/3-editable.pad 
{pad:spreadsheet 'loan', editable, formats='B4:€ #,##0.00'}
  Amount,10000
  Interest,5%
  Years,3
  Monthly,=PMT(B2/12,B3*12,-B1)
{/pad:spreadsheet}
Resultopen alone
A B
1 Amount
2 Interest
3 Years
4 Monthly € 299.71
