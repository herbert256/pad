<p class="nt-lead">Text in flag semaphore: a figure for every letter, holding two flags - red and
yellow, as they are flown at sea - at two of eight positions around the body. The positions go
round the circle as the code has them: A to G one arm down and the other going round, H to N
from the first position, and on; J and V break the pattern. Digits follow the numeral sign as
the letters A to K, and letters after digits follow J.</p>

<p class="nt-tag">Written <code>{semaphore 'PAD'/}</code>; <code>size=</code> is the width of a
figure.</p>
