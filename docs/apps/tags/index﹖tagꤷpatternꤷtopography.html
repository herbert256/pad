Tags/Patterns/pattern:topography

{pattern:topography}

The contour lines of a landscape the seed makes up.

Patterns Single tags

Syntax 

{pattern:topography seed=7/}

Description

One of the 29 patterns of pattern:*, written {pattern:topography seed=7/}.

The contour lines of a landscape the seed makes up: value noise of three octaves, its hills the size across, traced at levels= heights (12) by marching squares, every fifth line the bolder index contour in the second ink.

The noise: a lattice of random heights a size apart, eased between its points (3t² - 2t³), and two finer lattices of half and a quarter of that, at half and a quarter the weight, spread over 0 to 1 by the lowest and the highest point sampled. The heights are sampled on a grid of a twelfth of the size (3 to 12 pixels); each contour is the line of marching squares through that grid, a saddle decided by the average of its four corners. A line's points are the crossings on the edges of the grid, so two cells that share an edge share its point exactly, and the pieces are joined into one path a line - closed where the line closes, ending on the border where it leaves.

{pattern:topography seed=7, levels=16/}
