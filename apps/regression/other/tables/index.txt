        <p class="sheets-lead">
          Spreadsheets in a page, computed on the server: <code>{sheet:grid}</code> takes its cells as lines of text, a comma between them, or the rows of <code>data=</code>, and writes a table of what the formulas come to - =B2*C2, =SUM(D2:D9), =VLOOKUP(E2,$I$2:$J$6,2) - with the column letters and the row numbers around it. The formulas are spreadsheet syntax, read by a parser of their own, and call the 147 functions of <code>pad/subs/formulas/types/</code>, which answer what Excel answers. <code>editable</code> makes cells fields that post back and are computed again - in a form, or in a live region without a script of the page's own.
        </p>
