<p class="nt-lead">Chemical formulas and equations typed the way chemists type them - the notation
of <a href="https://mhchem.github.io/MathJax-mhchem/">mhchem</a>, without its backslash - and
drawn as MathML: the counts of <code>H2SO4</code> below the line, the charges of
<code>Fe^3+</code> and <code>SO4^2-</code> above it, coefficients, states, hydrates, isotopes
and the arrows of a reaction. Every element is checked against the periodic table, so a
typing slip is caught.</p>

<p class="nt-tag">Written <code>{chem 'CH4 + 2O2 -&gt; CO2 + 2H2O'/}</code>, or between the
tags; <code>display='block'</code> sets the equation apart.</p>
