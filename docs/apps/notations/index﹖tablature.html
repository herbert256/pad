Tablature

A tab for a guitar, a bass, a ukulele or any fretted instrument, written the way players have traded tabs in plain text since the newsgroups - a line a string, the highest on top, the fret to press on the line of its string, notes in one column played together - and drawn as a printed tab: the strings with their names, the frets on the lines, the bars across and the techniques as arcs, slanted lines, arrows and waves. The signs are those of the guide to tab notation of the On-line Guitar Archive: h hammer-on, p pull-off, b bend, r release, / and \ slides, v or ~ vibrato, t tap, x a muted string, <12> a harmonic and (8) a ghost note. A screen reader hears every note bar by bar with its string, its fret and its pitch.

Written {notation:tablature} with the tab between the tags. A line of text over or under the strings - chord names, the beats, the note lengths - stays in its columns. tuning= names a tuning - dropd, dadgad, openg, opend, seven, bass, bass5, ukulele, mandolin, banjo - or gives the notes from the lowest string, 'D A D G B E'; without it the names before the lines say it, else the usual tuning of so many strings.

Picked and strummed

A C chord picked one string at a time, then strummed - the notes in one column sound together - with the chord names over the strings.

Template 
{notation:tablature}
  C               C
e|-------0-------|-0-----|
B|-----1---1-----|-1-----|
G|---2-------2---|-0-----|
D|---------------|-2-----|
A|-3-------------|-3-----|
E|---------------|-------|
{/notation:tablature}

Techniques

The signs of the guide to tab notation: a hammer-on and a pull-off, a bend of a whole step struck again and released, vibrato, slides up and down, a harmonic, a ghost note and muted strings.

Template 
{notation:tablature}
e|---------------------------------|---------------<12>----|
B|---------3p0---7b9--9r7----------|-------------------(8)-|
G|-------------------------7~~~----|-----------------------|
D|---------------------------------|-5/7--7\5--------------|
A|-----5h7-------------------------|-----------x-x---------|
E|-0-------------------------------|-----------------------|
{/notation:tablature}

A bass line with its count

Four strings read as a bass tuned E A D G; the beats counted over the strings and the note lengths under them stand in their columns.

Template 
{notation:tablature}
  1   2   3   4   1   2   3   4
G|----------------|----------------|
D|----------2---4-|----------------|
A|0---3-----------|0-------3---2---|
E|----------------|----------------|
  q   q   q   q   h       q   q
{/notation:tablature}

Other tunings

The names before the lines tune the strings - D A D G B E from the bottom is drop D, so the low string sounds a D; tuning= names a tuning, here the ukulele with its high G on the bottom line.

Template 
{notation:tablature}
e|-----------------|
B|-----------------|
G|-----------------|
D|-------0---------|
A|-----0---0-------|
D|-0-0-------0-----|
{/notation:tablature}
{notation:tablature tuning='ukulele'}
A|-----0-----|
E|---0---0---|
C|-0-------0-|
G|-----------|
{/notation:tablature}
