Snake

Steer a snake round a field with the arrow keys, by swiping or with the buttons, to eat the apples; each one makes it longer and a little faster, and it ends where the snake runs into a wall or itself. size= the field, 18 squares a side unless given (10 to 30); speed= the steps a second it starts with, 6 unless given (2 to 15); wrap lets it run off one side and on at the other instead of into the wall.
