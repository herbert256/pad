Tags/Charts and codes/notation:*

{notation:*}

A text written in a notation, drawn on the server - 17 of them, from formulas in AsciiMath and chemical equations to a tune in ABC, guitar chords and tabs, braille, morse, signal flags, chess, go and draughts positions, Karnaugh maps, bridge deals, juggling patterns and cube moves.

Charts and codes Either form

Syntax 

{notation:kind 'text', ...options/}
{notation:kind ...options} text {/notation:kind}
{notation:kind 'text'/}
{notation:math 'a/b'/}  {notation:math display='block'} x^2 + y^2 = r^2 {/notation:math}
{notation:chem 'CH4 + 2O2 -> CO2 + 2H2O'/}
{notation:abc} X:1 ... K:G ... {/notation:abc}
{notation:chord 'Am', instrument='ukulele'/}
{notation:piano 'C4 E4 G4'/}
{notation:braille 'Hello'/}  {notation:morse 'SOS'/}  {notation:semaphore 'PAD'/}  {notation:signals 'PAD'/}
{notation:chess 'fen', flip, highlight='e2, e4', arrow='e2-e4'/}
{notation:go '(;SZ[9]AB[cc];W[gg])', numbers/}
{notation:draughts 'W:W31-50:B1-20', board=8/}
{notation:tablature tuning='dropd'} e|--0--3--| ... {/notation:tablature}
{notation:karnaugh 'm(1, 3, 5) + d(7)', form='pos'/}  {notation:karnaugh}F = A'B + AC{/notation:karnaugh}
{notation:bridge 'N:AKQ2.J9.T87.654 - - -', dealer='N', vulnerable='ns', points/}
{notation:siteswap '531'/}  {notation:siteswap '(4,2x)(2x,4)'/}
{notation:cube "R U R' U'", view='cube', case, layers=3/}

Parameters

kind after colon
The notation, a file of pad/subs/notations/types/: abc, braille, bridge, chem, chess, chord, cube, draughts, go, karnaugh, math, morse, piano, semaphore, signals, siteswap, tablature. The notation: prefix selects it - {notation:math}; {chart:chord} is a chart and {notation:chord} a guitar chord.
text
What is written in the notation - the formula, the tune, the word, the position. Without it the content between the tags is the text, taken as it stands before the level walks it, so the braces of AsciiMath and the brackets of SGF need no ignore.

Options

display
math, chem: inline (the default) or block - the formula on a line of its own, centred.
alttext
math, chem: the text a screen reader without MathML says - the formula as written unless given.
title
abc, chord, piano, semaphore, signals, chess, go, draughts, tablature, karnaugh, bridge, siteswap, cube: the accessible name.
size
The width in pixels - chord (120), semaphore a figure (64), signals a flag (40), chess, go and draughts the board (360), cube (240) - or the height of the piano (120).
view
cube: net (the default), the six faces unfolded, or cube, the up, front and right faces seen from above.
case
Bare option. cube: the cube the moves solve - the moves undone from solved - as an algorithm's case is drawn.
layers
cube: the layers of the cube, 2 to 7 - 3 unless given.
width
abc: the lines spread over that many pixels; without it each line takes its natural width.
instrument
chord: guitar (the default) or ukulele.
frets
chord: a shape of your own for the chord named - x32010, 8 10 10 9 8 8 past the ninth fret.
from
piano: the lowest key shown, C3; to the highest.
to
piano: the highest key shown, B5.
cells
braille: the cells drawn as raised dots instead of characters; cells=12 breaks the lines at 12 cells.
plain
Bare option. morse: the code alone, without the signal drawn; go: no coordinates.
numbers
Bare option. go: the move number on every stone a move put there; draughts: the number of every playing square; karnaugh: the number of every cell.
move
go: the position after that move.
flip
Bare option. chess, draughts: the board turned, Black at the bottom.
highlight
chess: squares to mark, 'e2, e4'; draughts: '32, 28'.
arrow
chess: arrows from square to square, 'e2-e4, g1-f3'.
board
draughts: 10 (international, the default) or 8 (English draughts and checkers).
tuning
tablature: the tuning of the strings - standard, dropd, dadgad, openg, opend, seven, bass, bass5, ukulele, mandolin, banjo - or their notes from the lowest, 'D A D G B E'; without it the names before the lines, else the usual tuning of so many strings.
vars
karnaugh: the variables in order, 'A, B, C' - a letter, digits after it; without it those of the expression, or A B C D.
dealer
bridge: the seat that dealt, n, e, s or w, ringed on the compass.
vulnerable
bridge: none (the default), ns, ew or all - as PBN writes it, love, - and both as well - the vulnerable sides red on the compass.
points
Bare option. bridge: the high-card points of every hand by its seat.
form
karnaugh: sop (the default), the 1s grouped for a minimal sum of products, or pos, the 0s for a minimal product of sums.

Description

{notation:math} draws a text written in a notation on the server, the way {diagram:auto} lays out a flowchart: a formula, a chemical equation, a tune, a guitar chord, a word in braille, morse, semaphore or signal flags, a chess, go or draughts position, a guitar tab, a Karnaugh map, a bridge deal, a juggling pattern, the moves of a cube. Every notation is a file of pad/subs/notations/types/ - its functions in pad/subs/notations/_lib/, its style in www/assets/notations/ - and the files there are the notations there are. Nothing needs a script: the formulas are MathML, which every current browser draws, the rest HTML and inline SVG, the same bytes for the same text.

The notation is selected by its prefix and name: {notation:braille 'Hello'/}. Its first parameter is the text. Without the text as a parameter the content between the tags is the text, taken as written before the level walks it: {notation:math}{(x, if x >= 0),(-x, if x < 0):}{/notation:math} needs no ignore. A field gives the text as a parameter, {notation:chess $fen/}.

  • math - AsciiMath, not TeX, as TeX's braces are PAD's: x^2, a/b, sqrt x, root(3)(x), sum_(i=1)^n, matrices [[a,b],[c,d]], text(...), the Greek letters and the symbols, written as MathML. chem - formulas and equations: H2SO4, Fe^3+, 2H2 + O2 -> 2H2O, <=>, states, hydrates, isotopes, every element checked.
  • abc - a tune in ABC notation as a staff: a treble clef, the key and the meter, the notes with their stems, beams, ledger lines and accidentals, rests, bars and repeats, chord names. chord - the box of a guitar or ukulele chord from its name or its frets. piano - notes or a chord on a keyboard.
  • braille - Unified English Braille, grade 1, as braille characters or drawn dots. morse - the international code and its signal. semaphore - the figures of flag semaphore. signals - the flags of the International Code of Signals.
  • chess - a position in FEN, selected with notation:chess. go - a game record in SGF played out, captures and all. draughts - a position in the FEN of PDN, international or on 8 by 8.
  • tablature - a tab for a guitar, a bass or a ukulele as players write it in plain text, a line a string, drawn as a printed tab with the signs of the On-line Guitar Archive's guide to tab notation - hammer-ons, pull-offs, bends, slides, vibrato, harmonics - and every note said with its string, its fret and its pitch.
  • karnaugh - a Boolean function of two to four variables - its minterms, its maxterms, the column of its truth table or an expression - as its Karnaugh map, the groups of a minimal cover looped round and the minimal sum of products, or with form='pos' product of sums, under it.
  • bridge - a deal of contract bridge in the Deal tag of PBN, the Portable Bridge Notation, as the diagram of a bridge column, the dealer and the vulnerable sides marked. siteswap - a juggling pattern in siteswap notation, vanilla, multiplex or synchronous, as a ladder diagram of the two hands, checked to be one a juggler can throw. cube - moves of a twisty cube in the notation of the World Cube Association, made on a solved cube and drawn as a net of its faces or seen from above.

Every drawing is an image for a screen reader with a name and a description in words - the pieces, the stones, the notes, the flags - or MathML, which a screen reader reads itself. A name that is no notation, an option a notation does not know and a text it cannot read - a FEN with seven ranks, an element that does not exist, a character morse has no sign for - are errors under the strict check.

Examples

A tune in ABC

The header names the tune, its meter, its unit length and its key; the lines below are the music, a line of staff each.

Templateexamples/notation/abc-1-tune.pad 
{notation:abc}
  X:1
  T:Speed the Plough
  M:4/4
  L:1/8
  K:G
  |:GABc dedB|dedB dedB|c2ec B2dB|c2A2 A2BA|
  GABc dedB|dedB dedB|c2ec B2dB|A2F2 G4:|
{/notation:abc}
Resultopen alone
