<h2>One item, repeated over the data</h2>

<p>The content between the tags is one item - a label, a badge, a letter - rendered once for every
row of the data, with the handling options of any tag: <code>sort=</code>, <code>where=</code>,
<code>first=</code>. <code>copies=</code> writes every item that many times, <code>skip=</code> leaves
the first labels of a sheet used before empty. A kind takes the parts of an item from the elements
that say what they are: a ticket's stub is its <code>&lt;aside&gt;</code>, a letter's address its
<code>&lt;address&gt;</code>.</p>

<h2>The screen, the paper and the PDF</h2>

<p>On the screen the sheets lie on a grey desk at their size in millimetres - <code>scale=</code>
makes the preview smaller. Printed, the page prints the sheets alone, each on a page of its own size
without a margin. And in a <code>{pad:pdf}</code>, or with <code>pdf</code> on the tag, PAD's
own PDF writer lays out the same sheets: <code>pdf</code> links them, the page asked again with
<code>&amp;padPdf</code>.</p>
