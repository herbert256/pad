Bridge deals

A deal of contract bridge written as the Deal tag of PBN, the Portable Bridge Notation every bridge program reads and writes - the seat of the first hand and a colon, then the four hands clockwise, each its spades, hearts, diamonds and clubs apart by dots, a dash for a hand not shown - drawn as the diagram of a bridge column: North at the top, South at the bottom, West and East either side, every suit from the ace down, the compass in the middle with the dealer ringed and the vulnerable sides red. A screen reader hears every hand card by card. A card dealt twice, a rank that is none and hands of different sizes are named.

Written {notation:bridge 'N:.63.AKQ987.A9732 A8654.KQ5.T.QJT6 ...'/}. dealer='N' rings the dealer, vulnerable= is none, ns, ew or all, points writes the high-card points of every hand.

A whole deal

The example deal of the PBN standard, the dealer North and North-South vulnerable, with the high-card points of every hand - forty between them, as in every deal.

Template 
{notation:bridge 'N:.63.AKQ987.A9732 A8654.KQ5.T.QJT6 J973.J98742.3.K4 KQT2.AT.J6542.85', dealer='N', vulnerable='ns', points/}

Two hands of a problem

A dash stands for a hand not shown, as a problem of declarer play shows only the two hands of one side; the first hand here is West's.

Template 
{notation:bridge 'W:KQT2.AT.J6542.85 - A8654.KQ5.T.QJT6 -', dealer='W', vulnerable='ew'/}
Result

An ending

Hands of fewer cards draw the last tricks of a deal - every hand holding as many, here three, a void drawn as a dash.

Template 
{notation:bridge 'N:AK..2. Q.2.A. ..KQ.3 J2.3..'/}
