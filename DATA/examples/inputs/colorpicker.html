A colour picked from a row of swatches, typed as a code or chosen in the browser's own picker: a native <input type="color">, which posts #rrggbb, and - where the script runs - the swatches and a text field for the hexadecimal code, each kept in step with the colour. swatches= the colours of the row, a list of #rgb or #rrggbb, eight of the palette unless given; value= the colour before a post, #000000 as a colour field has unless given. #rgb is written #rrggbb, which is all a colour field takes.
