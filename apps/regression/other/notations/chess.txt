
<p class="nt-lead">A chess position written in FEN, the Forsyth-Edwards Notation every chess program
reads and writes, drawn as a board with the pieces as shapes of PAD's own - no font, no image -
the coordinates along its edges, squares marked and arrows drawn for a move, a plan or a
threat. The placement of the pieces is all it needs; the side to move, the castling rights, the
en passant square and the counters are checked when they are written.</p>

<p class="nt-tag">Written <code>{notation:chess 'fen'/}</code>; options: <code>flip</code>, <code>highlight=</code>, <code>arrow=</code>, <code>size=</code>.</p>
