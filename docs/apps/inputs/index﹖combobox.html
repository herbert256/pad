Combobox

A text field that suggests from a list as it is typed: a native text field with a <datalist> of the choices, which the browser offers by itself, and where the script runs the WAI-ARIA combobox - a list under the field of the choices that hold what is typed, the part that matches marked, walked with the arrow keys, Enter taking a choice, Escape closing the list and then emptying the field. It posts what the field holds, a choice of the list or not: rules='in:...' holds it to the list. The choices are options=, a list, or the rows of the store data= names - field= the field of a row they are read from, its first unless given - or the lines between the tags.
