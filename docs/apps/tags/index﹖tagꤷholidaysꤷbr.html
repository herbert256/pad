Tags/Holidays/holidays:br

{holidays:br}

Brazil: the national holidays of federal law.

Holidays Either form

Syntax 

{holidays:br year=2026/}

Description

One of the 32 holidays of holidays:*, written {holidays:br year=2026/}.

Brazil: the national holidays of federal law - Lei 662/1949 with Lei 10.607/2002 (1 January, 21 April, 1 May, 7 September, 2 and 15 November, 25 December), Lei 6.802/1980 (Our Lady of Aparecida, 12 October) and, since 2024, Lei 14.759/2023 (Zumbi and Black Consciousness Day, 20 November) - with Good Friday, which the federal government's yearly ordinance of holidays lists as a national one. Carnival Monday and Tuesday and Corpus Christi are optional days of that ordinance (pontos facultativos), no days off by law - observances; Ash Wednesday's morning, the civil servants' day and the afternoons of 24 and 31 December are left out, as are the holidays of the states and towns. The file knows the years since 2003. Checked against the ordinances of the Ministry of Management (Portaria MGI 11.460/2025 for 2026: Carnival 16 and 17 February, Good Friday 3 April, Corpus Christi 4 June, 20 November a national holiday) and the laws they name, and the dates of 2003, 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026.

Example

Public holidays

Use region= for regional rules or countries= for several calendars with provenance.

{holidays:br year=2026/}
