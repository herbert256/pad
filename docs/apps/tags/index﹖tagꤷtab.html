Tags/Widgets/tab:*

{tab:*}

One item of a tabs, accordion, carousel, wizard, lightbox, splitpane or sortable widget: its label and its content, handed to that owner.

Widgets Tag pairs

Syntax 

{widget:tab 'label'} ... {/widget:tab}

Parameters

label
The tab's label, the summary of an accordion item or the caption of a carousel slide. Required in {widget:tabs}, {widget:accordion} and {widget:splitpane}, optional in {widget:carousel}; in {widget:sortable} the key the item is posted under.

Description

{widget:tab} is the item of the seven widgets that take a list of labelled parts: {widget:tabs} (a tab and its panel), {widget:accordion} (a <details> with the label as its summary), {widget:carousel} (a slide with the label as its caption), {widget:wizard} (a step), {widget:lightbox} (a picture with its caption), {widget:splitpane} (one of its two panes) and {widget:sortable} (an item put in order, the label its key). It prints nothing where it stands: its content is rendered, trimmed, and handed to the nearest of those owners above it, which builds its markup from the items once its own content has rendered.

Because the hand-over goes to the nearest owner and not to the direct parent, a tab may stand inside an {pad:if}, or inside a loop that makes a tab per row of data: {widget:tabs}{array:plans}{widget:tab $name}...{/widget:tab}{/array:plans}{/widget:tabs}. The owners are widgets, reached by the required widget: prefix; {widget:tab} is a built-in tag, so a row with a field named tab still makes tabs.

Under the strict check a {widget:tab} outside any owner is an error, as is one that never closes, and a tab of a {widget:tabs}, an {widget:accordion}, a {widget:wizard} or a {widget:splitpane} without a label, an item of a {widget:sortable} without its key. A carousel slide and a lightbox picture may go without a label; they then have no caption.

Examples

A tab in an if

A tab only shows up when its condition holds - the hand-over skips the if around it.

Templateexamples/tab/1-conditional.pad 
{pad:set $hasReviews = 0/}
{widget:tabs}
  {widget:tab 'Overview'}<p>A kettle in brushed steel.</p>{/widget:tab}
  {pad:if $hasReviews gt 0}
    {widget:tab 'Reviews'}<p>The reviews.</p>{/widget:tab}
  {/pad:if}
  {widget:tab 'Delivery'}<p>Delivered the next working day.</p>{/widget:tab}
{/widget:tabs}
Resultopen alone

A kettle in brushed steel.

Delivered the next working day.

The same tabs, another owner

Identical tab items laid out by an accordion instead of a tab set.

Templateexamples/tab/2-owners.pad 
{widget:accordion}
  {widget:tab 'Overview'}<p>A kettle in brushed steel.</p>{/widget:tab}
  {widget:tab 'Delivery'}<p>Delivered the next working day.</p>{/widget:tab}
{/widget:accordion}
