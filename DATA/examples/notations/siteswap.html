A juggling pattern in siteswap notation, the notation jugglers have written patterns in since the eighties: every throw the number of beats until that ball is thrown again - 3 the three-ball cascade, 531, 441, 97531, a letter from a = 10 up. It is drawn as a ladder diagram: the right and the left hand as two rails along the time, every throw from the beat it leaves to the beat it lands - an arc back into the same hand, a line across to the other - coloured by its orbit, the balls that pass through the same throws. A pattern can be juggled when no two balls land at once; then the average of its throws is the number of balls. A screen reader hears the balls, the period, whether the pattern starts from the ground state and what every throw does.

Written {notation:siteswap '531'/}. Several balls thrown at once from one hand stand in brackets, [33]33; both hands throwing together stand in pairs, (4,2x)(2x,4), the left hand first, every throw even and x sending it across, * after the pairs repeating them with the hands swapped.

Three balls
