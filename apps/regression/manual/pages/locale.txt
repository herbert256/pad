<h2>Catalogs</h2>

<p>The texts live in <code>_lang/&lt;locale&gt;.json</code>, one flat object of keys:</p>

<pre>{
  "cart.title": "Je winkelwagen",
  "cart.items": "%d artikel|%d artikelen",
  "cart.owner": "Bewaard voor :name"
}</pre>

<ul>
  <li><code>count=</code> picks the plural form and replaces <code>%d</code>: two forms are one and other, three are zero, one and other.</li>
  <li>Every other <code>name=</code> replaces <code>:name</code>.</li>
  <li>A catalog is looked up like a <code>_data</code> file: from the page's directory up to the application root, then <code>_common</code>, the most specific winning. For <code>nl_NL</code> the files <code>nl_NL.json</code>, <code>nl-NL.json</code> and <code>nl.json</code> all count.</li>
  <li>A key no catalog knows is written as itself.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Choosing the locale</h2>

<ul>
  <li><code>$padLocale</code> - the locale, <code>'en'</code> unless the configuration or the page says otherwise.</li>
  <li><code>$padLocales</code> - the locales the application speaks. When it lists any, a <code>?lang=nl</code> link chooses one and the <code>padLang</code> cookie keeps it; without a choice the visitor's <code>Accept-Language</code> picks the best match.</li>
  <li><code>$padTimezone</code> - the timezone dates are shown in.</li>
</ul>
