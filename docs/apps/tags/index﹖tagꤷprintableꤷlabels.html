Tags/Printables/printable:labels

{printable:labels}

A sheet of sticky labels.

Printables Tag pairs

Syntax 

{printable:labels data='products', sheet='a4-3x8'}...{/printable:labels}

Description

One of the 14 printables of printable:*, written {printable:labels data='products', sheet='a4-3x8'}...{/printable:labels}.

{printable:labels data='products', sheet='a4-3x8'}...{/printable:labels} - a sheet of sticky labels: the content between the tags is one label, repeated over the data, laid out in the grid of the sheet. sheet= the grid, named by its paper and its grid - a4-3x8 (63.5 by 33.9 mm, the default), a4-3x7, a4-2x7, a4-2x8, a4-2x4, a4-4x10, a4-5x13, letter-3x10, letter-2x10, letter-2x5, letter-2x3 and letter-4x20; a sheet of your own with across= and down=, width= and height= of a label, margin= the top and left margin and pitch= the step from a label to the next, across and down, all in mm, paper= and landscape. skip= leaves the first labels of a used sheet empty, copies= prints every label that many times, outline prints the edges as well; padding= (2 mm), size= (9 pt) and align= the label's text, set in the middle of its height.

Example

The labels

As the printables application shows it.

Template 
{fake:rows 'product:word, code:number', rows=24, seed=8, from=100000000000, to=999999999999, toData='products'/}
{printable:labels data='products', sheet='a4-3x8', scale=0.45, pdf}
  <b>{$product | capitalize}</b>
  {barcode:ean13 $code, height=40/}
{/printable:labels}
Result
24 labels on 1 sheet of A4, 210 × 297 mm
