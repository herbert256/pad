# blue-orange

Deviations around a neutral #f2f2f2 midpoint. Blue negative and orange positive arms have closely matched relative luminance at corresponding anchors (difference below 0.025). PAD original, GPL-3.0. No claim of universal color-vision accessibility.

Use `padPalette('blue-orange', 9)` or `{widget:palette 'blue-orange', steps=9/}`. Charts accept `palette='blue-orange'`. One step returns the middle color.

The colors also work in print; do not rely on color alone for categories or values. Inspect grayscale using the regression luminance measurements.
