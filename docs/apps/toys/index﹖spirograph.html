Spirograph

A toothed wheel rolls round inside a ring, a pen in one of its holes, and draws the curve - round and round until the teeth meet again where they began. Change the wheel, the hole and the ring, roll it outside the ring instead, and lay one drawing over the last in another colour. ring= the teeth of the ring (96 unless given, 40 to 160); wheel= the teeth of the wheel (52, 10 to 150 - fewer than the ring when it rolls inside); pen= how far out the hole is, in percent of the wheel (70, 5 to 100); outside rolls the wheel round the outside of the ring.
