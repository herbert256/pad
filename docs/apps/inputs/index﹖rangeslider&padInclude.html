A lower and an upper number chosen on one track, a price from 100 to 400: two native <input type="range">, one for each end, under a legend - two fields of the form, {input:rangeslider 'price'} posting price_from and price_to, names= two others. Where the script runs the two lie on one track, the part between them filled: two WAI-ARIA sliders, each reached with Tab and moved with the arrow keys, Page Up and Page Down, Home and End, as a range is, and neither passing the other - the lower stops at the upper. min= and max=, 0 and 100 unless given, step= 1; unit= follows the values as they are shown - ' EUR', '%'; labels= what each end says, 'From, To' unless given; value= the two before a post, the whole track unless given. A range is two fields and has no rules= - the page's PHP checks the two with padValidate, and each message stands after the frame. Without the script the two ranges stand one above the other, showing no number - the value written beside each would stay while it is moved - and either can be put past the other: the page's PHP gets the two as they were posted.
