Ripple tank

A ripple tank: waves spread over water, bounce off walls and run through each other, crest on crest and trough on trough, into the stripes of interference. Click or drag to touch the water; with walls ticked the pointer builds walls instead. setup= what the tank starts with - two (two sources beating in step, unless given), slits (a straight wave through two slits in a wall) or pond (raindrops); frequency= how fast the sources beat (4, 1 to 10).
