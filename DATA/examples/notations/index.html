Text written in a notation and drawn on the server - a formula, a chemical equation, a tune, a chord, a word in braille, morse or flags, a chess, go or draughts position, a guitar tab, a Karnaugh map, a bridge deal, a juggling pattern, the moves of a cube. Each is a tag of its own, a file of pad/subs/notations/types/ in the engine, written {notation:kind 'text'/}. The text is its first parameter or the content between its tags. What comes out is MathML, HTML or inline SVG - no script, the same for the same text, and readable by a screen reader.

  • ABC musicA tune in ABC notation drawn as a staff.{notation:abc}
  • BackgammonA board position with numbered points, checkers, bar, borne-off pieces and doubling cube.{notation:backgammon}
  • BrailleA text in braille.{notation:braille}
  • Bridge dealsA deal of contract bridge written as the Deal tag of PBN, the Portable Bridge Notation.{notation:bridge}
  • ChemistryChemical formulas and equations, typed as chemists type them, as MathML.{notation:chem}
  • ChessA chess position drawn as a board, the pieces shapes of PAD's own.{notation:chess}
  • Chord boxesA chord of a guitar or a ukulele as the box of a chord chart.{notation:chord}
  • Cube movesA sequence of moves of a twisty cube in the notation of the World Cube Association - U D L R F B a face a quarter turn clockwise as seen from it, ' the other way, 2 a half turn, Rw or r two layers, 3Rw three, 2R the second layer alone, M E S the middle slice, x y z the whole cube, ( ) a group a number after it repeats - applied to a solved cube and drawn.{notation:cube}
  • DraughtsA draughts position from the FEN of PDN, drawn as a board.{notation:draughts}
  • FretboardScale notes across a fretted instrument, labelled by note or interval.{notation:fretboard}
  • GoA go position from a game record in SGF, drawn as a board.{notation:go}
  • Karnaugh mapsA Boolean function of two to four variables drawn as its Karnaugh map, the cells in Gray-code order with their 1s, 0s and don't cares, the groups of a minimal sum of products looped round, and the minimal expression under the map in MathML.{notation:karnaugh}
  • MathematicsA formula written in AsciiMath, the notation of asciimath.org, as MathML.{notation:math}
  • Morse codeA text in the international Morse code of ITU-R M.1677-1.{notation:morse}
  • PgnBounded annotated mainline and optional initial FEN board.{notation:pgn}
  • Piano keysNotes on a piano keyboard, the keys pressed marked and named.{notation:piano}
  • SemaphoreA text in flag semaphore.{notation:semaphore}
  • ShogiStatic SFEN with promotions, hands and two board orientations.{notation:shogi}
  • Signal flagsA text spelt in the maritime signal flags of the International Code of Signals.{notation:signals}
  • SiteswapA juggling pattern in siteswap notation, each throw the number of beats until the ball is thrown again - 3 the cascade, 531, 441, 97531, b for 11 - drawn as a ladder diagram.{notation:siteswap}
  • TablatureA tab for a guitar, a bass, a ukulele or any fretted instrument, written as players write it in plain text and drawn as it is printed.{notation:tablature}
  • XiangqiA Chinese chess position on its nine-file, ten-rank board, with river and palaces.{notation:xiangqi}
