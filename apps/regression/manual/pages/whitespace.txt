<p>A template is laid out for people, with line breaks and indentation, and PAD keeps that
layout in what it writes. Usually that does not matter - HTML folds whitespace - but in
plain-text mail, CSV, JSON, console output and inline layouts it does.</p>

<h2>The ~ marker</h2>

<p>A <code>~</code> just inside a brace takes the whitespace on that side of it, newlines
included:</p>

<ul>
  <li><code>{~tag}</code> - the whitespace before the tag</li>
  <li><code>{tag~}</code> - the whitespace after it, which for an opening tag is the start of its content</li>
  <li><code>{~/tag~}</code> - both sides of the closing tag</li>
</ul>

<p>Only a brace that opens a tag counts: a <code>~</code> in text, in CSS or in a quoted
parameter stays as it is.</p>

<h2>spaceless</h2>

<p><b>{spaceless}</b> takes the whitespace between HTML tags out of everything it wraps, and
at both ends; text inside a tag keeps its spaces. For the whole page there is <b>{tidy}</b>.</p>
