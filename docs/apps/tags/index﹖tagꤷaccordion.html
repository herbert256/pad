Tags/Widgets/accordion

{accordion}

Items that open and close on a click, each a details element - no JavaScript.

Widgets Tag pairs

Syntax 

{accordion} {tab 'label'} ... {/tab} {tab 'label'} ... {/tab} {/accordion}
{accordion single, open=1} ... {/accordion}
{accordion open} ... {/accordion}

Options

single
Bare option: one item open at a time - the items share a name, the exclusive accordion of HTML.
open
The item open at first: its number from 1 or its label. Bare, open opens every item.

Description

{accordion} turns every {tab} inside it into a <details> element with the tab's label as its <summary>. The browser opens and closes the items, from the keyboard as well, a screen reader hears "collapsed" or "expanded", and a find in the page opens the item it finds a match in. There is no script.

The items are collected the way {tabs} collects them, so they may come from a loop or stand in an {if}. Without options every item starts closed and each opens on its own.

  • single gives the items one shared name: opening one closes the one that was open.
  • open= names the item open at the start, by its number from 1 or its label; open alone opens them all.

Under the strict check an item without a label, text between the items, an accordion without items, an open= naming no item, and a bare open on a single accordion (which can only have one open) are errors. The colours are --pad-accordion-accent, -text, -muted, -line and -hover on .pad-accordion.

Examples

A FAQ, one open at a time

single makes opening one question close the other; open=1 opens the first.

Templateexamples/accordion/1-faq.pad 
{accordion single, open=1}
  {tab 'When will my order arrive?'}
    <p>Orders placed before 22:00 on a working day are delivered the next day.</p>
  {/tab}
  {tab 'Can I return something?'}
    <p>Yes - within 30 days, unused and in its box.</p>
  {/tab}
  {tab 'Is there a warranty?'}
    <p>Two years on every appliance.</p>
  {/tab}
{/accordion}
Resultopen alone
When will my order arrive?

Orders placed before 22:00 on a working day are delivered the next day.

Can I return something?

Yes - within 30 days, unused and in its box.

Is there a warranty?

Two years on every appliance.

All open, from data

A bare open opens every item; the items come from a loop.

Templateexamples/accordion/2-all-open.pad 
{accordion open}
  {steps}
    {tab $step}<p>{$detail}</p>{/tab}
  {/steps}
{/accordion}
PHPexamples/accordion/2-all-open.php 
<?php

  $steps = [
    [ 'step' => 'Order',   'detail' => 'Pick the products and pay.'      ],
    [ 'step' => 'Pack',    'detail' => 'The warehouse packs the parcel.' ],
    [ 'step' => 'Deliver', 'detail' => 'The courier brings it to you.'   ] ];

?>
Resultopen alone
Order

Pick the products and pay.

Pack

The warehouse packs the parcel.

Deliver

The courier brings it to you.
