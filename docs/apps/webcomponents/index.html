Cards with slots

{card title='Revenue'} is a PAD custom tag that writes <pad-card> with its shadow root: encapsulated styles inlined by {shadow css=}, named slots the browser fills from the light DOM - and not a line of JavaScript.

{shadow}_tags/card.pad{parms}shadowrootmode<slot>::slotted

A rating that posts

<pad-rating value="4"> gets its value from PAD and its stars from the server; a click posts the new rating with the session's CSRF token, and the element shows the average PAD answers.
