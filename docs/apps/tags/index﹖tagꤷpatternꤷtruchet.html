Tags/Patterns/pattern:truchet

{pattern:truchet}

Smith's Truchet tiles: squares of the size, each with two quarter circles joining the middles of its sides.

Patterns Single tags

Syntax 

{pattern:truchet seed=7/}

Description

One of the 26 patterns of pattern:*, written {pattern:truchet seed=7/}.

Smith's Truchet tiles: squares of the size, each with two quarter circles joining the middles of its sides, turned one way or the other as the seed says - the arcs meet their neighbours' and wander on as endless curves.
