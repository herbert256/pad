Karnaugh maps

A Boolean function of two to four variables drawn as its Karnaugh map: the cells of its truth table laid out in Gray-code order, so two cells side by side - over the edges too - differ in one variable, each holding 1, 0 or a don't care. The groups of a minimal sum of products are looped round in colours of their own, wrapping over the edges where they must, and the minimal expression stands under the map. Every prime implicant is found by trying every group the map has, and the groups chosen are the fewest that cover the 1s, then the ones with the fewest variables; the expression is checked against every cell before it is drawn. A screen reader hears the rows of the map, the groups and the expression in words.

Written {notation:karnaugh 'm(1, 3, 5) + d(0)'/} - minterms and don't cares, maxterms M(0, 2), the output column of a truth table 0110 1001, or an expression between the tags: {notation:karnaugh}F = A'B + AC{/notation:karnaugh}, with ', ~ or not, + or or, ^ or xor, and two terms side by side for and. vars= names the variables, form='pos' loops the 0s for a product of sums, numbers writes the number of every cell.

From minterms

The cells that hold 1 by their numbers: three groups cover them, the smallest set there is. As a product of sums the 0s are grouped instead.

Template 
{notation:karnaugh 'm(6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14)', numbers/}
{notation:karnaugh 'm(6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14)', form='pos'/}
Result
F=AB¯+AC¯+BCD¯ F=(A+B)(A+C)(B¯+C¯+D¯)

Don't cares

A don't care may join a group or stay out of it: with cell 15 free the eight cells of A make one group. The four corners of a map are next to each other over its edges.

Template 
{notation:karnaugh 'm(6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14) + d(15)'/}
{notation:karnaugh 'm(0, 2, 8, 10) + d(5)'/}
Result
F=A+BCD¯ F=B¯D¯

From an expression

An expression is worked out cell by cell and drawn minimal again - the consensus term BC goes - and an exclusive or has no two 1s side by side, so nothing groups.

Template 
{notation:karnaugh}F = AB + A'C + BC{/notation:karnaugh}
{notation:karnaugh}Y(x1, x2, x3) = x1 ^ x2 ^ x3{/notation:karnaugh}
Result
F=A¯C+AB Y=x1¯x2¯x3+x1¯x2x3¯+x1x2¯x3¯+x1x2x3

From a truth table

The output column of a truth table, the row of all 0s first, a - for a don't care: the segment e of a seven-segment display for the digits 0 to 9, the codes 10 to 15 never sent.

Template 
{notation:karnaugh '1010 0010 10-- ----', vars='A, B, C, D', title='Segment e of a seven-segment display'/}
Result
F=B¯D¯+CD¯
