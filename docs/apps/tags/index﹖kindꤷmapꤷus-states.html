Tags and subsystem kinds/Map views/us-states

map / us-states

The fifty states of the United States and the District of Columbia, each under its ISO 3166-2 code - US-CA, or CA alone, the postal code - or its name; Alaska and Hawaii (its main islands, without the Northwestern Hawaiian Islands) drawn below the lower 48 at the left as on every map of the states.

The view parameter of {pad:map} selects a country or region.

All Map views · Using this with {map}

Usage example

The view without data; data= and value= colour its regions.

Template 
{pad:map 'us-states', width=540/}
Result

Description

us-states - The fifty states of the United States and the District of Columbia, each under its ISO 3166-2 code - US-CA, or CA alone, the postal code - or its name; Alaska and Hawaii (its main islands, without the Northwestern Hawaiian Islands) drawn below the lower 48 at the left as on every map of the states. The outlines are subs/maps/_lib/ us-states.json, from Natural Earth's 1:10m admin-1 states and provinces with the large lakes cut out (public domain, naturalearthdata.com), in the Albers equal-area conic projection of the United States with Alaska and Hawaii moved in - the composite of d3's geoAlbersUsa. Puerto Rico, Guam, the Virgin Islands, American Samoa, the Northern Mariana Islands and the minor outlying islands have their ISO 3166-2 codes too, but no outline here: a value of theirs is named in the title as one the view does not show.
