# ee — Estonia

Supported years: **2006–2026**, full years after Christmas Eve became a public holiday in May 2005. `{pad:holidays 'ee', year=2026/}` and `padHolidays('ee', 2026)` include the national day and eleven public holidays with Estonian local names. Regions are unsupported.

Good Friday, Easter Sunday and Pentecost Sunday follow Gregorian Easter. Easter Monday and Pentecost Monday are not public holidays. Weekend dates stay on their actual date; there is no next-Monday substitution. Shortened workdays before certain holidays and commemorative days are outside this full-day holiday definition.

Sources checked **2 November 2026**:

- [Public Holidays and Days of National Importance Act, sections 1–2, consolidated 30 December 2022](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/en/tolge/pdf/527122022003), including the 20 May 2005 Christmas Eve amendment.
- [State Chancellery's holiday names and scope, updated 10 February 2021](https://www.riigikantselei.ee/riiklikud-sumbolid-preemiad-ja-tahtpaevad/puhad-ja-tahtpaevad/riigipuhad).
- [Tallinn municipal 2024 calendar](https://www.tallinn.ee/et/kirsike/riiklikud-puhad-ja-luhendatud-toopaevad) and [2025–2026 calendar](https://www.tallinn.ee/et/lasteaed-ounake/aasta-20252026-riigipuhad-vabad-paevad).

The focused test compares complete holiday-date sets for 2024–2026, checks leap-year and weekend behavior, rejects unsupported years and checks all local names are present. No unsupported future declarations are inferred.
