<h1>Required type prefixes</h1>
<p class="lead">Every named tag starts with its type: <code>&#123;pad:if&#125;</code>,
<code>&#123;array:orders&#125;</code>, <code>&#123;data:orders&#125;</code> or
<code>&#123;app:card&#125;</code>. The prefix selects one namespace. Names are never
searched across types.</p>
<p>Close a pair with the same qualified name, such as <code>&#123;/array:orders&#125;</code>.
Variable and expression forms such as <code>&#123;$name&#125;</code> and
<code>&#123;!html&#125;</code> keep their shorthand.</p>
<p>Subsystem prefixes, options and helpers are listed in the
<a href="/pad/subsystems/">subsystems application</a>.</p>
