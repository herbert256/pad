Tags/Sequences/remove

{remove}

Removes the values of a sequence that belong to a sequence type - the inverse of keep.

Sequences Tag pairs

Syntax 

{remove '1..10', prime} ... {/remove}
{remove prime, rows=5} ... {/remove}
{remove:odd rows=5} ... {/remove:odd}
{remove store, even} ... {/remove}

Parameters

values
Where the values come from: a range '1..10', a stored sequence named bare (not quoted), or left out - the candidates then run from from upward.
type
The sequence type whose members are removed: prime, odd, square, ... A type with a value is written as an option: multiple=3.

Description

{remove '1..10', prime} runs a sequence in remove mode: every value that belongs to the named type is dropped, the rest stay - here 1 4 6 8 9 10. It is the same run as {sequence '1..10', remove, prime}, and the exact inverse of {keep}: together the two split a list in two.

Without a range the candidates run from from (default 1) upward, and rows counts what is left: {remove prime, rows=5} is the first five numbers that are not prime, 1 4 6 8 9. A stored sequence, named bare, works as the source as well, and the prefix form {remove:odd rows=5} names the type in the tag.

To invert an action - all but the first three - use the negative option of {sequence} instead; remove is the inversion for plays.

Examples

What is not prime

The same range through remove and keep - together they hold every number once.

Templateexamples/remove/1-not-prime.pad 
<p>remove: {remove '1..20', prime}{$sequence} {/remove}</p>
<p>keep:   {keep '1..20', prime}{$sequence} {/keep}</p>
Resultopen alone

remove: 1 4 6 8 9 10 12 14 15 16 18 20

keep: 2 3 5 7 11 13 17 19

From a store

The odd numbers removed from a stored list of the first ten triangular numbers.

Templateexamples/remove/2-store.pad 
{sequence triangular, rows=10, push='triangles'}
<p>All:  {pull 'triangles'}{$sequence} {/pull}</p>
<p>Not odd: {remove triangles, odd}{$sequence} {/remove}</p>
Resultopen alone

All: 1 3 6 10 15 21 28 36 45 55

Not odd: 6 10 28 36

See also

  • {flag} Replaces every value of a sequence with 1 or 0: whether it belongs to a sequence type.
  • {make} Replaces every value of a sequence with what a sequence type makes of it.
  • {sequence} Generates a range, a list or one of 80 mathematical sequences, and iterates its values.
  • {pull} Iterates a stored sequence, optionally through plays and actions.
