Tags/Games/toy:*

{toy:*}

A small live simulation or visual toy in the browser - 23 of them, from the Game of Life and a flock of birds to the Mandelbrot set and falling sand.

Games Either form

Syntax 

{toy:name/}
{toy:life pattern='gun', wrap/}
{toy:mandelbrot iterations=500/}
{toy:boids birds=200/}
{toy:sorting race/}
{toy:life}
  .O.
  ..O
  OOO
{/toy:life}
{toy:lsystem}
  axiom: X
  X: F[+X]-X
  angle: 25
{/toy:lsystem}

Parameters

kind after colon
The toy, a file of pad/subs/games/toys/types/: ant, automaton, boids, clocks, epicycles, fireworks, flowfield, galton, harmonograph, ising, kaleidoscope, life, lsystem, mandelbrot, orbits, pendulum, percolation, reactiondiffusion, ripples, sand, sorting, spirograph, starfield. The toy: prefix selects it - {toy:life/}.

Options

seed
A whole number: what the toy picks at random is the same on every load. Without it the browser picks.
title
The name the toy is shown under, else its own.
paused
Bare option: the toy waits for its Play button, as every toy does when the visitor asks for reduced motion.
width
life (64), sand (160), reactiondiffusion (192): the cells across; height the cells down.
size
ant (160), percolation (64), ising (128): the cells, sites or spins across and down.
speed
How fast it runs: generations, steps, rows or sweeps a second (life, ant, automaton, sorting, reactiondiffusion, ising), or a level of 1 to 10 (boids, flowfield, orbits, epicycles, percolation; starfield 1 to 20).
pattern
life: glider, spaceship, pulsar, gun, pentadecathlon, rpentomino, acorn, diehard, random or empty - or a pattern of your own between the tags; reactiondiffusion: the feed and kill rates of coral, mitosis, spots, mazes, worms, holes, waves or chaos.
initial
automaton: single or random; reactiondiffusion: what the second chemical starts as - spots, center or noise; ising: random, a hot start, or up, a cold one.
rule
life: B3/S23 and its like; ant: the turns, RL, LLRR ...; automaton: Wolfram's number, 0 to 255.
wrap
Bare option, life: the edges of the grid join.
algorithm
sorting: bubble, cocktail, insertion, selection, gnome, shell, comb, quick, merge, heap or radix; race runs them all side by side; ising: metropolis, a spin at a time, or clusters, Swendsen and Wang's.
order
sorting: what the bars start as - random, reversed, nearly or few; epicycles: the circles largest first, size, or slowest first, frequency.
colour
kaleidoscope: rainbow, ink or palette; percolation: flow, the water in the accent, or clusters, each one a colour.
feed
reactiondiffusion: the feed rate of the first chemical, 0 to 0.1 - a pattern's rates of your own, with kill.
kill
reactiondiffusion: the rate the second chemical is taken away, 0 to 0.1.
shape
epicycles: heart, star, square or trefoil - or the x,y points of a drawing of your own between the tags.
circles
epicycles: how many circles turn (30, at most one fewer than the points).
points
epicycles: the samples taken along the drawing (256, 16 to 1024).
probability
percolation: the chance a site is open (0.59, 0 to 1).
neighbours
percolation: 4, the sites beside, or 8, the corners too.
mode
percolation: what Play does - flow, the water seeping down, or experiment, trials that estimate the threshold.
temperature
ising: the heat, 0.5 to 5 (2.27, near the critical point).
field
ising: an outside field pulling the spins up, or down below 0 (0, -1 to 1).
iterations
mandelbrot: how long a point is followed (300); lsystem: how often the word is rewritten.
palette
mandelbrot: ocean, fire, rainbow or mono.
birds
boids: how many (150); separation, alignment and cohesion weigh the three rules, 0 to 10.
pendulums
pendulum: how many swing side by side, each started a hundredth of a degree apart (1); angles the start.
system
orbits: solar, binary, eight or swarm. An lsystem names its system as its first value: plant, dragon, koch, hilbert ...
setup
ripples: two, slits or pond.
show
clocks: analog, binary and words - which of them; zone a time zone.
mirrors
kaleidoscope: how many wedges (8).
levels
galton: the rows of pegs (12); balls how many, bias the chance of going right in percent.

Description

{toy:life} puts a small live simulation or visual toy on the page, run in the browser: the server checks the options it is given and writes its frame, and the toy's script - written once per page, with the CSP nonce - animates it. Every toy is a file of pad/subs/games/toys/types/, its script and its style in www/assets/toys/; the files of types/ are the toys there are.

The toy is selected by its prefix and name: {toy:life/}. The pattern of a Game of Life and the rules of an L-system stand between the tags, one a line - taken as written, before the level walks them, so a field or a tag there is not filled in.

Every toy can be touched - draw cells, drag a bob, throw a planet, stir the current - and has labelled controls and a status line; its canvas has role="img" and a label that says what it shows. A toy holds while the page is hidden or it is scrolled out of view, and waits for its Play button when the visitor asks for reduced motion or the tag says paused. Its root carries data-state: playing, paused or held.

seed= makes what a toy picks at random the same on every load. The colours are --pad-toy-ink, -canvas, -accent, -night and their like on .pad-toy, light and dark; the script resolves them for the canvas and again when the scheme changes.
