Starfield

Flying through space: stars come out of the dark ahead and rush past, faster the nearer they get. The pointer steers where the ship is heading, holding the button down speeds it up, and warp stretches every star into a streak. stars= how many (600 unless given, 50 to 3000); speed= how fast the ship flies (4, 1 to 20); warp starts it at warp.
