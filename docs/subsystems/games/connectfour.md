# games: connectfour

`{game:connectfour/}` is a local two-player gravity game on a 7 × 6 board.
Choose one of the seven column buttons with pointer or keyboard; pieces fall to the
lowest free row. Four horizontally, vertically or diagonally wins; full board draws.
Full columns and terminal games reject moves. Solid/hollow symbols and accessible
cell labels distinguish players without color. New game resets the whole board.
