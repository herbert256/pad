# amber

Warm scale from cream to dark amber. The relative luminance strictly decreases, including sRGB interpolated samples. PAD original, GPL-3.0; no claim of perceptual uniformity.

Use `padPalette('amber', 9)` or `{pad:palette 'amber', steps=9/}`. Charts accept `palette='amber'`. One step returns the middle color.

The colors also work in print; do not rely on color alone for categories or values. Inspect grayscale using the regression luminance measurements.
