Five in a row

Five in a row: black and white take turns on a square board, and the first to have five of their stones in a line - across, down or diagonal - wins. Against the computer, which weighs every free point by the lines it could make or block there; size= the board (15, from 9 to 19), opens='computer' lets it open, two is a game of two players at one screen.
