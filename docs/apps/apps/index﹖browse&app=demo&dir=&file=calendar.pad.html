calendar.pad 

<p>The <code>{ignore}{calendar}{/ignore}</code> tag draws a month with its events - from a <code>{ignore}{data}{/ignore}</code>
block, a page array or a <code>_data</code> file. The arrows move a month; today is marked.</p>

{calendar data='agenda', title='what'}

<p><code>{ignore}{calendar data='agenda', title='what'}{/ignore}</code></p>

<h2>Your own markup</h2>

<p>As a pair the tag hands over the weeks and their days as rows - <code>$day</code>, <code>$date</code>,
<code>$today</code>, <code>$count</code>, <code>$class</code>, the day's <code>events</code> - so a
mini calendar with a dot per busy day is a few lines:</p>

<table class="mini">
  <tr><th>Mo</th><th>Tu</th><th>We</th><th>Th</th><th>Fr</th><th>Sa</th><th>Su</th></tr>
  {calendar data='agenda'}
  <tr>
    {days}<td class="{$class}" title="{$date}">{$day}{if $count gt 0}<i></i>{/if}</td>{/days}
  </tr>
  {/calendar}
</table>

{ignore}{calendar data='agenda'}
  <tr>
    {days}<td class="{$class}">{$day}{if $count gt 0}<i></i>{/if}</td>{/days}
  </tr>
{/calendar}{/ignore}
