qr.pad 

<p>The <code>{ignore}{qr}{/ignore}</code> tag encodes a text as a QR code and writes it as inline SVG -
no image library, no remote service, no JavaScript. Type a text and scan the result with your phone.</p>

{form 'qr', error='Please correct the errors below.'}
  <p>{input 'text', label='Text or address:', value=$text, rules='required|max:1000'}</p>
  <p><input type="submit" value="Make the QR code"></p>
{/form}

<div class="qr-big">{qr $text, size=220}</div>

<p><code>{ignore}{qr $text, size=220}{/ignore}</code></p>

<h2>Error correction</h2>

<p>The higher the level, the more of the symbol may be damaged or covered - a logo in the
middle - and the more modules it takes.</p>

<table>
  <tr>
    {levels}<th>level='{$level}' - restores {$restores}</th>{/levels}
  </tr>
  <tr>
    {levels}<td>{qr $text, level=$level, size=150}</td>{/levels}
  </tr>
</table>
