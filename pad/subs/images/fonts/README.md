# Packaged social-card font

Noto Sans Regular from the [Noto project's archive](https://github.com/notofonts/noto-fonts/blob/main/hinted/ttf/NotoSans/NotoSans-Regular.ttf), distributed under the adjacent SIL Open Font License. Downloaded 2026-11-02. No font downloads happen at runtime.

`NotoSans-Regular.ttf` SHA-256: `b85c38ecea8a7cfb39c24e395a4007474fa5a4fc864f6ee33309eb4948d232d5`.
`NotoSans-coverage.json` lists inclusive Unicode ranges with nonzero glyphs from its cmap tables. The social-card renderer refuses missing glyphs and complex scripts requiring shaping instead of silently substituting boxes or visually incorrect ordering. Latin, Greek and Cyrillic are supported; CJK, emoji and complex text shaping require a future explicitly packaged font/renderer.
