
<p class="nt-lead">A sequence of moves of a twisty cube in the notation of the
<a href="https://www.worldcubeassociation.org/regulations/#article-12-notation">World Cube
Association</a>: <code>U D L R F B</code> turn a face a quarter clockwise as seen from that face,
<code>'</code> the other way, <code>2</code> a half turn; <code>Rw</code> or <code>r</code> turn two
layers, <code>3Rw</code> three, <code>2R</code> the second layer alone; <code>M E S</code> the middle
slice and <code>x y z</code> the whole cube. The moves are made on a solved cube - white up, green
in front, as the WCA scrambles - and the cube is drawn after them: its six faces unfolded as a
net, or three of them seen from above. A screen reader hears every face row by row.</p>

<p class="nt-tag">Written <code>{notation:cube "R U R' U'"/}</code> - in double quotes, as the moves
hold single ones. Brackets group moves and a number after them repeats the group,
<code>(R U R' U')6</code>. <code>case</code> draws the cube the moves solve - an algorithm's case;
<code>view='cube'</code> the cube seen from above, <code>layers=</code> 2 to 7, <code>size=</code> the
width in pixels.</p>

  <h2 id="cases-title">The case an algorithm solves</h2>
  <p class="nt-about">case draws the cube the moves bring back to solved - here the T permutation, which swaps two edges and two corners of the top layer - and a group repeated six times is no change at all.</p>
  <div class="nt-pair">
