A deal of contract bridge written as the Deal tag of PBN, the Portable Bridge Notation every bridge program reads and writes - the seat of the first hand and a colon, then the four hands clockwise, each its spades, hearts, diamonds and clubs apart by dots, a dash for a hand not shown - drawn as the diagram of a bridge column: North at the top, South at the bottom, West and East either side, every suit from the ace down, the compass in the middle with the dealer ringed and the vulnerable sides red. A screen reader hears every hand card by card. A card dealt twice, a rank that is none and hands of different sizes are named.
