Semaphore

Text in flag semaphore: a figure for every letter, holding two flags - red and yellow, as they are flown at sea - at two of eight positions around the body. The positions go round the circle as the code has them: A to G one arm down and the other going round, H to N from the first position, and on; J and V break the pattern. Digits follow the numeral sign as the letters A to K, and letters after digits follow J.

Written {notation:semaphore 'PAD'/}; size= is the width of a figure.

Words

A figure for every letter; N and D, the figures of the nuclear disarmament sign, are both arms down and one arm up.

Template 
{notation:semaphore 'Hello'/}
{notation:semaphore 'N D', size=48/}
