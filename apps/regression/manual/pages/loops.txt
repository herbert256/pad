<p>Most repetition in PAD is a data tag iterating its rows - that story is on
<a href="?pages/levels_and_occurrences">levels and occurrences</a>. This page is about the
counting loops, and about steering any loop from inside.</p>

<h2>while and until</h2>

<p><code>{while}</code> renders its content as long as its condition holds, and
<code>{until}</code> renders until its condition becomes true - checked before
each pass, so an <code>until</code> that is already true renders nothing.
<code>{increment}</code> and <code>{decrement}</code> step a variable
by one:</p>

<h2>Steering a loop: continue, cease and break</h2>

<p>All three name the tag they act on, so they reach the right loop from however deep they
are written. <code>{continue 'tag'/}</code> skips the rest of the current
occurrence; <code>{cease 'tag'/}</code> is the graceful stop - the current
occurrence finishes, then the loop ends; <code>{break 'tag'/}</code> is the hard
stop, ending the occurrence mid-way:</p>
