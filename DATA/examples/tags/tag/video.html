{video} embeds a YouTube or Vimeo video without letting the video site run anything before the visitor asks for it (lib/video.php). The page shows a poster with a play button; only a click on the button puts the player's iframe in the poster's place and moves the focus into it.

The poster of a YouTube video is its thumbnail on i.ytimg.com - an image, no script and no cookie - and the player comes from youtube-nocookie.com with autoplay=1&rel=0. A Vimeo video gets a quiet drawn poster unless poster names a picture, and its player is asked with dnt=1. The small script that swaps poster and player is written once per request, with the CSP nonce when $padCsp asks for one. Without script the button is hidden and a <noscript> link to the video on its own site stands in its place.

A file of www/<application>/ - .mp4, .webm, .ogv or .mov - is written as <video controls preload="none" playsinline> with its poster: nothing is loaded before play. A start time is written in the address - t=, start= or #t=, as 90, 1m30s or 1:30; the start option says the same, but it is also the handling option that skips occurrences, and as such it currently leaves the tag with nothing to write. ratio sets the proportion of the box. The colours are --pad-video-* custom properties on .pad-video.

title is required - it is what a screen reader says for the button and the player. A missing title and an address that is no known video are errors under the strict check.
