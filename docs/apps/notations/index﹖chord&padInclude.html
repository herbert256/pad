A chord of a guitar or a ukulele drawn as the box of a chord chart: the strings standing up, the frets across, a dot for every finger and a bar where one finger lies across the strings, o over a string played open and × over one not played, the note of every string below. A chord name - C, Am, F#m7, Bbmaj7, Dsus4, G7/B - is drawn in the shape a player learns first, or a shape found for it from its notes; a shape can be given as its frets.

Written {notation:chord 'Am'/} - bare, {chart:chord} is the chord diagram of the charts. instrument='ukulele' tunes it G C E A, frets='x32010' gives the shape.

Guitar chords

The open chords and the barre chords of a chord chart, each in the shape a player learns first.

Template
{notation:chord 'C'/} {notation:chord 'G'/} {notation:chord 'Am'/} {notation:chord 'F'/}
{notation:chord 'Bm'/} {notation:chord 'E7'/} {notation:chord 'Dsus4'/} {notation:chord 'Cmaj7'/}

Shapes found and shapes given

A chord no chart table holds gets a shape found from its notes; frets written out are drawn as they are and named by their notes; a name with frets= is drawn in that shape.

{notation:chord 'Cdim7'/} {notation:chord 'F#9'/} {notation:chord 'G/B'/}
