Tags/Charts and codes/palette:*

{palette:*}

The colours of a palette as swatches with their hex values - 29 of them: viridis and its family, the colour-blind safe Okabe-Ito and Tol schemes, ColorBrewer's scales - the same palettes palette= gives a chart.

Charts and codes Single tags

Syntax 

{pad:palette 'name'/}
{pad:palette 'viridis', steps=7/}
{pad:palette 'okabe-ito', title='Our chart colours'/}
{chart:bar data='sales', value='north, south', palette='viridis'/}

Parameters

name
The palette, a file of pad/subs/palettes/types/: batlow, blues, brbg, cividis, dark2, earth, glasbey, greens, greys, inferno, magma, okabe-ito, oranges, paired, pastel, piyg, plasma, puor, rdbu, rdylbu, romao, tol-bright, tol-muted, tol-vibrant, turbo, vik, viridis, ylgnbu, ylorrd. padPalette ( 'viridis', 7 ) answers the same colours to a page's PHP.

Options

steps
How many colours, 1 to 256: a sequential or diverging palette sampled evenly from its first colour to its last, a cyclic one evenly round its circle, never its first colour twice, a categorical one its colours in order, starting again past its last. Without it a categorical palette shows its own, a cyclic one twelve, the others nine.
title
The name of the row for a screen reader, else the palette's name and the count.

Description

{pad:palette} shows the colours of a palette as a row of swatches, each with its hex value written on it in black or white, whichever reads better. A palette is a file of pad/subs/palettes/types/ - its name, its type and its colours - and the files there are the palettes there are; the subsystem is pad/subs/palettes/.

Four types. The sequential ones run from low to high: viridis, magma, plasma, inferno and cividis from matplotlib's published 256-colour tables, perceptually uniform, readable in grey and by colour-blind eyes, Fabio Crameri's batlow, made the same way, ColorBrewer's blues, greens, oranges and greys and its multi-hue ylgnbu and ylorrd, and Google's turbo, a smooth rainbow for reading a value off its hue - neither in grey nor for colour-blind eyes. The diverging ones meet in a light middle - ColorBrewer's rdbu, brbg, piyg, rdylbu and puor and Crameri's vik. The cyclic one goes round, for an angle, a direction, the hour or the month: Crameri's romaO, whose last colour is next to its first, so twelve steps of it are twelve colours, none twice. The categorical ones tell things apart: okabe-ito and Paul Tol's bright, muted and vibrant, made for every kind of colour vision, ColorBrewer's pastel, dark2 and paired - six pairs of a light and a dark of one hue, for categories that come in twos - PAD's own earth and, for many categories, glasbey's 32 colours, each as far from those before it as it can be.

The same palettes colour a chart: palette='viridis' on any kind of {chart:bar} puts the palette's colours on its series - a scale spread over the series the chart has, so three series are its two ends and its middle - and on the heatmap's ramp, as custom properties on the svg. A chart drawn in the browser (client) gets the same colours. padPalette ( 'viridis', 7 ) answers them to a page's PHP.

The row is a list - role="list", a swatch an item - so a screen reader counts the colours and reads their values.

Colour palettes

Each kind has its own description and usage example:

Examples

Viridis in seven steps

A perceptual scale sampled evenly from its first colour to its last, each hex value on its swatch.

Templateexamples/palette/1-viridis.pad 
{pad:palette 'viridis', steps=7/}
