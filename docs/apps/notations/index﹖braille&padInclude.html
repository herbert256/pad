Text in Unified English Braille, grade 1 - a cell for every letter, no contractions - written with the characters of Unicode's braille block, the text itself the name a screen reader reads. A capital takes the capital sign, a word in capitals the capitals word indicator, a number the numeric indicator; punctuation and accented letters are written as UEB writes them.

Written {notation:braille 'Hello'/}; cells draws the cells as raised dots, each with the character it stands for under it.

Text

A capital takes the capital sign, a word in capitals the capitals word indicator - and its terminator when small letters follow - punctuation its own cells, an accented letter the modifier of its accent.

Template
<p>{notation:braille 'Hello, World!'/}</p>
<p>{notation:braille 'NASA and PADs'/}</p>
<p>{notation:braille}Café au lait, s'il vous plaît?{/notation:braille}</p>

Numbers

The numeric indicator, then the digits as the letters a to j; a decimal point stays in the number; a letter a to j right after a number takes the grade 1 indicator.

Template
<p>{notation:braille 'Room 101'/}</p>
<p>{notation:braille '3.14'/}</p>
<p>{notation:braille 'Gate 4b'/}</p>

Raised dots

cells draws every cell as its six dots, the raised ones full, with the character it stands for under it - a sign, an indicator, stands for nothing of its own.

Template
{notation:braille 'Lift 2', cells/}
{notation:braille 'Fire exit this way', cells=10/}
