Toys

{toy} puts a small live simulation or visual toy on the page - a Game of Life, a flock of birds, a double pendulum, falling sand, the Mandelbrot set. The server checks the options it is given and writes its frame; the toy's script, written once per page, runs it. There are 19 of them - every toy is a file of pad/toys/types/, and the toys application shows them all.

PHP
 HTML
fragments/toys_1.pad 		Result 
{toy 'life', pattern='glider', wrap/}
Game of Life

Three ways to write one

The tag names the toy: {toy 'life'/}. The toy: type does the same, {toy:life/}, and so does the bare name, {life/}, as long as no tag, store, field or sequence has that name. Every toy can be touched: draw cells, drag a bob, throw a planet, stir the current - and has its controls below it, each with a label, and the keys it reads while it has the focus.

Patterns and rules between the tags

A Game of Life takes a pattern of its own between its tags, a row a line - O alive, . dead - and an L-system its own rules: the word to start with, the turn, and a line per letter that is rewritten. They are taken as written, braces and all.

PHP
 HTML
fragments/toys_2.pad 		Result 
{lsystem}
  axiom: X
  X: F[-X][X]F[-X]+FX
  F: FF
  angle: 25
{/lsystem}
L-system

Options

Each toy has a few options of its own - the algorithm and the bars of a sort, the birds of a flock, the rule of an ant - checked on the server: a number out of its bounds or a word the toy does not know is named under the strict check.

PHP
 HTML
fragments/toys_3.pad 		Result 
{sorting algorithm='merge', bars=40, order='reversed', speed=60/}
Sorting algorithms

Waiting, and the same start every time

seed= makes what a toy picks at random the same on every load - the scattered cells, the birds, the shells of the fireworks. paused has a toy wait for its Play button, and every toy waits when the visitor asks for reduced motion; a drawing toy then shows its figure finished, as a still picture.

PHP
 HTML
fragments/toys_4.pad 		Result 
{boids birds=90, cohesion=8, seed=2026, paused/}
Boids
  • A toy holds while the page is hidden or the toy is scrolled out of view, and goes on where it was when it is seen again; its root carries data-state - playing, paused or held.
  • The colours are custom properties on .pad-toy - --pad-toy-accent, --pad-toy-canvas, --pad-toy-night and their like - with light and dark defaults; a canvas cannot paint them as they are, so the script resolves them for the page's scheme and again when it changes.
  • The script carries the CSP nonce, as the interactive tags' scripts do, and a toy a fragment or a live region brings later starts as well.
