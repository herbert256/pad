Tags/Toys/toy:harmonograph

{toy:harmonograph}

The Victorian drawing machine.

Toys Either form

Syntax 

{toy:harmonograph/}
{harmonograph/}

Description

One of the 19 toys of {pad:toy}, written {toy:harmonograph/}, {toy 'harmonograph'/}, {harmonograph ...}, bare, when no tag, field or store has the name.

The Victorian drawing machine: two pendulums swing a pen from side to side and two more swing it up and down, each a little slower than the last as it dies away, and the pen draws the figures their frequencies make - lissajous knots, roses, spirals that wind into the middle. Change the frequencies with the sliders, detune one a hair for the slow drift that makes the figure, or let Random pick. frequencies= the four pendulums - x, x, y, y - '2, 3, 3, 2.01' unless given (0.5 to 10 each); damping= how fast they die away (3, 0 to 20); rotary turns the paper in a circle under the pen as well.
