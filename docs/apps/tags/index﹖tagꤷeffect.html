Tags/Widgets/effect:*

{effect:*}

Motion and decoration on a text or a block, CSS first - 23 of them, from a typewriter, a glitch and a marker stroke to a scroll reveal, a marquee, loaders and confetti.

Widgets Either form

Syntax 

{effect:name ...} ... {/effect:name}
{effect:typewriter speed=14, delay=0, caret='bar', mono} ... {/effect:typewriter}
{effect:glitch repeat=3, duration=2.5} ... {/effect:glitch}
{effect:shimmer} ... {/effect:shimmer}
{effect:gradient colors='#f97316, #db2777', angle=90, animate} ... {/effect:gradient}
{effect:wave height=0.25} ... {/effect:wave}
{effect:highlighter color='gold', scroll} ... {/effect:highlighter}
{effect:underline style='line', color='#e11d48', thickness=2, hover} ... {/effect:underline}
{effect:strike style='line', scroll} ... {/effect:strike}
{effect:encircle style='circle', thickness=2, hover} ... {/effect:encircle}
{effect:rotator hold=2.5, repeat=3} teams, makers, you {/effect:rotator}
{effect:decipher duration=1.2, glyphs='symbols', mono} ... {/effect:decipher}
{effect:countup to=1250, from=0, prefix='€', suffix='+', scroll/}
{effect:marquee duration=20, direction='left', gap=48, label='name'} ... {/effect:marquee}
{effect:reveal motion='up', distance=32} ... {/effect:reveal}
{effect:scrollprogress at='top', size=3, color='#e11d48'/}
{effect:tilt angle=8, toward='top-right', glare} ... {/effect:tilt}
{effect:nudge style='bounce', repeat=2, delay=0} ... {/effect:nudge}
{effect:spotlight at='top', color='#f59e0b'} ... {/effect:spotlight}
{effect:spinner style='ring', label='Loading', size=24/}
{effect:skeleton shape='text', lines=3, height=160, size=48, label='Loading'/}
{effect:confetti pieces=120, spread=70, seed=7/}

Parameters

kind after colon
The effect, a file of pad/subs/widgets/effects/types/: confetti, countup, decipher, encircle, flipnumber, glitch, gradient, highlighter, marquee, nudge, reveal, ripple, rotator, scrollprogress, shimmer, skeleton, spinner, spotlight, strike, tilt, typewriter, underline, wave. The effect: prefix selects it - {effect:typewriter}...{/effect:typewriter}.
first
The effect's own first parameter, the second of {effect:typewriter}: the text of a single tag - {effect:typewriter 'Hello'/} - or the number of a count-up, {effect:countup 1250/}.

Options

duration
Seconds one run takes: a glitch's cycle, a shimmer's sweep, a marquee's round, a count.
delay
Seconds before it starts.
repeat
How often an animation that repeats runs - 3 unless given, forever as the author's choice.
speed
typewriter: letters a second (14).
caret
typewriter: bar or none.
mono
Bare option, typewriter and decipher: monospaced letters, one step or one sign a letter exactly.
glyphs
decipher: the signs it settles out of - symbols, letters, digits or binary.
colors
gradient, confetti: CSS colours between commas.
color
highlighter, underline, strike, encircle: the ink; scrollprogress: the bar; spotlight: the light; spinner: the spinner.
thickness
underline, strike, encircle: the stroke in pixels, 1 to 12.
hover
Bare option, underline and encircle: drawn while the pointer is on it or the focus is in it.
angle
gradient: the direction in degrees (90); tilt: how far it leans (8).
animate
Bare option, gradient: the colours flow along the letters.
height
wave: how far a letter rises, in em (0.25); skeleton: the picture of a block or a card, in pixels (160).
from
countup: the number it starts at (0); to the one it ends at.
prefix
countup: a text before the number, suffix one after it.
scroll
Bare option, countup, highlighter, underline, strike and encircle: counted or drawn as it scrolls into view.
direction
marquee: left or right.
gap
marquee: pixels between one round and the next (48).
label
marquee: the name of the band for a screen reader; spinner and skeleton: what it says (Loading).
motion
reveal: up, down, left, right, fade or zoom; distance how far in pixels (32).
toward
tilt: the corner that comes toward the viewer - top-right, top-left, bottom-right, bottom-left.
glare
Bare option, tilt: a sheen slides over the card as it leans.
at
spotlight: where the light rests - top, center, a corner, or two percentages; scrollprogress: top or bottom.
style
spinner: ring, dots, bars, pulse or orbit; underline: line, double, dashed or dotted; strike: line or double; encircle: circle or box; nudge: bounce, shake, pulse, wiggle or heartbeat.
size
spinner: pixels (24); skeleton: the avatar's (48); scrollprogress: the bar's height (3).
shape
skeleton: text, block, avatar, card or media - the shape of what is still on its way.
lines
skeleton: the lines of text (3, media 2).
hold
rotator: seconds each phrase stands (2.5).
pieces
confetti: how many (120); spread the angle they fly out in; seed which pieces.

Description

{effect:typewriter} puts motion or decoration on a text or a block - a line typed out, a glitch, a sweep of light, gradient letters, a wave, a marker stroke, a line drawn under, through or round the words, phrases that take turns, a heading that settles out of changing signs, a number counted up, a scrolling band, a block that comes into view, a bar the scrolling fills, a card that leans, a nudge that draws the eye, a spotlight, a loader, a placeholder while a part loads, a burst of confetti. CSS first: every effect but confetti and decipher is CSS alone, written once per page with the CSP nonce, and their scripts are written the same way. Every effect is a file of pad/subs/widgets/effects/types/, its look in www/assets/effects/; the files of types/ are the effects there are.

An effect has two explicit forms: {effect:typewriter}Hello{/effect:typewriter} and {effect:typewriter}Hello{/effect:typewriter}. A pair works on its content, rendered first; a single tag on its first parameter, {effect:typewriter 'Hello'/}, or on nothing at all, {effect:spinner/} and {effect:confetti/}.

Every effect keeps to the same rules. Its animation stands inside @media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference): a visitor who asks for less motion gets the content still and whole - typed out, counted, revealed, marked - never hidden. A screen reader reads the text once, as it ends: the letters of a wave and the layers of a glitch are a copy hidden from it, the typewriter's text is whole in the page while the CSS uncovers it, a count-up announces its last number. What repeats runs repeat= times, three unless given; the marquee, which moves for as long as it is shown, stops while the pointer or the focus is on it and has a Pause button that needs no script. The colours are --pad-effect-accent, -accent2, -accent3, -text, -mark and -track on .pad-effect, light and dark, falling back to the frame's own colours, so a theme colours them.

Examples

Typed out

A line typed letter by letter behind a caret - the text whole in the page all along.

Templateexamples/effect/1-typewriter.pad 
<h3>{effect:typewriter}Hello, world{/effect:typewriter}</h3>
{effect:typewriter 'npm install pad', mono/}
Resultopen alone

Hello, world

npm install pad

A marker stroke

A <mark> drawn behind the words, left to right.

Templateexamples/effect/2-highlighter.pad 
Read {effect:highlighter}the terms{/effect:highlighter} before you sign.
Resultopen alone
Read the terms before you sign.

Counting up

A number counted by the CSS; a screen reader reads the last one.

Templateexamples/effect/3-countup.pad 
{effect:countup to=1250, suffix='+'/} customers
Resultopen alone
+1250+ customers

Loaders

One effect for every loader, a status with its label.

Templateexamples/effect/4-spinner.pad 
{effect:spinner/} {effect:spinner style='dots', label='Saving'/} {effect:spinner style='bars'/}
Resultopen alone
Loading Saving Loading

Gradient text

Letters painted in the colours given.

Templateexamples/effect/5-gradient.pad 
<h3>{effect:gradient colors='#f97316, #db2777'}Launch week{/effect:gradient}</h3>
Resultopen alone

Launch week
