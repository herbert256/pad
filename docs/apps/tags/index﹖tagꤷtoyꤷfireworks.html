Tags/Toys/toy:fireworks

{toy:fireworks}

Shells climb into a night sky and burst into sparks that fall and fade.

Toys Either form

Syntax 

{toy:fireworks/}
{fireworks/}

Description

One of the 19 toys of {pad:toy}, written {toy:fireworks/}, {toy 'fireworks'/}, {fireworks ...}, bare, when no tag, field or store has the name.

Shells climb into a night sky and burst into sparks that fall and fade: a round peony, a ring, a drooping willow, a palm and a crackling crossette. Click the sky to send one up to that spot; the show goes on by itself as well. rate= how many shells the show sends up by itself, from 0 - only the ones clicked - to 10 (4 unless given); gravity= how fast the sparks fall (5, 0 to 10); shell= one kind of shell for the whole show, else a mix of all.
