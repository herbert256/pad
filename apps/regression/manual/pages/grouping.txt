<p>The <code>group</code> option folds the rows of any data tag into one occurrence per
distinct value of a field. Each group holds that value, <code>$count</code> - the number of
rows in the group - and <code>rows</code>, the group's own rows as nested data. Inside
<code>{rows}</code> a field is the row's own value again.</p>

<h2>Aggregates</h2>

<p><code>sum</code>, <code>avg</code>, <code>min</code> and <code>max</code> name the fields to
aggregate over each group's rows. The aggregate is stored under the field's own name -
<code>{$total}</code> above is the sum - and under the aggregate joined to it -
<code>{$sum_total}</code>, <code>{$avg_total}</code> - so one field can be
summed and averaged at once. A field list such as <code>sum='total, quantity'</code>
aggregates each of them.</p>

<h2>In order with the other options</h2>

<p>Handling options run in the order they are written. Groups come in the order their first
row appears: a <code>sort</code> written before the group orders the groups and the rows
inside them, a <code>sort</code> written after it orders the groups - also on an aggregate,
as above. <code>first</code>, <code>page</code> and <code>where</code> written after the group
count groups: <code>{orders group='customer', where='$count gt 1'}</code> keeps the
customers with more than one order. Several fields - <code>group='year, month'</code> - group
on the combination, and <code>{rows group='month'}</code> groups again inside a
group.</p>
