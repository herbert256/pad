A few words as a comma list: a native text field that posts the list as it is typed - 'pad, php, forms' - and where the script runs a chip per word with a button that takes it out, a word added with Enter or a comma, the last one taken back with Backspace in the empty field. The list the form posts is then a hidden field, joined with ', ', the same text a field without the script posts. suggestions= - a list, a store named by data= or the lines between the tags - are offered as the field is typed in (a datalist); most= the most words it takes, a word past it is not added.

{pad:metatitle='Tag input'/}{include:about/}{input:form'try', class='inputs-try', error}{input:taginput'topics', label='Topics', value='pad, forms', suggestions='pad, php, forms, htmx, charts, games', rules='required|max:80'/}{input:taginput'guests', label='Guests', most=5/}<buttonclass="inputs-send">Send</button>{/input:form}{include:received/}{include:template/}
