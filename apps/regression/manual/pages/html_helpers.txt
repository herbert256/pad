<p>The result is easiest to see in the page source: the button reads
<code>&lt;button disabled title="Saves the &amp;quot;draft&amp;quot; &amp;lt;now&amp;gt;" aria-expanded="false"&gt;</code>
and the div <code>class="panel active"</code>.</p>

<h2>attrs</h2>

<ul>
  <li><code>name=expression</code> - the attribute with its value, quoted and escaped.</li>
  <li>A boolean HTML attribute - <code>disabled</code>, <code>checked</code>, <code>selected</code>, <code>required</code>, <code>hidden</code> ... - is written bare when its value is true and left out when it is not.</li>
  <li>Any other attribute is left out for FALSE, written bare for TRUE, and otherwise written with its value: the text <code>false</code> stays <code>aria-expanded="false"</code>.</li>
  <li>The names are attribute names, dashes included - <code>data-id</code>, <code>aria-label</code> - and never engine options: <code>content=</code> is the meta attribute.</li>
</ul>

<h2>classes</h2>

<ul>
  <li>An expression without a name - a string of names, or an array - is always in.</li>
  <li><code>name=condition</code> puts the name in when the condition holds.</li>
  <li>The list is deduplicated and escaped.</li>
</ul>
