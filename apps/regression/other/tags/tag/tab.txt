<p><code>{tab}</code> is the item of the three widgets that take a list of labelled parts:
<a href="?tag/tabs"><code>{tabs}</code></a> (a tab and its panel),
<a href="?tag/accordion"><code>{accordion}</code></a> (a <code>&lt;details&gt;</code> with
the label as its summary) and <a href="?tag/carousel"><code>{carousel}</code></a> (a slide
with the label as its caption). It prints nothing where it stands: its content is rendered,
trimmed, and handed to the nearest of those owners above it, which builds its markup from
the items once its own content has rendered.</p>

<p>Because the hand-over goes to the nearest owner and not to the direct parent, a tab may
stand inside an <code>{if}</code>, or inside a loop that makes a tab per row of data:
<code>{tabs}{plans}{tab $name}...{/tab}{/plans}{/tabs}</code>.</p>

<p>Under the strict check a <code>{tab}</code> outside any owner is an error, as is one that
never closes, and a tab of a <code>{tabs}</code> or an <code>{accordion}</code> without a
label. A carousel slide may go without a label; it then has no caption.</p>
