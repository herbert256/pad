Tags/Toys/toy:sand

{toy:sand}

A falling sand box: paint with sand that piles up, water that runs and finds its level, walls, wood that burns.

Toys Either form

Syntax 

{toy:sand/}
{sand/}

Description

One of the 19 toys of {pad:toy}, written {toy:sand/}, {toy 'sand'/}, {sand ...}, bare, when no tag, field or store has the name.

A falling sand box: paint with sand that piles up, water that runs and finds its level, walls, wood that burns, fire that rises and spreads, and plants that grow wherever water touches them - each grain moved by a few rules of its own, every frame. Pick a material and paint with the pointer, the number keys pick one too. width= and height= the grains across and down (160 by 100 unless given, 60 to 300 and 40 to 200); material= the one to paint with first, sand unless given; brush= its size (3, 1 to 12); scene= what the box starts with - hourglass, garden or empty.
