page's files. A directory names a part of the application; here the shop the
<em>Clean URLs</em> page routes through:</p>

<p>The routes <code>products/[id]</code> and <code>blog/[year]/[slug]</code> are not in the
list: a bracketed page has no one address. Also left out are <code>_</code> entries,
directories that hold a <code>_guard.php</code>, pages with no template whose PHP only
redirects or writes, pages whose template says <code>{meta sitemap=false/}</code>,
and every page or directory listed in <code>$padSitemapSkip</code>.</p>

<p>With <code>$padSitemap = TRUE</code> in <code>_config/config.php</code> the application
answers <code>?sitemap.xml</code> with the same list as the XML search engines read, and
<code>?robots.txt</code> with a line that points to it.</p>
