Tags/Widgets/widget:tooltip

{widget:tooltip}

A short tip shown while the pointer is on its trigger or the trigger has the focus.

Widgets Either form

Syntax 

{widget:tooltip 'Billed every month'}Starter plan{/widget:tooltip}
{widget:tooltip 'What this means'/}

Description

One of the 13 widgets of {pad:widget}, written {widget:tooltip ...}, {widget 'tooltip', ...}, {tooltip ...}, bare, when no tag, field or store has the name.

{tooltip 'Billed every month'}Starter plan{/tooltip} or {tooltip 'What this means'/} - a short tip shown while the pointer is on its trigger or the trigger has the focus: CSS alone shows it, the trigger names it for a screen reader (aria-describedby). The pair's content is the trigger; the single tag gets a small information button. place= puts the tip above (top, the default) or below its trigger. A small script (CSP nonce) hides a shown tip on Escape. widgets/_lib/tooltip.php.

The pair's content is template, rendered first: like {live}, the pair asks for the end walk and frames what rendered.

Example

From the tags application

Its example on the page of {pad:widget}.

Templateexamples/widget/tooltip-1-plans.pad 
<div>The {tooltip 'Billed every month, cancel any time'}Starter plan{/tooltip} costs € 9 a month,
VAT {tooltip 'Value added tax: 21% on most goods'/} included.</div>
Resultopen alone
The Starter planBilled every month, cancel any time costs € 9 a month, VAT Value added tax: 21% on most goods included.
