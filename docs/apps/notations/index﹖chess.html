Chess

A chess position written in FEN, the Forsyth-Edwards Notation every chess program reads and writes, drawn as a board with the pieces as shapes of PAD's own - no font, no image - the coordinates along its edges, squares marked and arrows drawn for a move, a plan or a threat. The placement of the pieces is all it needs; the side to move, the castling rights, the en passant square and the counters are checked when they are written.

Written {notation:chess 'fen'/}; options: flip, highlight=, arrow=, size=.
