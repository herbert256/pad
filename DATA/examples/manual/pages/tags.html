PAD is about {tags}

A single {tag/} or an open/close combination {tag} .... {/tag}

A single tag is closed with a trailing / character. A tag written without it opens a pair, and its closing tag has to follow: under the strict syntax check a tag that has neither is an error - the pair {echo} never closes - a single tag is written {echo .../} - and the lenient walk reads it as a single tag. The slash may stand in front of an opening pipe as well: {echo $x /| upper}. The variable forms - {$x}, {!x}, {#x} ... - are never pairs and take no slash.
