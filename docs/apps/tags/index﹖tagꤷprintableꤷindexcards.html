Tags/Printables/printable:indexcards

{printable:indexcards}

Index cards, ruled as the ones in a card box are.

Printables Tag pairs

Syntax 

{printable:indexcards data='words'}...{/printable:indexcards}

Description

One of the 17 printables of printable:*, written {printable:indexcards data='words'}...{/printable:indexcards}.

{printable:indexcards data='words'}...{/printable:indexcards} - index cards, ruled as the ones in a card box are - a red line under the heading, blue lines below it - as many to the sheet as fit, with crop marks to cut along: the content between the tags is one card, its heading in an <h3>. card= 5x3 (5 by 3 inches, the default), 6x4, a6 or a7; paper= a4 or letter, turned the way more cards fit unless landscape says so; plain leaves out the ruling, skip= and copies= as for labels; padding= (4 mm), size= (10 pt) and align= the card's text.

Example

The indexcards

As the printables application shows it.

Template 
{fake:rows 'word, sentence', rows=4, seed=3, toData='words'/}
{printable:indexcards data='words', scale=0.45, pdf}
  <h3>{$word | capitalize}</h3>
  {$sentence}
{/printable:indexcards}
Result
4 index cards on 1 sheet of A4, 297 × 210 mm
