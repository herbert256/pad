Letters

A letter for a window envelope, one to an A4 sheet, laid out as DIN 5008 lays out its form B: the content between the tags is the letter, its <address> the address, which stands in the window of a DL envelope when the letter is folded in three at its fold marks, under the return line of sender=; its <aside> the block beside it - the date, a reference - its <header> the letterhead and its <footer> the foot of the page, the rest the body. A letter for every row of the data - a mail merge. size= (10 pt) the body. A letter is one page: a longer body is cut at the foot.

The template 

{fake:rows 'name, street, postcode, city', rows=2, seed=6, toData='customers'/}
{printable:letter data='customers', sender='PAD, Main Street 1, 3511 AB Utrecht', scale=0.45, pdf}
  <header><h2>PAD</h2>Main Street 1, 3511 AB Utrecht</header>
  <address>{$name}<br>{$street}<br>{$postcode} {$city}</address>
  <aside>Utrecht, 1 November 2026<br>Our reference: 2026-114</aside>
  <p>Dear {$name},</p>
  <p>Thank you for your order. Your labels are on their way and should be with you within three working days.</p>
  <p>Kind regards,<br>The PAD team</p>
  <footer>PAD - Main Street 1 - 3511 AB Utrecht - hello@example.org</footer>
{/printable:letter}
