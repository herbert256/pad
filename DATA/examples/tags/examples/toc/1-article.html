Choosing the beans

Buy whole beans roasted in the last few weeks.

Light or dark roast

A light roast keeps the fruit; a dark roast trades it for body.

Grinding

Grind just before brewing, coarse for a press and fine for espresso.

Brewing

Pour-over

Bloom the grounds for thirty seconds, then pour slowly.

French press

Steep four minutes and press gently.
