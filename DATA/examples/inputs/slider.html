A range of numbers to drag along: a native <input type="range"> with its value beside it in an <output>, the ends of the range under it. min= and max=, 0 and 100 unless given - a max of min= + 100 when min= is 100 or more - step= 1; unit= follows the value as it is shown - '%', ' kg'. marks= a list of values the track shows a tick at. Without a value the slider stands in the middle, as a range does, and that is what it posts. The script keeps the output - and the value a screen reader says, aria-valuetext - in step as it moves.
