<section class="sx-kind" id="nudge">
  <h2><a href="?effect/nudge">Nudge</a></h2>
  <p>A short movement that draws the eye to something, then lets it be: style= bounce (the default, a hop and a smaller one), shake (side to side, as a field that was filled in wrong), pulse (it swells and settles), wiggle (it rocks about its middle) or heartbeat (two beats). One nudge takes duration= seconds (a second or so, as the style has it), repeat= nudges (2, or forever) one after the other, after delay= seconds. The content stays as it is - a link or a button inside keeps its click and its focus - and nothing around it moves: the nudge is a transform. Under reduced motion it stands still.</p>

<section class="sx-kind" id="rotator">
  <h2><a href="?effect/rotator">Rotator</a></h2>
  <p>One place in a line where its phrases take turns: each stands for hold= seconds (2.5, 0.5 to 30), then slides up and away for the next, round after round - repeat= rounds (3, or forever) after delay= seconds - and the first stands again when they end. The phrases are the text between commas, two to twelve, their markup not drawn; the place is as wide as the widest, so the line does not shift as they turn. The phrases are in the page as a list, one after the other with their commas: a screen reader reads them all, once, and under reduced motion, without the style and where a browser has no lh unit they stand there just so - teams, makers, you. One line: it does not wrap. speed= letters a second (14, 1 to 100), delay= seconds before it starts (0, up to 60), caret= bar or none, mono - the typewriter&#039;s own monospaced letters, in which every step is one letter exactly. Under reduced motion the line stands typed, without a caret.</p>

  <h2><a href="?effect/underline">Underline</a></h2>

<section class="sx-kind" id="wave">
  <h2><a href="?effect/wave">Wave</a></h2>
  <p>The letters rise and fall one after the other, a wave running through the word. The moving letters are a copy hidden from a screen reader; the text stands once beside it for assistive technology, as written. One wave runs through in duration= seconds (1.6), repeat= times (3, or forever), after delay= seconds; height= is how far a letter rises, in em (0.25, 0.05 to 1). The letters are the text&#039;s characters - an accented letter or an emoji with its modifiers counts as one; the markup of a pair&#039;s content is not drawn in the wave. Under reduced motion the text stands still.</p>
