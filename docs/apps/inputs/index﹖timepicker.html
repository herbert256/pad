Time picker

A time of day typed or chosen from the times of a list, every quarter or half hour: a native <input type="time">, which posts 09:30 and can be typed into, with the times to choose from every every= minutes - 30 unless given - from min= to max=, 00:00 to 23:59 unless given: a <datalist> the browser offers, and where the script runs a button that opens them as a list the arrow keys walk. step= stays the time field's own: what the field takes, not what the list offers.

The template 

{meta title='Time picker'/}
{about/}

{form 'try', class='inputs-try', error}
  {timepicker 'pickup', label='Pick-up time', min='08:00', max='18:00', every=15, rules='required|time'/}
  {timepicker 'alarm', label='Alarm'/}
  <button class="inputs-send">Send</button>
{/form}
