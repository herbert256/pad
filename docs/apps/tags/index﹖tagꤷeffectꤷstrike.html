Tags/Effects/effect:strike

{effect:strike}

A line drawn through the words from left to right, once.

Effects Either form

Syntax 

{effect:strike}€49{/effect:strike}
{effect:strike 'was 49'/}

Description

One of the 21 effects of effect:*, written {effect:strike}€49{/effect:strike} {effect:strike 'was 49'/}.

{effect:strike}€49{/effect:strike} or {effect:strike 'was 49'/} - a line drawn through the words from left to right, once: an <s>, text no longer right - the old price beside the new one, a task done - read as it is. style= line (the default) or double; color= the ink (the text's own colour when not given); thickness= whole pixels (1 to 12, a tenth of the letters when not given); duration= seconds the stroke takes (0.5), delay= seconds before it starts. scroll draws it as it scrolls into view, where the browser has scroll-driven animations - a strike has no hover: what it says, that the words are no longer right, is no state of the pointer. Over several lines every line is struck. Under reduced motion the line stands drawn; on paper and in forced colours it is the text's own line-through.

The strike effect

As the sections application shows it.

Template 
<p>Was {effect:strike delay=0.6}€49{/effect:strike}, now <b>€29</b>.</p>
Result

Was €49, now €29.
