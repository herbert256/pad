Blueprint

White lines on the deep blue of a cyanotype drawing: a faint grid on the page, a fine one every 20 pixels and a stronger one every 100 as on drawing paper, the headings lettered in capitals as a title block is, the links the yellow of an engineer's marking pencil - for technical pages, demos and diagrams. The grid is light enough that the dim text still reads at 4.71 to 1 where two of its lines cross.

Readable

A page set for readers with dyslexia, after the British Dyslexia Association's Dyslexia Style Guide: dark text on cream rather than black on white, a plain, evenly spaced sans-serif (Atkinson Hyperlegible where it is installed, else the guide's Verdana, Tahoma or Trebuchet), 16 pixels, the lines 1.6 apart, the letters .06em and the words .16em further apart - within what WCAG 1.4.12 asks a page to bear - paragraphs no wider than 70 characters, bold instead of italics, no block capitals in the side's headings, and the messages in Okabe and Ito's blue, orange and vermilion, darkened to AA: no green and no red, which the guide asks to avoid for colour-blind readers.

Solarized Dark

The deep blue-green and cream of Solarized's dark mode: its base tones for the page, the panels, the side and the code, its blue for the links, its green, yellow and red for the messages. Solarized is Ethan Schoonover's palette (https://ethanschoonover.com/solarized/, (c) 2011 Ethan Schoonover, MIT licence); its values are kept where they reach WCAG AA here and lightened where they do not - the body text is base2, not the base0 that reads at 4.11 to 1 on base02, and the blue, the green, the yellow and the red are lighter than the palette's - so this is a theme after Solarized, not the palette itself.
