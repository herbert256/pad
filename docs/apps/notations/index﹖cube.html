Cube moves

A sequence of moves of a twisty cube in the notation of the World Cube Association: U D L R F B turn a face a quarter clockwise as seen from that face, ' the other way, 2 a half turn; Rw or r turn two layers, 3Rw three, 2R the second layer alone; M E S the middle slice and x y z the whole cube. The moves are made on a solved cube - white up, green in front, as the WCA scrambles - and the cube is drawn after them: its six faces unfolded as a net, or three of them seen from above. A screen reader hears every face row by row.

Written {notation:cube "R U R' U'"/} - in double quotes, as the moves hold single ones. Brackets group moves and a number after them repeats the group, (R U R' U')6. case draws the cube the moves solve - an algorithm's case; view='cube' the cube seen from above, layers= 2 to 7, size= the width in pixels.

Moves on a solved cube

One quarter turn of the right face brings the front up; the three middle slices turned half make the checkerboard; the superflip turns every edge over in its place.

Template 
{notation:cube 'R', size=200/}
{notation:cube 'M2 E2 S2', size=200/}
{notation:cube "U R2 F B R B2 R U2 L B2 R U' D' R2 F R' L B2 U2 F2", size=200/}

The case an algorithm solves

case draws the cube the moves bring back to solved - here the T permutation, which swaps two edges and two corners of the top layer - and a group repeated six times is no change at all.

Template 
{notation:cube "R U R' U' R' F R2 U' R' U' R U R' F'", case, view='cube', size=160/}
{notation:cube "R U R' U' R' F R2 U' R' U' R U R' F'", case, size=200/}
{notation:cube "(R U R' U')6", view='cube', size=160/}
Result

Other cubes

layers= makes a pocket cube of two layers or a bigger one up to seven: Rw turns two layers, 3Rw three, 2R the second layer alone.

Template 
{notation:cube "R U2 R' U'", layers=2, view='cube', size=140/}
{notation:cube "Rw U 2R'", layers=4, size=200/}
{notation:cube "3Rw2 M2", layers=5, view='cube', size=160/}
