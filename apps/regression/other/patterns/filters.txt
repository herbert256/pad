<p class="patterns-lead">The filters of <code>{img}</code> - each a file of
<code>pad/subs/filters/types/</code> in the engine, named in <code>filter=</code>, a comma list applied
in the order written. The picture is made once with GD and kept in <code>_thumbs/</code> like any
thumbnail, the filter and its options part of its name. halftone and ascii write the picture another
way; blurhash, thumbhash, dominant and lqip are placeholders - shown here with the picture made transparent, so
what stands behind it while it loads is what you see.</p>
