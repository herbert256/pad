<p><b>{toy:life}</b> puts a small live simulation or visual toy on the page - a Game of Life, a flock of
birds, a double pendulum, falling sand, the Mandelbrot set. The server checks the options it is
given and writes its frame; the toy's script, written once per page, runs it. There are
30 of them - every toy is a file of <code>pad/subs/toys/types/</code>, and the
<a href="../games/?toys/index">toys</a> gallery in Games shows them all.</p>

<h2>Select a kind</h2>

<p>Use <code>{toy:life/}</code> to select the kind. Every toy can be touched: draw cells, drag a bob,
throw a planet, stir the current - and has its controls below it, each with a label, and the keys
it reads while it has the focus.</p>

<h2>Patterns and rules between the tags</h2>

<p>A Game of Life takes a pattern of its own between its tags, a row a line - <code>O</code> alive,
<code>.</code> dead - and an L-system its own rules: the word to start with, the turn, and a line per
letter that is rewritten. They are taken as written, before the level walks them - a field or a tag
there is not filled in.</p>
