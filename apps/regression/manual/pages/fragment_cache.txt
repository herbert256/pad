

<p>The page cache answers a whole request from a store. The fragment cache keeps a <em>section</em> of a page rendered, while the page around it stays dynamic: on a hit nothing inside the section runs - no tag, no query - and the stored rendering takes its place.</p>

<h2>Two forms</h2>

<ul>
  <li><code>{cache 'name', ttl=300} ... {/cache}</code> - a named section; the name is its key within the application, so the same name is the same section on every page.</li>
  <li><code>{anyTag cache=3600}</code> - the option form on any tag; on a hit the tag's own handler is skipped as well. It is keyed on the page, the tag as written and its evaluated parameters.</li>
</ul>

<h2>What the key depends on</h2>

<p>Everything that changes the rendering must be in the key. Add it with <code>vary</code>: <code>{cache 'basket', vary=$userId}</code> keeps one copy per user. PHP that runs before the template - the page's own <code>.php</code> - runs on a hit too; the saving is the work done inside the section.</p>

<h2>Parts that stay live</h2>

<p>A <code>{nocache}...{/nocache}</code> part of a section renders on every request, the hit included: the section is stored with the part as its source, and the part renders again in the stored rendering. Inside a loop of the section it gets the fields of its row back.</p>

<p>The same works for the page cache: a page it keeps renders its <code>{nocache}</code> parts on every hit. No PHP runs on such a hit - not the page's, nor an <code>_inits.php</code> - so there a part sees the request and session values, <code>_lib</code> and the application's tags.</p>

<h2>Settings and housekeeping</h2>

<ul>
  <li><code>$padFragmentCache</code> - <code>'file'</code> (default, under <code>DATA/cache/fragments/</code>), <code>'apcu'</code>, or <code>FALSE</code> to render every time.</li>
  <li><code>padFragmentForget ( 'name' )</code> drops a named section when what it shows has changed - one kept with <code>vary=</code> per value, <code>padFragmentForget ( 'basket', $userId )</code>; <code>padFragmentForget ()</code> drops them all.</li>
  <li>Closing pipes and end options - <code>{/cache | upper}</code>, <code>toContent</code> - run on a hit as on a fresh rendering.</li>
</ul>
