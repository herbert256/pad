Tags/Toys/toy:life

{toy:life}

Conway's Game of Life: a grid of cells, each alive or dead.

Toys Either form

Syntax 

{toy:life/}
{life/}

Description

One of the 19 toys of {pad:toy}, written {toy:life/}, {toy 'life'/}, {life ...}, bare, when no tag, field or store has the name.

Conway's Game of Life: a grid of cells, each alive or dead, where a dead cell with three live neighbours is born and a live one with two or three lives on. Draw cells with the pointer or the arrow keys and Enter, then play, step, clear or scatter new ones, or drop in a pattern - a glider, a spaceship, the glider gun, a pulsar. width= and height= the cells across and down (64 by 40 unless given, 10 to 160); speed= the generations a second (10, 1 to 60); pattern= what it starts with; wrap joins the edges; rule= another rule of the family, B36/S23 for HighLife. A pattern of your own stands between the tags, a line per row, O or # for a live cell and . for a dead one.

The life toy

As the toys application shows it.

Template 
{life pattern='gun'/}
