A rating to give: a row of max= stars, 5 unless given, each a native radio button of the value 1 to max, in a fieldset whose legend is the label. The arrow keys move along them as along any radio group, and the style alone fills the stars up to the one chosen or pointed at. The script shows a button that takes the rating back, unless the field is required - a radio group, once chosen, cannot be emptied otherwise. The first star has the field's id, the others the id with their value, as the boxes of {input}'s group have.
