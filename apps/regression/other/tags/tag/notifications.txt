<p><code>padNotify ( $user, 'order-shipped', $data, [ 'database' ] )</code> keeps a notification on the
site, under <code>DATA/notifications/</code>; <code>{pad:notifications/}</code> lists the unread ones of the
user logged in, newest first - its title, its text, its time and a <em>Mark read</em> link. The link
brings the page back with the id and a signature of the application, the recipient and the id, so
it marks only that notification of that person. A guest gets nothing.</p>

<p>As a pair the notifications are its rows - <code>id</code>, <code>name</code>, <code>title</code>,
<code>text</code>, <code>html</code>, <code>url</code>, <code>created</code>, <code>read</code> and
<code>link</code> - and the handling options apply to them. From PHP:
<code>padNotifications ( $to, $all )</code> and <code>padNotificationRead ( $id, $to )</code>.</p>

<p>Its examples need notifications kept first, so they are shown, not run.</p>
