Confetti

A burst of confetti from where it stands: the single tag bursts once, when it comes into view; the pair every time something in it is clicked, its button - the content is shown and works as it is. The one effect with a script, written once per page with the CSP nonce (www/assets/effects/confetti.js); the pieces fly on a canvas over the window for three seconds, hidden from a screen reader and taking no clicks. pieces= how many (120, 10 to 500), spread= the angle they fly out in, in degrees (70, 10 to 360), colors= two to eight CSS colours (the accent colours and an amber and a green when not given), seed= which pieces: the same seed the same pieces, on every burst and every load (7 when not given). Under reduced motion, and without script, there is no burst. 

{effect:confetti}<button type="button">Celebrate</button>{/effect:confetti}

The order is placed. 

<p>The order is placed.{effect:confetti pieces=160, seed=11/}</p>

Countup

A number counted up to its value by the CSS alone. It starts at from= (0) and gets there in duration= seconds (2), after delay= seconds: an integer custom property (@property, <integer>) animated by the CSS and shown by a counter - no script. prefix= and suffix= stand before and after it - '€', '+', '%'. scroll counts as the number scrolls into view, where the browser has scroll-driven animations, and in time where it has not. The number is a whole one, from -999999999 to 999999999, written without grouping; counting down is from= above to=. A screen reader reads the last number only - the counting copy is hidden from it; under reduced motion the number stands at its value.

+12500+ pages served, %98% of them from the cache. 

<p><strong>{effect:countup to=12500, suffix='+'/}</strong> pages served,
   <strong>{effect:countup to=98, suffix='%', duration=3/}</strong> of them from the cache.</p>

Decipher

A short heading that resolves out of scrambled signs: for duration= seconds (1.2, 0.2 to 10) its letters are signs that change, then they settle into the text one after the other, left to right - when it comes into view, after delay= seconds, and again when the pointer comes on it or the focus into it. glyphs= the signs: symbols (the default), letters, digits or binary; mono, monospaced letters, in which the signs take the room of the text exactly. The text itself never changes: it stands in the page all the time, read once by a screen reader and selected and copied as it is, and the signs are a layer over it, hidden from a screen reader and taking no clicks, gone when they settle - the effect's script (www/assets/effects/decipher.js) draws them. Without script and under reduced motion the text simply stands there. A short text, 80 letters at most - not for what has to be read while it changes: a password, a figure, a menu.

ACCESS GRANTED 

<h2>{effect:decipher}ACCESS GRANTED{/effect:decipher}</h2>

SIGNAL FOUND - point at it to play it again. 

<p>{effect:decipher glyphs='binary', mono, duration=2}SIGNAL FOUND{/effect:decipher} - point at it to play it again.</p>

Encircle

A ring drawn round a word or a short phrase as a pen circles it on paper: the stroke goes round once and runs a little past where it began. style= circle (the default, an oval) or box, a rectangle drawn by hand; color= the ink (--pad-effect-accent2 when not given); thickness= whole pixels (1 to 12, 2 when not given); duration= seconds the ring takes (0.9), delay= seconds before it starts. scroll draws it as it scrolls into view, where the browser has scroll-driven animations; hover draws it while the pointer is on it or the focus is in it. The ring is an svg over the words that stretches to them, hidden from a screen reader and taking no clicks - the words stay as they are, a link inside works. A word or a phrase: the ring holds one box, so a long text wraps inside it rather than across lines. Under reduced motion the ring stands drawn, or comes at once on hover.

The one thing to remember is the deadline. 

<p>The one thing to remember is {effect:encircle}the deadline{/effect:encircle}.</p>

The answer is 42 - or point at this word. 

<p>The answer is {effect:encircle style='box', color='#16a34a', thickness=3}42{/effect:encircle} - or point at {effect:encircle hover}this word{/effect:encircle}.</p>

Glitch

A digital glitch: for a moment the text tears into slices that jump aside in two colours, then it stands still again. The slices are two copies of the text laid over it, hidden from a screen reader - the text is there once, as written. A burst opens every cycle of duration= seconds (2.5), repeat= cycles (3, or forever), after delay= seconds; the pointer on it, or the focus in it, plays a burst again. The colours are --pad-effect-accent2 and --pad-effect-accent3. Under reduced motion the text stands still, without the copies.

Gradient

Text painted with a gradient: colors= two to six CSS colours between commas, the accent colours --pad-effect-accent and --pad-effect-accent2 when not given; angle= the direction in degrees (90, left to right). animate lets the colours flow along the letters, one pass in duration= seconds (4), repeat= times (3, or forever), after delay= seconds - still under reduced motion. The letters stay text; where a browser cannot paint into letters they are the text's own colour.

Build pages, not plumbing

Highlighter

A stroke of a marker pen drawn behind the words, left to right, once: a <mark>, so a screen reader that tells marked text says so, the words read as they are. color= the ink (a CSS colour, --pad-effect-mark when not given: a pale yellow under dark text, a translucent amber under light text in a dark scheme), duration= seconds the stroke takes (0.8), delay= seconds before it starts; scroll draws it as it scrolls into view, where the browser has scroll-driven animations. Over several lines every line gets its stroke. Under reduced motion the words stand marked. (Named highlighter: {widget:highlight} is the tag that colours code, and a pipe.)

Read the terms of sale before you order - and the price.

Marquee

A band whose content scrolls by without end, a logo strip, a ticker. The content goes round once in duration= seconds (20, 2 to 600), to the left or, direction='right', to the right; gap= pixels between one round and the next (48). It stops while the pointer is on it or the focus is in it, and a Pause button that works without script stops it for good - moving content that goes on for more than five seconds must be able to be paused. label= names the band for a screen reader ('Scrolling content'). The band shows the content twice, end to end; the second copy is inert and hidden from a screen reader, its ids taken off. Under reduced motion it stands still and wraps, shown once, without the button.

Nudge

A short movement that draws the eye to something, then lets it be: style= bounce (the default, a hop and a smaller one), shake (side to side, as a field that was filled in wrong), pulse (it swells and settles), wiggle (it rocks about its middle) or heartbeat (two beats). One nudge takes duration= seconds (a second or so, as the style has it), repeat= nudges (2, or forever) one after the other, after delay= seconds. The content stays as it is - a link or a button inside keeps its click and its focus - and nothing around it moves: the nudge is a transform. Under reduced motion it stands still.

Reveal

A block that comes into view as it is scrolled to: it fades in while it slides up, as far as the scrolling has taken it - a scroll-driven animation (animation-timeline: view()), no script. motion= how it comes: up (the default), down, left, right, fade or zoom; distance= how far it travels, in pixels (32, 0 to 400). Where the browser has no scroll-driven animations, and under reduced motion, the block simply stands there - shown, never hidden. A block already in view when the page opens stands as it is. Only a pair: it wraps what it reveals.

Rotator

One place in a line where its phrases take turns: each stands for hold= seconds (2.5, 0.5 to 30), then slides up and away for the next, round after round - repeat= rounds (3, or forever) after delay= seconds - and the first stands again when they end. The phrases are the text between commas, two to twelve, their markup not drawn; the place is as wide as the widest, so the line does not shift as they turn. The phrases are in the page as a list, one after the other with their commas: a screen reader reads them all, once, and under reduced motion, without the style and where a browser has no lh unit they stand there just so - teams, makers, you. The copy of the first that closes a round is hidden.

Scrollprogress

A thin bar along the top of the window that fills as the reader scrolls the page, from its top to its end: how far into a long article, a manual page, a post they are. A scroll-driven animation (animation-timeline: scroll()), no script. at= top (the default) or bottom; size= its height in pixels (3, 1 to 12); color= the bar (--pad-effect-accent when not given). The bar is decoration, hidden from a screen reader and taking no clicks; it stands where the tag is written, anywhere in the page. Where a browser has no scroll-driven animations, and under reduced motion - its moving is motion the scrolling sets off - there is no bar. Only single: it follows the page's own scrolling, and the reading of a part of it could only be followed while no box around that part clips it - a card with rounded corners, a frame's column - so it is not offered.

Shimmer

A band of light that sweeps across the letters and rests, as on something freshly polished: the text is painted with a gradient - --pad-effect-text with a band of --pad-effect-accent - that moves across it in duration= seconds (2.5), repeat= times (3, or forever), after delay= seconds. The letters stay text, read as they are. Where a browser cannot paint a background into letters, and under reduced motion, it is the text in its own colour.

Skeleton

A placeholder in the shape of what is still on its way, a sheen sweeping over it: grey bones where the lines, the picture or the avatar will stand, while a fragment, a live region or a lazy part loads, and gone when what it stands in for replaces it. shape= text (the default: lines= lines, 3, the last one shorter), block (a picture, height= pixels high, 160), avatar (a circle, size= pixels, 48), card (a block, a title and lines) or media (an avatar beside a title and lines, 2). It is a status (role="status"): a screen reader says its label - label=, 'Loading' when not given - the bones are hidden from it. Its colours are the text colour, faint, so a theme colours it; the sheen sweeps for as long as it is shown - it stands for something going on, as the spinner does - and under reduced motion the bones stand still. Only single: what it stands in for is not there yet.

Spinner

A loading indicator, the one effect for every loader: style= ring (the default), dots, bars, pulse or orbit. It is a status (role="status"): a screen reader says its label - label=, 'Loading' when not given - and the pair's content is a label shown beside it. size= in pixels (24, 8 to 200); its colour is --pad-effect-accent, color= another. It turns for as long as it is shown - it stands for something going on - and under reduced motion it stands still, its label then shown beside it. (style= is no option of PAD's - nothing in pad/options/ or pad/handling/ has the name - so the spinner may have it.)

Spotlight

A block lit by a soft light behind its content: the light sweeps across it from the left and comes to rest where at= says - top (the default), center, bottom, left, right, a corner (top left, bottom right ...) or two percentages, '30% 20%'. The pointer on it, or the focus in it, sweeps it again. color= the light (a CSS colour; --pad-effect-accent, faint, when not given); duration= seconds a sweep takes (2.5), repeat= sweeps (1, or forever), delay= seconds before the first. The light lies behind the content and takes no clicks. Under reduced motion it stands where it rests. Only a pair: it wraps what it lights.

Strike

A line drawn through the words from left to right, once: an <s>, text no longer right - the old price beside the new one, a task done - read as it is. style= line (the default) or double; color= the ink (the text's own colour when not given); thickness= whole pixels (1 to 12, a tenth of the letters when not given); duration= seconds the stroke takes (0.5), delay= seconds before it starts. scroll draws it as it scrolls into view, where the browser has scroll-driven animations - a strike has no hover: what it says, that the words are no longer right, is no state of the pointer. Over several lines every line is struck. Under reduced motion the line stands drawn; on paper and in forced colours it is the text's own line-through.

Tilt

A card that leans in 3D when the pointer is on it or the focus is in it, and settles again when it leaves: CSS alone, so it leans the same way wherever the pointer is - toward= the corner that comes toward the viewer, top-right (the default), top-left, bottom-right or bottom-left; angle= how far, in degrees (8, 1 to 30). glare adds a sheen that slides over it as it leans. The content keeps its links and fields, and nothing moves the page around it. Under reduced motion it does not lean. Only a pair: it wraps what it tilts.

Typewriter

A line of text typed out letter by letter behind a caret, once, then the caret blinks a few times and stays. The CSS uncovers the text from left to right in as many steps as it has letters - the text itself is whole in the page all the time, so a screen reader reads it at once, never in frames. One line: it does not wrap. speed= letters a second (14, 1 to 100), delay= seconds before it starts (0, up to 60), caret= bar or none, mono - the typewriter's own monospaced letters, in which every step is one letter exactly. Under reduced motion the line stands typed, without a caret.

Underline

A line drawn under the words from left to right, once: a stroke of emphasis rather than the filled mark of the highlighter, and a link inside keeps its look and its click. style= line (the default), double, dashed or dotted; color= the ink (--pad-effect-accent when not given); thickness= whole pixels (1 to 12, a tenth of the letters when not given); duration= seconds the stroke takes (0.6), delay= seconds before it starts. scroll draws it as it scrolls into view, where the browser has scroll-driven animations; hover draws it while the pointer is on it or the focus is in it and takes it back when they leave - the underline of a link that grows. Over several lines every line gets its stroke. Under reduced motion the line stands drawn, or comes at once on hover; on paper and in forced colours it is the text's own underline.

Wave

The letters rise and fall one after the other, a wave running through the word. The moving letters are a copy hidden from a screen reader; the text stands once beside it for assistive technology, as written. One wave runs through in duration= seconds (1.6), repeat= times (3, or forever), after delay= seconds; height= is how far a letter rises, in em (0.25, 0.05 to 1). The letters are the text's characters - an accented letter or an emoji with its modifiers counts as one; the markup of a pair's content is not drawn in the wave. Under reduced motion the text stands still.
