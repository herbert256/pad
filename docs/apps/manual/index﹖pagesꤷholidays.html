Public holidays

The public holidays of twenty-eight countries, computed from rules - no service to ask, no table of dates to keep up to date. A country is a file of pad/subs/holidays/types/, named by its two-letter ISO code: at, be, br, ca, ch, cz, de, dk, es, fi, fr, gb, hu, ie, it, jp, lu, mx, nl, no, nz, pl, pt, ro, se, sk, us and za. {pad:holidays} lists the holidays of a year; four helpers answer a page's PHP; {pad:calendar} marks them in a month.

PHP
 HTML
fragments/holidays_1.pad 		Result 
<ul>
  {pad:holidays 'nl', year=2026}
    <li>{$date} - {$name} <i>{$local}</i>{pad:if $type eq 'observance'} (no day off){/pad:if}</li>
  {/pad:holidays}
</ul>
  • 2026-01-01 - New Year's Day Nieuwjaarsdag
  • 2026-04-03 - Good Friday Goede Vrijdag (no day off)
  • 2026-04-05 - Easter Sunday Eerste Paasdag
  • 2026-04-06 - Easter Monday Tweede Paasdag
  • 2026-04-27 - King's Day Koningsdag
  • 2026-05-05 - Liberation Day Bevrijdingsdag (no day off)
  • 2026-05-14 - Ascension Day Hemelvaartsdag
  • 2026-05-24 - Whit Sunday Eerste Pinksterdag
  • 2026-05-25 - Whit Monday Tweede Pinksterdag
  • 2026-12-25 - Christmas Day Eerste Kerstdag
  • 2026-12-26 - Second Day of Christmas Tweede Kerstdag
  • Every row has date, name (English), local (the country's own language), type, region and substitute.
  • type is public for a day off and observance for a national day that is none: Good Friday in the Netherlands, and Liberation Day there in the years that do not divide by five - a day off for the civil service only in 2025, 2030 ...
  • King's Day is the 27th of April, or the 26th when the 27th is a Sunday (2025).
  • where, sort, first and the other handling options work on the rows; lang='fr' picks the language of local for a country of more than one - Belgium, Luxembourg, Switzerland, Canada, Finland.

A region, a table

A region follows its country as ISO 3166-2 writes it: de-by is Bavaria, with Epiphany, Corpus Christi and All Saints' Day added - their region is by. Germany's sixteen states, Scotland and Northern Ireland (gb-sct, gb-nir), Alsace-Moselle and France's overseas departments have holidays of their own. Written as a single tag, the holidays are a table.

Holidays on a weekend

In the United Kingdom a bank holiday on a Saturday or Sunday gets the next weekday that is no holiday yet: Christmas 2021 on a Saturday was made up for on Monday the 27th, Boxing Day on the Sunday on Tuesday the 28th. In the United States a federal holiday on a Saturday is observed on the Friday before, on a Sunday on the Monday after - New Year's Day 2022 on Friday 31 December 2021. Such a day is a row of its own with substitute TRUE. from and to ask for the days between two dates instead of a year.

PHP
 HTML
fragments/holidays_3.pad 		Result 
<p>December 2021 in England:</p>
<ul>
  {pad:holidays 'gb', from='2021-12-20', to='2022-01-05'}
    <li>{$date} {$name}</li>
  {/pad:holidays}
</ul>
<p>The United States, 2021:</p>
<ul>
  {pad:holidays 'us', year=2021, where='$substitute'}
    <li>{$date} {$name}</li>
  {/pad:holidays}
</ul>

Other calendars, other rules

Romania counts Good Friday, Easter and Whitsun from the Orthodox Easter, the Julian computus's Sunday as a Gregorian date - in 2027 five weeks after the Western one. Japan makes up for a holiday on a Sunday on the next day that is no holiday yet, and holds a day between two holidays as the Citizens' Holiday. New Zealand observes Anzac Day on a weekend on the following Monday, a holiday already or not: in 2038 it is Easter Sunday, and Easter Monday is its day. South Africa makes up for a Sunday on the Monday after it.

 HTML
fragments/holidays_6.pad 		Result

  {pad:holidays 'nl', year=2027, where='$name eq "Easter Sunday"'}<li>Netherlands {$date}</li>{/pad:holidays}
</ul>
<p>Japan, May 2026:</p>
<ul>
  {pad:holidays 'jp', from='2026-05-01', to='2026-05-31'}

</ul>

<ul>

Workdays in PHP

function what it answers
padHolidays ( $country, $year, $region = '' ) the rows of a year, as {pad:holidays} hands them over
padIsHoliday ( $date, $country ) whether the date is a public holiday there
padWorkdays ( $from, $to, $country ) the weekdays from one date to the other that are no public holiday, both counted
padAddWorkdays ( $date, $n, $country ) the date $n workdays later - or earlier, for a negative $n
PHP
fragments/holidays_4.php 		HTML
fragments/holidays_4.pad 		Result 
<?php

  $kingsDay = padIsHoliday ( '2026-04-27', 'nl' ) ? 'a holiday' : 'a workday';
  $april    = padWorkdays ( '2026-04-01', '2026-04-30', 'nl' );
  $after    = padAddWorkdays ( '2026-04-24', 1, 'nl' );
  $bavaria  = padWorkdays ( '2026-01-01', '2026-12-31', 'de-by' );

?>
 
<p>27 April 2026 in the Netherlands is {$kingsDay}.</p>
<p>April 2026 has {$april} workdays there.</p>
<p>One workday after Friday 24 April: {$after}.</p>
<p>Bavaria has {$bavaria} workdays in 2026.</p>

27 April 2026 in the Netherlands is a holiday.

April 2026 has 20 workdays there.

One workday after Friday 24 April: 2026-04-28.

Bavaria has 252 workdays in 2026.

  • A country takes its region the same way: 'de-by', 'gb-sct'.
  • An observance counts as a workday; a holiday on a weekend counts once - the weekend already is no workday - and its substitute day is a day off.
  • A country without a file, a region it does not have or a year before 1583 is an error that names what there is.
  • So is a year the country's file does not know: New Zealand's Matariki dates are fixed by law to 2052, and its file knows 2010 to 2052.
