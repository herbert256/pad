A signature drawn with the mouse, a pen or a finger: a canvas, and the picture drawn posted as a PNG data URL - data:image/png;base64,... - in a hidden field. The page's PHP decodes it: base64_decode of what follows the comma is the PNG. Who cannot draw types the name in the field beside it, and the name is drawn on the canvas in a hand of writing; Clear wipes it. width= and height= the canvas, 400 by 150 unless given. A signature needs the script: without it the page says so. After a post that came back the picture is drawn again from the posted field - one that is no PNG data URL is not refilled.
