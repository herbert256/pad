Tags/Printables/printable:staffpaper

{printable:staffpaper}

Music paper, manuscript paper to write music on.

Printables Tag pairs

Syntax 

{printable:staffpaper copies=4/}

Description

One of the 14 printables of printable:*, written {printable:staffpaper copies=4/}.

{printable:staffpaper copies=4/} - music paper, manuscript paper to write music on: empty staves over the width of the sheet, no clef, no bar, as many as fit with room between them to write in - twelve on A4. rastral= the size of a staff, the height of its five lines from the lowest to the highest, as engravers number them (Elaine Gould, Behind Bars, Faber Music 2011, p. 483): 0 9.2 mm, 1 7.9, 2 7.4, 3 7.0 (the default, the size of a part or a piano score), 4 6.5, 5 6.0, 6 5.5, 7 4.8, 8 3.7; lines= 5 (1 to 6 - 6 for guitar tablature, 1 for percussion), its lines a space, a quarter of that height, apart; staves= how many, spread evenly from the top margin to the bottom one; grand pairs them for the piano, the two joined by a line at their start, the room between pairs wider than in one; margin= (15 mm, or 'top left'), color= (#3a3a37), paper= a4, a3, a5, letter or legal, landscape. The lines are drawn exactly, on paper as in the PDF. The content between the tags is a line over the staves - a title, a name - with size= (10 pt) and align=.

Example

The staffpaper

As the printables application shows it.

Template 
{fake:name rows=2, seed=33, toData='pupils'/}
{printable:staffpaper data='pupils', grand, scale=0.45, pdf}
  <b>Piano lesson</b> - {$name}
{/printable:staffpaper}
Result
2 pages of staff paper on 2 sheets of A4, 210 × 297 mm
