<p>A content store is a named block of template, defined once and usable anywhere after it.
<a href="?pages/the_content_tag">The content tag</a> page covers defining one and the ways
two blocks merge; this page is about what the construct does for a whole page: one block,
many tags, each deciding how to wear it.</p>

<h2>A store used by name</h2>

<p>Writing the store's name as a tag renders it. The tag may bring content of its own -
without <code>merge=</code> the store comes first, and <code>merge='bottom'</code> or
<code>merge='replace'</code> say otherwise:</p>

<h2>content= on any tag, and a store with two halves</h2>

<p>Every tag takes a <code>content=</code> option naming a store, with the same
<code>merge=</code> choices. And a store may carry an <b>@</b><b>else</b><b>@</b> of its own: which
half is used is decided by the tag wearing it - the same store gives its first half to a tag
that produced something and its second half to one that came up empty:</p>
