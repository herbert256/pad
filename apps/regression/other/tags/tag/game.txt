<p><code>{game}</code> puts a little game on the page, played in the browser: the server checks
what the game is given and writes its board, and the game's script - written once per page, with
the CSP nonce - plays it. Every game is a file of <code>pad/games/types/</code>, its script and
its style in <code>pad/games/assets/</code>; the files of <code>types/</code> are the games there are.</p>

<p>A game is written three ways, the same game each time: the tag with the game's name,
<code>{game 'memory'/}</code>; the game: type, <code>{game:memory/}</code>; and the bare name,
<code>{memory/}</code>, when no tag, store, field or sequence has that name. The words of a word
game, the clues of a crossword, the questions of a quiz and the picture of a nonogram stand
between the tags, one a line - taken as written, so they may hold braces - or come from a store
named by <code>data=</code>.</p>

<p>The board carries <code>data-result="won"</code> or <code>"lost"</code> when a game ends and
sends a <code>pad-game-end</code> event, which a page can listen to. <code>seed=</code> makes a
game the same on every load - the same shuffle, maze or puzzle. The colours are
<code>--pad-game-ink</code>, <code>-accent</code>, <code>-good</code>, <code>-bad</code> and their
like on <code>.pad-game</code>, light and dark.</p>

<p>Sudoku and crossword can also be printed: <code>paper</code> draws the puzzle without a script,
<code>solve</code> - <code>solution</code> for the crossword - with the answers filled in.</p>
