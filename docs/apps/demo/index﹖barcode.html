Barcodes

The {barcode} tag writes EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A and Code 128 barcodes as inline SVG - sharp at any size and on paper, no font, no image library. A scanner or a phone reads them straight from the screen.

Design Patterns
Paper filters

{barcode $ean, height=50/} - twelve or thirteen digits are an EAN-13; given without its check digit, the tag adds it.
