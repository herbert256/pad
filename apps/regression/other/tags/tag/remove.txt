<p><code>{remove '1..10', prime}</code> runs a sequence in <em>remove</em> mode: every value
that belongs to the named type is dropped, the rest stay - here
<code>1 4 6 8 9 10</code>. It is the same run as
<code>{sequence '1..10', remove, prime}</code>, and the exact inverse of
<a href="?tag/keep">{keep}</a>: together the two split a list in two.</p>

<p>Without a range the candidates run from <code>from</code> (default 1) upward, and
<code>rows</code> counts what is left: <code>{remove prime, rows=5}</code> is the first five
numbers that are not prime, <code>1 4 6 8 9</code>. A stored sequence, named bare, works as the
source as well, and the prefix form <code>{remove:odd rows=5}</code> names the type in the tag.</p>

<p>To invert an <em>action</em> - all but the first three - use the <code>negative</code>
option of <a href="?tag/sequence">{sequence}</a> instead; <code>remove</code> is the inversion
for plays.</p>
