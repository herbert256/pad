<p>Menus, category trees and threaded comments are trees: each row may hold a list of rows
like itself. <code>{pad:tree}</code> renders its body for every row, and inside that
body <code>{pad:recurse}</code> renders the same body again for the current row's
children. <code>{pad:branch}</code> renders only when the row has children - the place
for the <code>&lt;ul&gt;</code> around the next level.</p>

<p>The rows are named by the first parameter or by <code>data=</code> - a
<code>{format:auto}</code> block, an array of the page, a <code>_data/</code> file.
<code>children</code> names the field that holds a row's children, <code>children</code> when
not given.</p>

<h2>Depth and the other properties</h2>

<p><code>depth@tree</code> is 1 for the tree's own rows and one more for every level down -
for indentation. The other properties, such as <code>current@tree</code> and
<code>count@tree</code>, speak of the list being rendered. The <b>@</b><b>else</b><b>@</b> part of
<code>{pad:branch}</code> renders for a leaf, and the handling options
<code>sort</code>, <code>where</code> and <code>reverse</code> on the tree apply to every list,
the children included.</p>
