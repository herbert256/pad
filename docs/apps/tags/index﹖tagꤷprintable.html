Tags/Pictures and media/printable:*

{printable:*}

Pages made for paper - 14 kinds, from sheets of labels and name badges to a letter for a window envelope - the content between the tags one item, repeated over the data and laid out on sheets that print as they show, and come out of the PDF writer alike.

Pictures and media Tag pairs

Syntax 

{printable:name data='rows', ...} ... {/printable:name}
{printable:labels data='products', sheet='a4-3x8'}<b>{$name}</b>{/printable:labels}
{printable:tickets data='seats', pdf}<h2>{$show}</h2><aside>{barcode:qr $code/}</aside>{/printable:tickets}
{printable:letter data='customers', sender='PAD, Utrecht'}<address>{$name}</address><p>...</p>{/printable:letter}

Parameters

kind after colon
The kind, a file of pad/subs/printables/types/: badges, calendar, cards, certificate, envelope, foldcard, graphpaper, indexcards, invoice, labels, letter, placecards, staffpaper, tickets. The printable: prefix selects it - {printable:labels}...{/printable:labels}; it is never reached bare.

Options

sheet
The grid of a sheet, named by its paper and its grid: a4-3x8, a4-2x7, letter-3x10 ... - each kind of a grid has its own.
paper
The paper: a4, a5, a6, letter, legal ...; an envelope dl, c4, c5 or c6.
across
The items across a sheet; down the items down.
width
The width of an item in mm; height its height.
margin
The top and the left margin in mm, one number for both - the grid centred without it.
pitch
The step from an item to the next in mm, across and down.
skip
The items of the first sheet left empty - a sheet of labels used before.
copies
Every item that many times.
padding
The room round an item's text in mm; size its text size in points, align left, center or right.
outline
Bare option: the edges of the labels printed as well.
scale
The preview on the screen made smaller, 0.1 to 3 - paper and PDF keep the size.
pdf
Bare option: links to the sheets as a PDF - the page asked with &padPdf answers them; file= its name.
title
badges: the event on the band; certificate: its title; invoice: the heading of the body.
sender
envelope, letter, invoice: the return address, its lines between commas.
color
badges: the band; certificate: the frame and the title.
signed
certificate: the names under the lines to sign on, between semicolons, a role after a comma.
number
tickets: the number of the first ticket, on a stub without an aside.
card
indexcards: 5x3, 6x4, a6 or a7.
grid
graphpaper: squares, dots or lines.
spacing
graphpaper: the step of the grid in mm, 5 (8 for lines).
major
graphpaper: every that many lines a heavier one, or a bigger dot - millimetre paper is spacing=1, major=10.
year
calendar: its twelve months, this year the default.
month
calendar: the month it starts with, 2026-09; months how many, 1 to 60.
sunday
calendar: bare option, the weeks from Sunday instead of Monday; weeks their ISO 8601 numbers before them.
holidays
calendar: the public holidays of a country of the holidays subsystem marked, nl, de-by.
folds
foldcard: bare option, the folds printed as light lines, where the screen alone shows them.
rastral
staffpaper: the size of a staff as engravers number them, 0 (9.2 mm) to 8 (3.7 mm), 3 (7.0 mm) the default.
staves
staffpaper: how many staves; lines the lines of a staff, 5, 6 for tablature; grand pairs them for the piano.

Description

{printable:labels} makes pages for paper - sheets of labels, name badges, business cards, tickets with a stub, a certificate, envelopes, a letter and an invoice for a window envelope, index cards and folded place cards, a card folded twice - and paper to write and draw on, graph paper and music paper, and a wall calendar. Every kind is a file of pad/subs/printables/types/; the files of types/ are the kinds there are.

The content between the tags is one item - a label, a badge, a letter - rendered once per row of the data, with the handling options of any tag, and laid out by the kind on its sheets. A ticket's stub is its <aside>; a letter's address its <address>, its information block its <aside>, its letterhead and foot its <header> and <footer>; a folded card's front its <header>, its back its <footer>.

On the screen the sheets lie on a grey desk at their size in millimetres. Printed, a page with printables prints the sheets alone, each on a page of its own size. In a {pad:pdf}, or with pdf on the tag, the PDF writer lays out the same sheets - the boxes, rectangles and lines where the screen has them, the barcodes and QR codes drawn. Every item is one region of a sheet: what does not fit is cut at its edge.

Examples

Address labels

A sheet of a4-2x7, the first three labels used before.

Templateexamples/printable/1-labels.pad 
{fake:rows 'name, street, postcode, city', rows=6, seed=21, toData='people'/}
{printable:labels data='people', sheet='a4-2x7', skip=3, scale=0.4}
  <b>{$name}</b><br>{$street}<br>{$postcode} {$city}
{/printable:labels}
