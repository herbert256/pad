Tags/Printables/printable:calendar

{printable:calendar}

A wall calendar, a month to a sheet.

Printables Tag pairs

Syntax 

{printable:calendar year=2027, holidays='nl'/}

Description

One of the 14 printables of printable:*, written {printable:calendar year=2027, holidays='nl'/}.

{printable:calendar year=2027, holidays='nl'/} - a wall calendar, a month to a sheet: the month and its year over a grid of its weeks, a column a weekday, a box to write in for every day, its number in the corner - the public holidays of holidays= marked, their names under the number, Sundays in the colour of the month. year= its twelve months, the year this is the default; month='2027-09' from that month on, one month unless months= (1 to 60) says how many - an academic year is month='2026-09', months=12. sunday starts the weeks on Sunday instead of Monday, weeks puts the ISO 8601 week numbers in a column before them; holidays= a country of the holidays subsystem, 'nl', or with its region, 'de-by' - a public holiday red, an observance grey - the names in the language of the page's locale when the country speaks it, as the month and the weekdays are; color= the month and the marked days (#c0392b); paper= a4, a3, a5 or letter, landscape. The content between the tags stands over every month - a picture, a line of text - with padding=, size= (12 pt) and align= (center); with data= a row is a month, the rows taken in turn.

Example

The calendar

As the printables application shows it.

Template 
{fake:rows 'city, name', rows=3, seed=27, toData='photos'/}
{printable:calendar month='2027-04', months=3, holidays='nl', weeks, data='photos', scale=0.45, pdf}
  <h2>{$city}</h2>
  Photo: {$name}
{/printable:calendar}
Result
3 calendar months on 3 sheets of A4, 210 × 297 mm
