App: notations - Page: abc
PHP
 HTML
abc.pad 		Result 
{pad:meta title='ABC music'/}
<p class="nt-lead">A tune written in <a href="https://abcnotation.com/wiki/abc:standard:v2.1">ABC
notation</a> - the plain-text music notation folk musicians have traded tunes in since the
eighties - drawn as a staff in SVG: a treble clef, the key and time signatures, the notes with
their stems, beams and ledger lines, accidentals, rests, bar lines and repeats, chord names over
the notes. Lines of music in the text are lines of staff in the drawing.</p>

<p class="nt-tag">Written <code>{notation:abc}</code> with the tune between the tags, its header
first - <code>T:</code> the title, <code>M:</code> the meter, <code>L:</code> the unit note
length, <code>K:</code> the key. <code>width=</code> spreads the lines over that many pixels.</p>

{pad:meta title='Braille'/}
<p class="nt-lead">Text in <a href="https://iceb.org/ueb.html">Unified English Braille</a>, grade 1 -
a cell for every letter, no contractions - written with the characters of Unicode's braille
block, the text itself the name a screen reader reads. A capital takes the capital sign, a word

accented letters are written as UEB writes them.</p>

<p class="nt-tag">Written <code>{notation:braille 'Hello'/}</code>; <code>cells</code> draws the cells as
raised dots, each with the character it stands for under it.</p>

App: notations - Page: bridge
PHP
 HTML
bridge.pad 		Result 
{pad:meta title='Bridge deals'/}
<p class="nt-lead">A deal of contract bridge written as the Deal tag of
<a href="https://www.tistis.nl/pbn/">PBN</a>, the Portable Bridge Notation every bridge program
reads and writes - the seat of the first hand and a colon, then the four hands clockwise, each its
spades, hearts, diamonds and clubs apart by dots, a dash for a hand not shown - drawn as the
diagram of a bridge column: North at the top, South at the bottom, West and East either side,
every suit from the ace down, the compass in the middle with the dealer ringed and the vulnerable
sides red. A screen reader hears every hand card by card. A card dealt twice, a rank that is
none and hands of different sizes are named.</p>

<p class="nt-tag">Written <code>{notation:bridge 'N:.63.AKQ987.A9732 A8654.KQ5.T.QJT6 ...'/}</code>.
<code>dealer='N'</code> rings the dealer, <code>vulnerable=</code> is <code>none</code>,
<code>ns</code>, <code>ew</code> or <code>all</code>, <code>points</code> writes the high-card points
of every hand.</p>

<p class="nt-lead">A chord of a guitar or a ukulele drawn as the box of a chord chart: the strings

App: notations - Page: cube
PHP
 HTML
cube.pad 		Result 
{pad:meta title='Cube moves'/}
<p class="nt-lead">A sequence of moves of a twisty cube in the notation of the
<a href="https://www.worldcubeassociation.org/regulations/#article-12-notation">World Cube
Association</a>: <code>U D L R F B</code> turn a face a quarter clockwise as seen from that face,
<code>'</code> the other way, <code>2</code> a half turn; <code>Rw</code> or <code>r</code> turn two
layers, <code>3Rw</code> three, <code>2R</code> the second layer alone; <code>M E S</code> the middle
slice and <code>x y z</code> the whole cube. The moves are made on a solved cube - white up, green
in front, as the WCA scrambles - and the cube is drawn after them: its six faces unfolded as a
net, or three of them seen from above. A screen reader hears every face row by row.</p>

<p class="nt-tag">Written <code>{notation:cube "R U R' U'"/}</code> - in double quotes, as the moves
hold single ones. Brackets group moves and a number after them repeats the group,
<code>(R U R' U')6</code>. <code>case</code> draws the cube the moves solve - an algorithm's case;
<code>view='cube'</code> the cube seen from above, <code>layers=</code> 2 to 7, <code>size=</code> the
width in pixels.</p>

App: notations - Page: karnaugh
PHP
 HTML
karnaugh.pad 		Result 
{pad:meta title='Karnaugh maps'/}
<p class="nt-lead">A Boolean function of two to four variables drawn as its Karnaugh map: the
cells of its truth table laid out in Gray-code order, so two cells side by side - over the edges
too - differ in one variable, each holding 1, 0 or a don't care. The groups of a minimal sum of
products are looped round in colours of their own, wrapping over the edges where they must,
and the minimal expression stands under the map. Every prime implicant is found by trying every
group the map has, and the groups chosen are the fewest that cover the 1s, then the ones with
the fewest variables; the expression is checked against every cell before it is drawn. A screen
reader hears the rows of the map, the groups and the expression in words.</p>

<p class="nt-tag">Written <code>{notation:karnaugh 'm(1, 3, 5) + d(0)'/}</code> - minterms and don't
cares, maxterms <code>M(0, 2)</code>, the output column of a truth table <code>0110 1001</code>, or
an expression between the tags: <code>{notation:karnaugh}F = A'B + AC{/notation:karnaugh}</code>,
with <code>'</code>, <code>~</code> or <code>not</code>, <code>+</code> or <code>or</code>,
<code>^</code> or <code>xor</code>, and two terms side by side for and. <code>vars=</code> names the
variables, <code>form='pos'</code> loops the 0s for a product of sums, <code>numbers</code> writes
the number of every cell.</p>
