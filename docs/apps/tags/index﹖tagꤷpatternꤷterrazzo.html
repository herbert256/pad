Tags/Patterns/pattern:terrazzo

{pattern:terrazzo}

Chips of stone set in a ground of the background.

Patterns Single tags

Syntax 

{pattern:terrazzo seed=7/}

Description

One of the 26 patterns of pattern:*, written {pattern:terrazzo seed=7/}.

Chips of stone set in a ground of the background: angular pieces of the inks the seed picks, a few as large as the size and many small ones filling in between them, each turned its own way, none touching another.

A chip is tried at a place the seed picks, as large as the room there allows - its corners within a circle that keeps clear of every chip laid, less a gap of an eighth of the size - and at most the size across, that most shrinking to a sixth of it as the tries go on, so the large chips are laid first and the small ones fill the holes, a third of the room left free so the ground shows; every square of the size is tried 32 times. A chip is four to seven corners around its middle, each at an angle and a distance of its own; it may run off the picture. The chips laid are kept in a grid of squares of the size and the gap, so a try looks at the squares around it alone. A chip is written from its first corner, each next one from the one before.

A terrazzo pattern
