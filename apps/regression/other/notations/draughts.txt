<p class="nt-lead">A draughts position written as the FEN of PDN, the Portable Draughts Notation:
the side to move, then each colour with its squares, a K before a king. International draughts
on the board of 10 by 10, or English draughts and checkers on 8 by 8 - the playing squares
numbered as both games number them, from Black's side at the top.</p>

<p class="nt-tag">Written <code>{draughts 'W:W31-50:B1-20'/}</code>; <code>board=8</code>,
<code>numbers</code>, <code>flip</code>, <code>highlight=</code>, <code>size=</code>.</p>
