
<p class="nt-lead">Text in the international Morse code of ITU-R M.1677-1: the code written out, its
characters apart by a space and its words by a slash, and under it the signal as it sounds - a
dot one unit long, a dash three, a gap of one unit inside a character, three between characters
and seven between words. The letters, the digits, é and the punctuation of the recommendation;
a character the code has no sign for is reported, not guessed.</p>

<p class="nt-tag">Written <code>{notation:morse 'SOS'/}</code>; <code>plain</code> writes the code
alone.</p>
