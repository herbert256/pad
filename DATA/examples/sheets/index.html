Spreadsheets in a page, computed on the server: {pad:spreadsheet} takes its cells as lines of text, a comma between them, or the rows of data=, and writes a table of what the formulas come to - =B2*C2, =SUM(D2:D9), =VLOOKUP(E2,$I$2:$J$6,2) - with the column letters and the row numbers around it. The formulas are spreadsheet syntax, read by a parser of their own, and call the 126 functions of pad/subs/formulas/types/, which answer what Excel answers. editable makes cells fields that post back and are computed again - in a form, or in a live region without a script of the page's own.

  • BudgetA month of a household: amounts by item, their share of the total, SUMIF adding them up by category, and the largest item found with INDEX and MATCH.
  • LoanA loan calculator in a form: change the amount, the interest or the years and recalculate - PMT for the monthly payment and FV for the balance at the end of every year, the years past the term left empty with IF.
  • GradesTest scores averaged, graded with VLOOKUP on a table of the lowest score of each grade, ranked with RANK, counted with COUNTIF, and a student looked up by name with XLOOKUP.
  • DatesInvoices and their due dates: days added to a date, the weekday of the due date with TEXT and WEEKDAY, a due date in the weekend moved to the Monday, the end of the month with EOMONTH, a reminder a month later with EDATE and the days late with DAYS.
  • EditableA shopping list in a live region: change a quantity, a price or the VAT and the sheet is computed again on the server and swapped in - no JavaScript of the page's own.
  • FunctionsEvery function a formula can call, what it does and an example of it on a sample sheet.
