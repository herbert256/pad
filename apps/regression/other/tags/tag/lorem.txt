<p><code>{lorem}</code> writes placeholder text: "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, ..." and then
Latin words, for a layout that has to be filled before its copy is written. Without a length it
is 50 words. <code>words=</code> gives so many words exactly, made into sentences - fewer than
five are a title, capitalised and without a full stop. <code>sentences=</code> gives so many
sentences of six to fourteen words, <code>paragraphs=</code> so many <code>&lt;p&gt;</code>
elements of four to seven sentences, or of <code>sentences=</code> each when both are given.</p>

<p>The words are picked by the seeded sequence of <code>lib/fake.php</code>, the same on every
machine and every request: a page does not change each time it is loaded, and a test can compare
it. <code>seed=</code> asks for another text - any value, a number or a word - and
<code>varied</code> leaves the classic opening out, so two placeholders on one page do not start
alike. The sequence's own state is put back afterwards, so a page that seeds the
<code>padFake*</code> functions for its data gets the same values with or without a
<code>{lorem}</code> on it.</p>

<p>A length that is no number from 1 to 10000, and <code>words=</code> beside
<code>sentences=</code> or <code>paragraphs=</code>, are strict errors.</p>
