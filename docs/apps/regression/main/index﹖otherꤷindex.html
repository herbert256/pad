670 pages, 670 tests, 0 failed - all ok

Every test here is a page of an application without a suite of its own - the demos, the documentation front, the utilities - fetched over HTTP a dozen at a time, and compared with the prediction of the same name in apps/regression/other/. The pages stay in their own applications; the store holds nothing but what each must answer. A new application lands here as new until its predictions are written - and a page that draws, the clock and the counter among them, answers a /pattern/ pinning its skeleton, everything else an exact body.
