Truchet

Smith's Truchet tiles: squares of the size, each with two quarter circles joining the middles of its sides, turned one way or the other as the seed says - the arcs meet their neighbours' and wander on as endless curves.

Seed 1 - another picture with seed 0 or seed 2. 

{pattern:truchet seed=1, width=960, height=320/}

Its own example 

{pattern:truchet size=20, seed=5, colors='#1d1d1b, #f2c14e'/}

As a background

as='url' writes the pattern as a CSS url() of a data URI, for a style attribute or a style sheet.

Text over a pattern 

<div style="background-image:{pattern:truchet seed=1, as='url'/}; background-size:cover; padding:48px 24px">
  <p style="margin:0; padding:12px 16px; background:#fffc; color:#222; display:inline-block">Text over a pattern</p>
</div>
