Rich form fields, each a tag of its own - a file of pad/subs/inputs/types/ in the engine, written {input:name 'field'/} or, when nothing else has the name, bare. Every one posts a real value in a plain form submit, refills from the post and shows the message of its rules=, as {pad:input} does; its script makes more of a native field where it runs. One form holds them all.

  • colorpickerA colour picked from a row of swatches, typed as a code or chosen in the browser's own picker.
  • comboboxA text field that suggests from a list as it is typed.
  • datepickerA day typed or chosen from a month that opens beside the field.
  • daterangeA first and a last day chosen as one, a stay or a leave.
  • dropzoneA file dropped on a frame or chosen, its name and size listed.
  • durationpickerA length of time, an hour and a half, a lap of 3 minutes 12.
  • mdeditorMarkdown written beside its preview.
  • multiselectA few choices of a list.
  • newpasswordA password chosen, with a meter of its strength and a button that shows it.
  • otpA one-time code, the digits a text message or an app gives.
  • rangesliderA lower and an upper number chosen on one track, a price from 100 to 400.
  • segmentedOne of a few choices shown side by side as one bar, the chosen one filled.
  • signatureA signature drawn with the mouse, a pen or a finger.
  • sliderA range of numbers to drag along.
  • starsA rating to give.
  • stepperA number with a minus and a plus beside it.
  • taginputA few words as a comma list.
  • timepickerA time of day typed or chosen from the times of a list, every quarter or half hour.
  • toggleA switch, on or off.
  • treepickerOne item of a hierarchy, a category of a category, a place in a country.
