<p>An expression is the part of a tag that works out a value: <code>1 + 2</code>,
<code>$price * $qty</code>, <code>$name | upper</code>, <code>$state in ('open',
'pending')</code>. PAD's evaluator reads it - numbers, strings, fields, lists, operators,
functions and pipes - and answers one value. This page goes through all of it; every example
is live, its template beside what PAD made of it.</p>

<h2>Where expressions stand</h2>

<p>An expression is read wherever a tag takes a value: the parameter of <b>{echo}</b> and of
<code>{if}</code> and <code>{elseif}</code>, the right side of
<code>{set $x = .../}</code>, the value of <code>{case}</code> and its
<code>{when}</code>s, the condition of <code>{while}</code> and
<code>{until}</code>, a quoted option such as <code>where='...'</code>, and an
occurrence assignment <code>%name = ...</code> on a tag that loops:</p>

<h2>Values</h2>

<h3>Numbers</h3>

<p>Whole numbers, decimals, a decimal that starts with its point (<code>.5</code>) and
exponents (<code>1e3</code>, <code>2.5E-1</code>). A minus before a number belongs to it.
<code>0x</code> followed by hexadecimal digits is not a number but a string of bytes:
<code>0x41</code> is the letter A.</p>

<h3>Strings</h3>

<p>Text stands between single or double quotes, which mean the same: a <code>$name</code>
inside double quotes stays text, it is not filled in as PHP would. A backslash starts an
escape - <code>\n</code>, <code>\r</code>, <code>\t</code>, <code>\\</code>,
<code>\'</code> and <code>\"</code>; any other letter after it is an error. The dot joins
strings, and numbers with them:</p>

<h3>Bare words</h3>

<p>A word without quotes and without a sigil is looked up. An operator word -
<code>eq</code>, <code>and</code>, <code>not</code> ... - is the operator; a PHP constant -
<code>TRUE</code>, <code>FALSE</code>, <code>NULL</code>, <code>PHP_EOL</code>,
<code>M_PI</code> - is its value; a word that names nothing stands for its own text.</p>

<h2>Operators</h2>

<p>The operators bind in this order, strongest first. Those of one row bind equally and are
applied left to right - <code>10 - 2 + 3</code> is 11 - except <code>**</code>, which binds
right to left. Round brackets group as usual.</p>

<table border="1" cellpadding="8" cellspacing="0" style="border-collapse: collapse;">
  <tr style="background: #f0f0f0;"><th>operators</th><th>what they do</th></tr>
  <tr><td><code>**</code></td><td>power, right to left: <code>2 ** 3 ** 2</code> is <code>2 ** 9</code></td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>!</code>, a sign <code>-</code> <code>+</code> before a value</td><td>not, and the signs - weaker than <code>**</code>: <code>-3 ** 2</code> is -9</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>*</code> <code>/</code> <code>%</code></td><td>multiply, divide, remainder</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>+</code> <code>-</code></td><td>add, subtract</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>.</code></td><td>join as text</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>lt</code> <code>le</code> <code>gt</code> <code>ge</code> - <code>&lt;</code> <code>&lt;=</code> <code>&gt;</code> <code>&gt;=</code></td><td>order</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>eq</code> <code>ne</code> - <code>==</code> <code>=</code> <code>!=</code> <code>&lt;&gt;</code></td><td>equal, not equal</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>not</code></td><td>denies what follows, a comparison included</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>and</code> - <code>&amp;&amp;</code></td><td>both hold</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>xor</code></td><td>exactly one holds</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>or</code> - <code>||</code></td><td>either holds</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>??</code></td><td>the left side, or the right one when the left is empty or missing</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>? :</code> and <code>?:</code></td><td>the inline ternary, weakest of all</td></tr>
</table>

<p>The tests <code>in</code>, <code>like</code>, <code>matches</code>, <code>range</code>,
<code>between</code> and <code>contains</code> read infix as well; they have
<a href="#tests">a section of their own</a>.</p>

<h3>Arithmetic</h3>

<p>As in PHP: <code>/</code> answers a decimal when it does not divide evenly, <code>%</code>
works on whole numbers. A text that is a number counts as one - <code>'5' + '5'</code> is 10 -
a text that is not, <code>'a' + 1</code>, is an error, and so is a division by zero.</p>

<h3>Joining text</h3>

<p>The dot binds weaker than <code>+</code> and <code>-</code>: in <code>1 + 2 . 3</code> the
sum comes first and is then joined with the 3, and in <code>1 . 5 + 5</code> the 1 is joined
with 10. After a value a dot is the join, also right against it - <code>'a'.1</code> - and
only where no value stands before it does a dot start a number.</p>

<h3>Comparing</h3>

<h3>Logic</h3>

<p><code>and</code>, <code>or</code>, <code>xor</code> and <code>not</code>, with
<code>&amp;&amp;</code>, <code>||</code> and <code>!</code> as their symbols. The word
<code>not</code> binds weaker than the comparisons, so <code>not $a eq $b</code> denies the
comparison; <code>!</code> binds as tightly as a sign, so <code>!$a eq $b</code> compares
<code>!$a</code> with <code>$b</code>. <code>and</code> and <code>or</code> stop at a left
side that decides: <code>$n gt 0 and 10 / $n gt 1</code> never divides by zero.</p>

<h3>A default: ??</h3>

<p><code>a ?? b</code> answers <code>a</code> unless it is empty - <code>''</code>, NULL, FALSE,
an empty list - or a field that does not exist; then it answers <code>b</code>. A 0 is a value
and stays. A field in front of <code>??</code> may be missing, also under the strict check:
giving it a default is what the operator is for. The right side is worked out only when it
is wanted. In a pipe it takes the piped value, and the <code>default</code> function does
the same:</p>

<h3>The inline ternary</h3>

<p><code>cond ? then : else</code> binds weakest of all: everything before the
<code>?</code> is the condition, and each branch runs to its <code>:</code> or to the end -
<code>0 ? 'yes' : 'no' . '!'</code> joins the <code>'!'</code> to the no. Only the branch
chosen is worked out. Ternaries in a row read from the right, <code>a ? b : c ? d : e</code>
being <code>a ? b : (c ? d : e)</code>. The short form <code>a ?: b</code> answers
<code>a</code> itself when it holds, and with nothing before the <code>?</code> the piped
value is the condition:</p>

<h2>Truth</h2>

<p>Where a condition is wanted - <code>{if}</code>, <code>where=</code>, the
ternary, <code>and</code> and <code>or</code> - a value is tested as PHP tests it:
<code>''</code>, <code>'0'</code>, 0, NULL, FALSE and an empty list are false, every other
value is true - the texts <code>'0.0'</code>, <code>'false'</code> and a space among
them.</p>

<h2 id="tests">Tests: in, like, matches, range, between, contains</h2>

<table border="1" cellpadding="8" cellspacing="0" style="border-collapse: collapse;">
  <tr style="background: #f0f0f0;"><th>test</th><th>holds when</th></tr>
  <tr><td><code>$x in (a, b, ...)</code></td><td>the value equals one of the list</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>$x like 'A%'</code></td><td>the value matches the SQL pattern: <code>%</code> any run of characters, <code>_</code> one character, <code>\\%</code> and <code>\\_</code> themselves; case is ignored</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>$x matches '/re/'</code></td><td>the regular expression matches</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>$x range (a, b)</code></td><td>a &lt;= value &lt;= b, the ends included</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>$x between (a, b)</code></td><td>a &lt; value &lt; b, the ends left out</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>$x contains y</code></td><td>the text holds y - or, for a list, an item of it equals y</td></tr>
</table>

<p>A <code>not</code> between the value and the test denies the test, as SQL writes it:
<code>$s not in (...)</code>, <code>not like</code>, <code>not matches</code>,
<code>not range</code>. The value is everything left of the test - <code>5 + 1 in (6,
7)</code> tests 6.</p>

<h2>Functions</h2>

<p>The built-in pipe functions - <code>upper</code>, <code>left</code>, <code>replace</code>,
<code>date</code> and the rest of the <b>{functions}</b> list - are used two ways. After a pipe
they take the value on its left: <code>$s | left(2)</code>. Written as a call with nothing
piped in they take it as their first argument: <code>left($s, 2)</code>, as PHP's own
functions do - so they nest, and stand wherever a value can. The number of parameters is
checked.</p>

<h2>When an expression is wrong</h2>

<p>Under the strict syntax check - <code>$padCheckSyntax</code>, on by default - a mistake in
an expression is an error that names the fault and its place, and the page answers with the
error report instead of guessing. The report shows the template's file, line and column with
the lines around it (see <a href="?pages/template_errors">errors in the template</a>). What
some mistakes are told:</p>

<table border="1" cellpadding="8" cellspacing="0" style="border-collapse: collapse;">
  <tr style="background: #f0f0f0;"><th>written</th><th>the error says</th></tr>
  <tr><td><code>{echo (1 + 2/}</code></td><td>the ( opened at position 1 is never closed</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>{echo 'abc/}</code></td><td>the string opened with ' at position 1 is never closed</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>{echo $name | uppr/}</code></td><td>there is no pipe function named 'uppr'</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>{echo nofunc(1)/}</code></td><td>there is no function named 'nofunc'</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>{if $typo eq 1}</code></td><td>there is no field named '$typo'</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>{echo $items + 1/}</code></td><td>the field '$items' is a list, not a value</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>{if $n eq}</code></td><td>the operator 'eq' has nothing on its right</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>{echo $n + * 3/}</code></td><td>the operators '+' and '*' stand side by side - a value is missing between them</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>{if $n === 5}</code></td><td>'===' is no operator - eq (==) compares two values</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>{echo $n | +1/}</code></td><td>a pipe operator needs a space before its operand - write '| + 1', not '| +1'</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>{echo 5 &amp; 3/}</code></td><td>'&amp;' is no operator or value</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>{if $n not 5}</code></td><td>a 'not' after a value denies a test - write $a not in (...), not like, not matches or not range, or put it in front: not $a eq $b</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>{echo 7 / 0/}</code></td><td>a division by zero in /</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>{echo 'a' * 2/}</code></td><td>'a' is not a number for *</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>{echo $n ? 'a'/}</code></td><td>the ? of an inline ternary has no : - write cond ? then : else</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>{echo [ 'a' =&gt; ]/}</code></td><td>the key 'a' of a list has no value after its =&gt;</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>{echo replace('abc')/}</code></td><td>the function 'replace' wants 2 parameters after the value it works on - written as a call its first argument is that value: replace($value, ...)</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>{echo php:str_repeat('x', -1)/}</code></td><td>the PHP function 'str_repeat' refused its arguments: str_repeat(): Argument #2 ($times) must be greater than or equal to 0</td></tr>
  <tr><td><code>{echo 'a\qb'/}</code></td><td>Unsupported \ char</td></tr>
</table>

<h2>The lenient walk</h2>

<p>With <code>$padCheckSyntax = FALSE</code> in <code>_config/config.php</code> nothing is
reported and the evaluator answers what it can: a text that is no number counts as 0
(<code>'a' + 1</code> is 1), a missing field is empty (<code>$nope + 1</code> is 1), an
unknown escape loses its backslash, and an expression that cannot be worked out at all - a
bracket never closed, a division by zero, a PHP function refusing its arguments - answers
nothing. That suits old templates; a new one is better off with the check, which finds the
typo the lenient walk hides.</p>
