Tartan

A plaid whose sett the seed makes up: three to six stripes of the background and the inks, each so many threads wide, mirrored about the first and the last as most tartans are, woven the same way across and down - so where two stripes cross their colours mix, and a fine twill runs over the cloth at 45 degrees. The size is the sett, the repeat, in pixels. A tile repeated by a <pattern>, the seed choosing the sett.

Seed 1 - another picture with seed 0 or seed 2. 

{pattern:tartan seed=1, width=960, height=320/}

Its own example 

{pattern:tartan size=120, seed=3, colors='#1f3a5f, #2e6b4f, #c0392b, #f1c40f'/}

As a background

as='url' writes the pattern as a CSS url() of a data URI, for a style attribute or a style sheet.

Text over a pattern 

<div style="background-image:{pattern:tartan seed=1, as='url'/}; background-size:cover; padding:48px 24px">
  <p style="margin:0; padding:12px 16px; background:#fffc; color:#222; display:inline-block">Text over a pattern</p>
</div>
