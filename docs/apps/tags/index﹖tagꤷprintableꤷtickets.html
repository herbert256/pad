Tags/Printables/printable:tickets

{printable:tickets}

Tickets with a stub to tear off.

Printables Tag pairs

Syntax 

{printable:tickets data='seats'}...<aside>...</aside>{/printable:tickets}

Description

One of the 14 printables of printable:*, written {printable:tickets data='seats'}...<aside>...</aside>{/printable:tickets}.

{printable:tickets data='seats'}...<aside>...</aside>{/printable:tickets} - tickets with a stub to tear off: the content between the tags is one ticket, its <aside> the stub - a {barcode:qr} of the ticket's code, a seat - and the rest the main part, with a dashed perforation between them; without an <aside> the stub holds the ticket's number, No. 0001 on, from number= on. sheet= a4-1x4 (190 by 65 mm, the default), a4-1x5 (190 by 52) or letter-1x4 (7.75 by 2.5 inches), and the grid options of the labels; padding=, size= (10 pt) and align= the main part's text. The edges are printed, to cut along.

Example

The tickets

As the printables application shows it.

Template 
{fake:rows 'name, code:uuid', rows=4, seed=9, toData='guests'/}
{printable:tickets data='guests', scale=0.45, pdf}
  <h2>The Magic Flute</h2>
  Saturday 14 November 2026, 20:00 - Grand Hall<br>{$name}
  <aside>{barcode:qr $code, size=90/}</aside>
{/printable:tickets}
Result
4 tickets on 1 sheet of A4, 210 × 297 mm
