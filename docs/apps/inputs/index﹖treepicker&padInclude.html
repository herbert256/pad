One item of a hierarchy, a category of a category, a place in a country: nested lists of native radio buttons in a fieldset whose legend is the label, which a form posts without any script, and where the script runs a button showing the path of the choice - Europe › France › Paris - that opens the tree as the WAI-ARIA tree view: the arrow keys up and down the open items, Right opening an item or going to its first child, Left closing it or going to its parent, Home and End, a letter typed going to the next item it starts, * opening every item beside the one with the focus, Enter or Space choosing, Escape closing. It posts the value of the item chosen. The items are the paths of options= - 'Europe > France > Paris, Asia > Japan', each step an item under the one before it, its text its value - or the rows of the store data= names - flat, each naming its parent= field (parent unless given, empty for a root - or 0 when no row is 0), or nested, its children in the field children= - the value of a row its key= field, its id unless given, the text its field=, else its name, label or title; or the lines between the tags, a line indented deeper than the one before it its child, its text its value. A value names one item: a second one, a row under a parent that is none and a cycle of parents are named under the strict check, and left out without it. leaves lets only an item without children be chosen; value= the item chosen before a post, placeholder= what the button says before a choice. After a post it shows the item posted - none when the form came back without one, and a posted value that is no item is not shown: rules='required|in:...' holds it to the items.
