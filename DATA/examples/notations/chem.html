Chemical formulas and equations typed the way chemists type them - the notation of mhchem, without its backslash - and drawn as MathML: the counts of H2SO4 below the line, the charges of Fe^3+ and SO4^2- above it, coefficients, states, hydrates, isotopes and the arrows of a reaction. Every element is checked against the periodic table, so a typing slip is caught.

Written {notation:chem 'CH4 + 2O2 -> CO2 + 2H2O'/}, or between the tags; display='block' sets the equation apart.

Formulas

The count after an element or a bracket goes below the line; the elements are checked against the periodic table.

Ions and charges

A charge after a caret, or a lone + or - at the end, goes above the line, stacked over the count before it.
