Signal flags

Text spelt in the flags of the International Code of Signals, the flags ships hoist: a flag for every letter in the code's colours and patterns, a numeral pennant for every digit. Each flag shows its name in the phonetic alphabet on hover - Alfa, Bravo, Charlie - and what it means hoisted alone: O is man overboard, V I require assistance.

Written {notation:signals 'PAD'/}; size= is the width of a flag.

The flags of the alphabet

A flag for every letter, in the colours and patterns of the code; hover a flag for its name and its meaning hoisted alone.

Template 
{notation:signals 'ABCDEFGHI JKLMNOPQR STUVWXYZ', size=36/}
