tablature.pad 		Result 
{pad:meta title='Tablature'/}
<p class="nt-lead">A tab for a guitar, a bass, a ukulele or any fretted instrument, written the way
players have traded tabs in plain text since the newsgroups - a line a string, the highest on top,
the fret to press on the line of its string, notes in one column played together - and drawn as a
printed tab: the strings with their names, the frets on the lines, the bars across and the
techniques as arcs, slanted lines, arrows and waves. The signs are those of the
<a href="https://www.classtab.org/tabbing.htm">guide to tab notation</a> of the On-line Guitar
Archive: <code>h</code> hammer-on, <code>p</code> pull-off, <code>b</code> bend, <code>r</code>
release, <code>/</code> and <code>\</code> slides, <code>v</code> or <code>~</code> vibrato,
<code>t</code> tap, <code>x</code> a muted string, <code><12></code> a harmonic and
<code>(8)</code> a ghost note. A screen reader hears every note bar by bar with its string, its
fret and its pitch.</p>

<p class="nt-tag">Written <code>{notation:tablature}</code> with the tab between the tags. A line of
text over or under the strings - chord names, the beats, the note lengths - stays in its columns.
<code>tuning=</code> names a tuning - <code>dropd</code>, <code>dadgad</code>, <code>openg</code>,
<code>opend</code>, <code>seven</code>, <code>bass</code>, <code>bass5</code>, <code>ukulele</code>,
<code>mandolin</code>, <code>banjo</code> - or gives the notes from the lowest string,
<code>'D A D G B E'</code>; without it the names before the lines say it, else the usual tuning of
so many strings.</p>

<?php

  // A tab just under the 20 000-byte budget with a bar line after every note: 9 900 bars,
  // said in a fraction of a second - where counting the bar lines again for every note took
  // nearly ten. The template keeps only the end of the description.

  $tab = 'e|' . str_repeat ( '0|', 9900 ) . '-';

  $start = hrtime ( TRUE );

?>
