Tags/Toys/toy:galton

{toy:galton}

Galton's board: balls drop through rows of pegs, bouncing left or right at each one by chance.

Toys Either form

Syntax 

{toy:galton/}
{galton/}

Description

One of the 19 toys of {pad:toy}, written {toy:galton/}, {toy 'galton'/}, {galton ...}, bare, when no tag, field or store has the name.

Galton's board: balls drop through rows of pegs, bouncing left or right at each one by chance, and pile up in the bins below into the bell of the normal distribution - drawn over them for the count dropped. Drop one ball, a hundred, or let them pour; tilt the board to make every bounce lean one way. levels= the rows of pegs (12 unless given, 4 to 20); balls= how many the board pours before it stops (500, 10 to 5000); bias= the chance in percent that a ball goes right (50, 5 to 95).
