A play is a sequence, but not used to build a list, it is used to exucute on a sequence list.

<p>There are 4 play operators

<table border=1>


  <tr>
    <td> make </td>
    <td> Make all entries of a sequence list that sequence     </td>
  </tr>

  <tr>
    <td> keep </td>
    <td> Keep sequence list entries that are that sequence     </td>
  </tr>

  <tr>
    <td> remove </td>
    <td> Remove sequence list entries that are that sequence     </td>
  </tr>

  <tr>
    <td> flag </td>
    <td> Flag each sequence list entry with 1 when it is that sequence, else 0     </td>
  </tr>


</table>
