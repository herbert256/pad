Flow field

Thousands of particles drift on a field of currents made from noise, each leaving a fine trail, and slowly paint the shape of the flow - silk, smoke, marbled paper. The pointer stirs a whirl into the current, a click calls up a new field, and the field itself drifts as time goes by. particles= how many (1500 unless given, 100 to 6000); scale= how wide the currents are (4, 1 to 10); speed= how fast the particles go (3, 1 to 10); fade= how soon the trails fade (3, 0 - never - to 10).
