  <h2>Choosing the beans</h2>
  <p>Buy whole beans roasted in the last few weeks.</p>
  <h3>Light or dark roast</h3>
  <p>A light roast keeps the fruit; a dark roast trades it for body.</p>
  <h2>Grinding</h2>
  <p>Grind just before brewing, coarse for a press and fine for espresso.</p>
  <h2>Brewing</h2>
  <h3>Pour-over</h3>
  <p>Bloom the grounds for thirty seconds, then pour slowly.</p>
  <h3>French press</h3>
  <p>Steep four minutes and press gently.</p>
