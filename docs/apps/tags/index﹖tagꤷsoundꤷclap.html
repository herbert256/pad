Tags/Sounds/sound:clap

{sound:clap}

A hand clap as a drum machine makes it.

Sounds Single tags

Syntax 

{sound:clap/}

Description

One of the 30 sounds of sound:*, written {sound:clap/}.

A hand clap as a drum machine makes it: three bursts of noise a hundredth of a second apart, the hands not meeting all at once, then the short ring of the room.

Example

The clap sound

A button that plays it, and its WAV file.

Template 
{sound:clap download/}
Result
clap.wav
