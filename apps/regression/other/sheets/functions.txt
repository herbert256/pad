It rounds the number as it shows, to its 15 digits: MROUND(1.3, 0.2) is 1.4.</td>
      <td><ul class="sheets-examples"><li><code>=MROUND(10, 3)</code> <samp>9</samp></li><li><code>=MROUND(-10, -3)</code> <samp>-9</samp></li><li><code>=MROUND(1.3, 0.2)</code> <samp>1.4</samp></li><li><code>=MROUND(5, -2)</code> <samp>#NUM!</samp></li></ul></td>
