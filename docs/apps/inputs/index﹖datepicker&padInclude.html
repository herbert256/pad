A day typed or chosen from a month that opens beside the field: a native <input type="date">, which posts the day as 2026-10-31 and can be typed into, with a button beside it - where the script runs - that opens a month to choose from. The month is a grid the arrow keys walk, a day a step and a week a row; Page Up and Page Down go a month, with Shift a year; Home and End to the week's ends; Enter takes the day, Escape closes it and gives the focus back to the field. min= and max= - days written 2026-01-31 - hold both the field and the month to them; weekstart= 'sunday' begins the week on Sunday, Monday unless given.
