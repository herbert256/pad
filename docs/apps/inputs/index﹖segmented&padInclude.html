One of a few choices shown side by side as one bar, the chosen one filled: a native radio group in a fieldset whose legend is the label, drawn as segments by its style alone, so it needs no script - Tab comes to the group once, the arrow keys move along it and choose, as in any radio group. The choices are options=, a list, or the rows of the store data= names - field= the field of a row they are read from, its first unless given - or the lines between the tags, as a combobox's; value= the choice made before a post. After a post it shows the choice posted - none when the form came back without one, and a posted value that is no choice is not shown: rules='required|in:...' holds the field to the list. The first choice has the field's id, the others the id with their place, size-2, as the boxes of {input:field}'s group have.
