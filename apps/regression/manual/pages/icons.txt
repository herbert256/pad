<h2>An icon is a file</h2>

<p>Every icon is a file of <code>pad/subs/icons/types/</code>, <code>&lt;name&gt;.svg</code> - a whole SVG
that a browser shows as it is - and the files there are the icons there are. The icon type writes one
by its name, <code>{icon:cloud-upload/}</code>; the names are never used alone, as they
are the names of fields and pages too. <code>size=</code> and <code>stroke=</code> are the size in
pixels and the width of the lines.</p>
