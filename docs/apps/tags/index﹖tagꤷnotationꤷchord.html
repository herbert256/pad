Tags/Notations/notation:chord

{notation:chord}

A chord of a guitar or a ukulele as the box of a chord chart.

Notations Either form

Syntax 

{notation:chord 'text'/}

Description

One of the 20 notations of notation:*, written {notation:chord 'text'/}.

A chord of a guitar or a ukulele as the box of a chord chart: its name above, a dot per finger, a bar where one finger lies across the strings, o and × over the strings played open and not played, the note of every string below. The text is a chord name - C, Am, F#m7, Bbmaj7, Dsus4, G7/B, Cdim7 ... - drawn in the shape a player learns first, or a shape found for it when there is none; or the frets themselves, 'x32010', '8 10 10 9 8 8' past the ninth fret, the chord then named by its notes. frets= draws a name in a shape of your own, instrument= is guitar (the default) or ukulele, size= the width in pixels (120 unless given), title= the accessible name. Bare, {chart:chord} is the chord diagram of the charts, which comes first: this is {notation:chord}.

From the tags application

Its example in the notation collection.

{notation:chord 'Am'/} {notation:chord 'F'/} {notation:chord 'Bbmaj7'/}
{notation:chord 'G7', instrument='ukulele'/} {notation:chord 'x32010'/}
