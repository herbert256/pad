Tags/Pictures and media/embed:*

{embed:*}

Embeds a video, a sound, a map, a pen or a document of another site behind a click-to-load facade - 19 providers, nothing of theirs loaded before the click.

Pictures and media Single tags

Syntax 

{embed:auto 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=id', title='text'/}
{embed:vimeo 'id', title='text', at='1:30', ratio='4:3'/}
{embed:openstreetmap 'latitude,longitude', zoom=15, title='text'/}
{embed:auto 'https://codepen.io/user/pen/id', title='text', tab='html,result'/}
{embed:audio 'sounds/bell.mp3', title='text'/}

Parameters

address
An address one of the providers knows, a file of pad/subs/embeds/types/: applemusic, applepodcasts, audio, codepen, dailymotion, figma, internetarchive, loom, openstreetmap, pdffile, peertube, soundcloud, spotify, stackblitz, tiktok, twitch, videofile, vimeo, youtube. The embed type names one - {embed:youtube 'id'/} - and then a bare id of that provider is read as well.

Options

title
Required: the button's text, the frame's title, what a screen reader says.
poster
A picture of www/<application>/ (versioned as {pad:asset}) in place of the drawn poster.
thumbnail
Bare option: the provider's own picture as the poster, where it has one at a known address (YouTube) - an image asked of the provider before the click.
ratio
The proportion, 16:9 unless the provider has another (SoundCloud and Spotify 2:1, a PDF 4:5, a short and a TikTok 9:16, Apple Music and Apple Podcasts 4:3 - a song or an episode 5:2).
at
YouTube, Vimeo, PeerTube, Dailymotion, Loom, a video of Twitch: the moment to start at - 90, 1m30s or 1:30.
lat
OpenStreetMap: the latitude of the place, beside lon - or the place as the first parameter, '52.37,4.89'.
lon
OpenStreetMap: the longitude of the place.
zoom
OpenStreetMap: 1 (the world) to 19 (a street), default 13.
theme
Figma: the viewer's theme - light (Figma's default), dark or system, the visitor's.
file
StackBlitz: the file open in the editor - a ?file= in the address says it too.
view
StackBlitz: what is shown - editor, preview or both (default).
domains
Twitch: host names beside the page's own its player may play under - a page shown in a frame of another site.
tab
CodePen: the panes shown - result (default), html, css, js, or two of them.

Description

{embed:auto} puts a video, a sound, a map, a pen or a document of another site in the page without the page asking that site for anything before the visitor does. What is written first is a facade: a poster drawn for the kind of thing it is - or a picture of the application's own with poster= - the title, the name of the site it comes from and a button. Pressing the button puts the provider's frame in its place, by a small script written once per page with the CSP nonce, and moves the focus into it. Without script the button is hidden and a link to the address on its own site stands in its place.

A provider is a file of pad/subs/embeds/types/ - the files there are the providers there are, and a provider is added by adding its file. It says the addresses it knows, the site its frame comes from and what the frame is given. {embed:auto 'address'} asks each of them, the ones that know their host first; the embed type names one - {embed:vimeo '76979871'} - and then reads a bare id of that provider as well. The type prefix is required. A file of the application - a video, a sound, a PDF - is an element of its own that loads nothing before it is played, or a frame of the application loaded lazily; nothing about it is another site's.

The frames need the providers' origins in a Content Security Policy's frame-src: padEmbedSources() lists the ones the files name - https://www.youtube-nocookie.com https://player.vimeo.com ... - and a PeerTube instance is added by hand. thumbnail shows YouTube's own picture as the poster, which asks Google's image server for it before the click, and needs img-src https://i.ytimg.com. For the video sites alone, with the poster of the site, {widget:video} is the older form.

A frame is given what the provider's own embed code gives it: the permissions of its allow and, where that code has one, its sandbox - Apple Music and Apple Podcasts are framed with the six tokens of Apple's, so their frame may do only what Apple's player needs.

Twitch plays only on the host names its player is told: those of $padHosts, else the page's own, and domains= adds more. Offline and in a static copy, where the page could be anywhere and $padHosts does not say, the poster links to Twitch instead of holding a player that would refuse to play.

Examples

The provider from the address

A YouTube address: a drawn poster, the title and a button - the player comes from youtube-nocookie.com only after the click.

Templateexamples/embed/1-address.pad 
<div style="max-width:480px">
  {embed:auto 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aqz-KE-bpKQ', title='Big Buck Bunny - Blender Foundation'/}
</div>
Resultopen alone

A map of a place

The openstreetmap provider named bare, the place as latitude and longitude - no tile is asked before the click.

Templateexamples/embed/2-map.pad 
<div style="max-width:480px">
  {embed:openstreetmap '52.3731,4.8922', zoom=15, title='Dam Square, Amsterdam'/}
</div>
Resultopen alone

A pen with its code

A CodePen address with tab= - the HTML beside the result once it is loaded.

Templateexamples/embed/3-pen.pad 
<div style="max-width:480px">
  {embed:auto 'https://codepen.io/team/codepen/pen/PNaGbb', title='A pen of CodePen', tab='html,result'/}
</div>

Sound, a PDF, PeerTube and Spotify

Four more providers, each known by its address - a sound file and a PDF of another site, a PeerTube video and a Spotify track: nothing of theirs is loaded before a click.

Templateexamples/embed/4-more.pad 
<div style="max-width:480px; display:grid; gap:16px">
  {embed:auto 'https://example.org/sounds/bell.mp3', title='A bell'/}
  {embed:auto 'https://www.example.org/docs/plan.pdf', title='The floor plan'/}
  {embed:auto 'https://framatube.org/w/kkGMgK9ZtnKfYAgnEtQxbv', title='What is PeerTube?'/}
  {embed:auto 'https://open.spotify.com/intl-nl/track/4uLU6hMCjMI75M1A2tKUQC', title='A track on Spotify'/}
</div>

See also

  • {widget:video} Shows a YouTube or Vimeo video as its poster with a play button, loading the player only when pressed - or a video file as a video element.
  • {pad:map} Draws a choropleth map as inline SVG - every country, or every state or province of a country, coloured by a value of its row - of the world or one of its 26 regions.
  • {pad:img} Writes a picture of the application's www directory as a thumbnail of the size the page shows it at, resized once with GD.
