A tune written in ABC notation - the plain-text music notation folk musicians have traded tunes in since the eighties - drawn as a staff in SVG: a treble clef, the key and time signatures, the notes with their stems, beams and ledger lines, accidentals, rests, bar lines and repeats, chord names over the notes. Lines of music in the text are lines of staff in the drawing.

Written {notation:abc} with the tune between the tags, its header first - T: the title, M: the meter, L: the unit note length, K: the key. width= spreads the lines over that many pixels.

A scale

C is middle C, c the octave above, a comma lowers a note an octave and an apostrophe raises it; the notes under the staff and over it get their ledger lines.

Template
{notation:abc}
  T:The C major scale and beyond
  M:4/4
  L:1/4
  K:C
  C D E F|G A B c|d e f g|a b c' z|
  A,, C, E, G,|c4|]
{/notation:abc}
Result

A reel with its chords

Eighth notes written together are beamed; a repeat closes each part; chord names in double quotes stand over their notes.

Template
{notation:abc}
  X:1
  T:Speed the Plough
  C:Trad.
  M:4/4
  L:1/8
  Q:1/4=110
  K:G
  |:"G"GABc dedB|"G"dedB dedB|"C"c2ec "G"B2dB|"Am"c2A2 "D"A2BA|
  "G"GABc dedB|"G"dedB dedB|"C"c2ec "G"B2dB|"D"A2F2 "G"G4:|
{/notation:abc}

Rhythms, rests and keys

Dotted notes and broken rhythm, a triplet, a chord of three notes, sharps and naturals before the notes against the flats of the key, a tie, rests, and first and second endings.

Template
{notation:abc}
  T:Rhythms
  M:3/4
  L:1/8
  K:Bb
  B>c d2 f2|(3def g2 z2|[DFB]6|e2 ^e2 =e2|=e4- e2|
  B4 A2|1 G6:|2 G4 z2|]
{/notation:abc}
