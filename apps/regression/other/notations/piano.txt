<p class="nt-lead">Notes on a piano keyboard, the keys pressed marked and named: the notes written
out with their octaves - middle C is C4 - or one chord name, voiced upwards from its root. The
keyboard runs from the C under the lowest note to the B over the highest, two octaves at least,
and every C is named so the octave is found at a glance.</p>

<p class="nt-tag">Written <code>{piano 'C4 E4 G4'/}</code> or <code>{piano 'Dm7'/}</code>;
<code>from=</code> and <code>to=</code> set the ends of the keyboard.</p>
