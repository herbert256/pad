<p><code>{parent}</code> stands inside a <a href="?tag/block"><code>{block}</code></a> of a
page that overrides a block of its layout or wrapper. It is replaced by the content of the
block being overridden, so the page can add to a region - before it, after it, or around it
- instead of replacing it entirely.</p>

<p>It is resolved in the text while the page is assembled, together with the blocks and
<a href="?tag/extends"><code>{extends}</code></a>, and is gone before the page renders.
Through a chain of layouts, each <code>{parent}</code> is the next block down the chain.</p>

<p>A <code>{parent}</code> anywhere else - outside a block, or in a block that overrides
nothing - reaches the render, and is an error under the strict check.</p>
