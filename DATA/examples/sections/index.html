What is here

Four subsystems, one look

They share the frame's palette, so a theme colours them all at once.

  • Sections

    A hero, the features, the prices, the questions, a footer - no script at all

    , laid out by container queries.

  • Effects

    A typewriter, a marker, a count-up, a reveal on scroll, loaders and confetti - still and whole under reduced motion.

  • Themes

    Paper, terminal, newspaper, ocean, a high-contrast one - for a page, an application or the visitor's own choice.

  • Palettes

    Viridis and its family, Okabe-Ito and Tol for every kind of colour vision, ColorBrewer's scales.

How it works

A landing page in three tags

  1. Pick the blocks

    {section:hero}, {section:pricing}, {section:faq} - in the order the page tells its story.

  2. Fill them

    Options for the words, {pad:slot} pairs for markup, data= or {section:item} for the lists.

  3. Choose the colours

    $padTheme = 'paper' - or let the visitor pick one in the bar above.

What builders say

Questions

Do the sections need JavaScript?

No. They are HTML and CSS - the questions here are <details>, the forms post back to the page.

What happens under reduced motion?

Every effect shows its content still and whole: typed out, counted, revealed. Confetti does not burst.

Where does my theme choice go?

Into a cookie of this application: every page of it is in that theme until you choose Default - another application is not.

Can a chart use a palette?

Any kind of chart: palette='viridis' - drawn on the server or in the browser, the same.
