A first and a last day chosen as one, a stay or a leave: two native <input type="date">, one for each end, under a legend - two fields of the form, {input:daterange 'stay'} posting stay_from and stay_to as 2026-06-01, names= two others - typed into as they are, and where the script runs a button that opens one month to choose both from, the WAI-ARIA date picker dialog: the arrow keys walk the days, Page Up and Page Down the months (with Shift the years), Home and End the week; Enter or a click takes the first day, then the last, the days between marked as the pointer or the focus moves - a day before the first starts again from there - and Escape closes it. Both days are the range's own: the last is in it. nights counts the nights between them - a hotel's arrival and departure, the last after the first - where the days are counted unless given, the count shown beside the fields. min= and max= - days written 2026-01-31 - hold both fields and the month to them, weekstart= 'sunday' begins the week on Sunday; labels= what each end says, 'From, To' unless given; value= the two before a post. A range is two fields and has no rules= - the page's PHP checks the two with padValidate, after:stay_from on the last for nights.
