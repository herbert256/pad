Tags/Patterns/pattern:guilloche

{pattern:guilloche}

The engraved lines of a certificate or a banknote.

Patterns Single tags

Syntax 

{pattern:guilloche seed=7/}

Description

One of the 29 patterns of pattern:*, written {pattern:guilloche seed=7/}.

The engraved lines of a certificate or a banknote: bands the size high, each woven of lines= copies of one wave (12), the copies set a step apart along it and every second one turned upside down, so they cross and interlace into lenses and braids. The wave is two sines, the second two or three times as fast; their lengths, weights and phase are the seed's. The inks take turns line by line. Decoration only - no security printing.

The wave reaches 0.6 of the size either way, so the bands beside each other interlace too. It is drawn once, a polyline from a wavelength left of the picture to its right edge, its points a twelfth of the faster sine's wavelength apart and 3 pixels at most; a band is that wave used again - a <use> - a line at a time, the upside-down ones mirrored about the band's middle, and every band a <use> of the first: the SVG grows with the width, not with the lines or the bands.
