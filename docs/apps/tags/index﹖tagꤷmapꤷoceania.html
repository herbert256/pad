Tags/Maps/map:oceania

{map:oceania}

Australia, New Zealand, New Guinea and the islands of the western Pacific, Palau, Guam.

Maps Either form

Syntax 

{map:oceania width=540/}

Description

One of the 31 maps of map:*, written {map:oceania width=540/}.

Australia, New Zealand, New Guinea and the islands of the western Pacific, Palau, Guam, Micronesia and the Marshall Islands north of the equator with them. The islands across the 180th meridian - Samoa and Tonga - are not in it: a value of theirs is named in the title.

Example

A map view

Add data= with country values, or mode=markers/bubbles with latitude and longitude fields.

Template 
{map:oceania width=540/}
