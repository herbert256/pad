Tags/Variables and stores/holidays:*

{holidays:*}

The public holidays of a country - twenty-eight of them, each a file of rules - as rows or a table, its regions too.

Variables and stores Either form

Syntax 

{pad:holidays 'nl', year=2026}{$date} {$name} {$local}{/pad:holidays}
{pad:holidays 'de-by', year=2026/}
{pad:holidays 'gb', region='sct', from='today', to='+6 months', lang='en'}...{/pad:holidays}

Parameters

country
The ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code of the country - at, be, br, ca, ch, cz, de, dk, es, fi, fr, gb, hu, ie, it, jp, lu, mx, nl, no, nz, pl, pt, ro, se, sk, us, za - with a region after it as ISO 3166-2 writes it: de-by.

Options

region
The region apart - region='by': Germany's states, the United Kingdom's nations (sct, nir), France's Alsace-Moselle and overseas departments, Finland's Åland (01), Portugal's Azores and Madeira (20, 30), New Zealand's provinces (auk, can ...).
year
The year, 1583 to 9999 - this year by default.
from
With to: the days between two dates instead of a year - today, 2026-03-01, +3 months.
to
The last date of from; one of the two alone is a year from it (or from today).
lang
The language of local for a country of more than one: be nl fr de, lu lb fr de, ch de fr it, ca en fr, fi fi sv.

Description

{pad:holidays 'nl', year=2026}...{/pad:holidays} hands over the public holidays of the Netherlands in 2026 as rows, sorted by date: $date, $name in English, $local in the country's own language, $type - public for a day off, observance for a national day that is none - $region for a regional holiday and $substitute for the day that makes up for a holiday on a weekend. As a single tag it writes them as a table.

No service is asked and no table of dates kept: every country is a file of rules in pad/subs/holidays/types/ - a fixed date, days from Easter Sunday by the Gregorian computus, the nth or last weekday of a month, the years a holiday is held or moved, the move off a Sunday of King's Day, the substitute days of the United Kingdom (the next free weekday) and the United States (the Friday before, the Monday after). A country is added by adding its file.

The rules reach further: days from the Orthodox Easter (Romania), a Sunday made up for on the Monday after (South Africa) or on the next free day, and a day between two holidays that is one too (Japan), the following Monday for New Zealand's Anzac Day. A file may name the years it knows - New Zealand's Matariki dates are fixed by law to 2052 - and a year outside them is a strict error rather than a list with a holiday missing.

A region follows its country, de-by, or stands in region=. The handling options work on the rows - where='$type eq "public"' leaves the observances out. holidays= on {pad:calendar} marks them in a month, and padHolidays, padIsHoliday, padWorkdays and padAddWorkdays answer a page's PHP. A country without a file, a region it has not, a year out of range or a lang it has no names in is a strict error.

Holiday calendars

Each kind has its own description and usage example:

Examples

The holidays of a year as rows

Every row has its date, the English and the local name, and whether it is a day off.

Templateexamples/holidays/1-rows.pad 
<ul>
  {pad:holidays 'nl', year=2026}
    <li>{$date} {$name} - <i>{$local}</i>{pad:if $type eq 'observance'} (no day off){/pad:if}</li>
  {/pad:holidays}
</ul>

  • 2026-01-01 New Year's Day - Nieuwjaarsdag
  • 2026-04-03 Good Friday - Goede Vrijdag (no day off)
  • 2026-04-05 Easter Sunday - Eerste Paasdag
  • 2026-04-06 Easter Monday - Tweede Paasdag
  • 2026-04-27 King's Day - Koningsdag
  • 2026-05-05 Liberation Day - Bevrijdingsdag (no day off)
  • 2026-05-14 Ascension Day - Hemelvaartsdag
  • 2026-05-24 Whit Sunday - Eerste Pinksterdag
  • 2026-05-25 Whit Monday - Tweede Pinksterdag
  • 2026-12-25 Christmas Day - Eerste Kerstdag
  • 2026-12-26 Second Day of Christmas - Tweede Kerstdag

Substitute days

A bank holiday on a weekend gets the next free weekday; from= and to= ask for the days between two dates.

Templateexamples/holidays/3-substitute.pad 
<ul>
  {pad:holidays 'gb', from='2021-12-20', to='2022-01-10', lang='en'}
    <li>{$date} {$name}{pad:if $substitute} - made up for{/pad:if}</li>
  {/pad:holidays}
</ul>
Resultopen alone
  • 2021-12-25 Christmas Day
  • 2021-12-26 Boxing Day
  • 2021-12-27 Christmas Day (substitute day) - made up for
  • 2021-12-28 Boxing Day (substitute day) - made up for
  • 2022-01-01 New Year's Day
  • 2022-01-03 New Year's Day (substitute day) - made up for

Marked in a calendar

A substitute day and a day between

Japan makes up for a holiday on a Sunday on the next free day and holds a day between two holidays as one too.

Templateexamples/holidays/5-days-between.pad 
<ul>
  {pad:holidays 'jp', from='2026-05-01', to='2026-09-30'}
    <li>{$date} {$name} - <i>{$local}</i></li>
  {/pad:holidays}
</ul>
