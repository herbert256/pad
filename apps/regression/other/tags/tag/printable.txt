<p><code>{printable}</code> makes pages for paper - sheets of labels, name badges, business cards,
tickets with a stub, a certificate, envelopes, a letter and an invoice for a window envelope, index
cards and folded place cards - and paper to write and draw on, graph paper. Every kind is a file of <code>pad/subs/printables/types/</code>; the
files of <code>types/</code> are the kinds there are.</p>

<p>The content between the tags is one item - a label, a badge, a letter - rendered once per row of
the data, with the handling options of any tag, and laid out by the kind on its sheets. A ticket's
stub is its <code>&lt;aside&gt;</code>; a letter's address its <code>&lt;address&gt;</code>, its
information block its <code>&lt;aside&gt;</code>, its letterhead and foot its
<code>&lt;header&gt;</code> and <code>&lt;footer&gt;</code>.</p>

<p>On the screen the sheets lie on a grey desk at their size in millimetres. Printed, a page with
printables prints the sheets alone, each on a page of its own size. In a <code>{pdf}</code>, or with
<code>pdf</code> on the tag, the PDF writer lays out the same sheets - the boxes, rectangles and lines
where the screen has them, the barcodes and QR codes drawn. Every item is one region of a sheet:
what does not fit is cut at its edge.</p>
