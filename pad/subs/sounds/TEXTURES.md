# Short texture recipes

`{sound:pluck/}` combines a decaying 220 Hz fundamental with two harmonics and a quiet noise attack (0.45 s, peak 0.45). `{sound:brush/}` sweeps seeded noise through a band-pass filter (0.32 s, peak 0.30). Both use the existing deterministic synthesis engine and user-gesture playback gate. `download` supplies the same samples as a WAV file. The independent visual-additions test compares every 16-bit sample against the browser renderer, including seeded noise.
