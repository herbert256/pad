Tags/Pictures and media/pattern:*

{pattern:*}

Draws a seeded SVG background or a piece of generative art on the server - 29 kinds, from stripes and dots to contour lines and circle packing - the same bytes for the same options.

Pictures and media Single tags

Syntax 

{pattern:name/}
{pattern:topography seed=7, width=960, height=320/}
{pattern:dots size=24, colors='#fdfbf7, #e0c9a6'/}
{pattern:voronoi seed=3, title='Shards of colour'/}
<div style="background-image:{pattern:waves as='url'/}">...</div>

Parameters

kind after colon
The pattern, a file of pad/subs/patterns/types/: basketweave, blobs, brick, checks, chevron, circles, constellation, crosshatch, dots, flowfield, grain, grid, guilloche, herringbone, hexagons, houndstooth, isometric, maze, mesh, scales, squircle, stripes, tartan, terrazzo, topography, triangles, truchet, voronoi, waves. The pattern: prefix selects it - {pattern:topography/}; a pattern is never reached bare.

Options

seed
A number or a word: what the pattern picks at random, the same for the same seed (1 when left out). A tile repeated - stripes, dots, grid, checks, chevron, isometric, herringbone, scales, houndstooth, a brick wall of one ink - does not change with it.
colors
A list of colours, the background first and the inks after it: colors='#fff, #1d3557, #e63946' - hex, a name, or rgb(), hsl() and their like; none leaves the background out. Each kind has its own palette.
size
The size of the motif in pixels - a stripe, a dot's tile, a hexagon's radius, a hill of the topography, the largest circle; each kind its own default and bounds.
width
The width of the picture in pixels (480); the SVG covers its box when it is made larger or smaller, preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMid slice".
height
The height in pixels (240).
opacity
From 0 to 1: how strongly the motif shows over the background (1; grain 0.5).
as
svg, the default, writes the SVG; url writes url(data:image/svg+xml,...) for a style attribute - in either quotes - or a style sheet.
title
The accessible name: the pattern becomes role="img" with aria-label and <title>. Without it the pattern is decoration, aria-hidden.
angle
stripes and herringbone: the angle in degrees (45).
levels
topography: the number of contour lines (12), every fifth the bolder index line.
ratio
herringbone: a block's length to its width, a whole number (3); brick: a brick's length to its height (3).
offset
brick: how far a course is shifted along the one below, in bricks - 0.5 the running bond, 0 stacked, a third or a quarter raking; taken in twelfths.
joint
brick: the width of the mortar between the bricks in pixels (2), at most nearly half a brick's height.
rings
scales: the circles in a scale (3) - 1 a plain fish scale, more the waves of seigaiha.
lines
guilloche: the lines of a band (12), copies of one wave a step apart along it, every second upside down.

Description

{pattern:dots} draws a background or a piece of generative art as SVG on the server: regular tiles - stripes, dots, a grid, checks, chevrons, isometric cubes, herringbone parquet, brick walls, fish scales, houndstooth - and pictures the seed makes up - contour lines of an imagined landscape traced by marching squares, Truchet tiles, a Voronoi diagram, packed circles, hexagons and triangles in the seed's colours, blobs, film grain and a soft mesh gradient - the sett of a tartan, the engraved lines of guilloche the streamlines of a made-up current, a maze, the chips of terrazzo and a constellation of stars. Every pattern is a file of pad/subs/patterns/types/; the files there are the patterns there are.

The same options give the same bytes on every request and every machine: the random numbers come from PAD's own seeded sequence (the one of padFake, put back afterwards) and every number is written to a fixed precision. Without seed= the seed is 1, so a pattern never changes unless it is told to.

A pattern is decoration: aria-hidden, unless title= names it. With as='url' it is a CSS url() of a data URI, its quotes and the characters a URL or HTML would read escaped, for a style attribute - a background that repeats at the width and height given. In a page's PHP, padPattern ( 'topography', [ 'seed' => 7 ] ) draws the same.

An unknown pattern, a colour that is none, a number outside its bounds and an option the kind does not take are errors under the strict check; the bare name of a pattern is no tag, as its names - dots, grid, waves - are those of fields and pages.

Examples

Contour lines

A landscape the seed makes up, traced at sixteen heights - another seed, another landscape.

Templateexamples/pattern/1-topography.pad 
{pattern:topography seed=7, levels=16, width=640, height=200/}
Resultopen alone

Tiles in colours of your own

The background first, then the inks; size= is the motif.

Templateexamples/pattern/2-tiles.pad 
<p style="display:flex; gap:12px; flex-wrap:wrap">
  {pattern:stripes size=12, angle=-30, colors='#fff, #e9c46a, #2a9d8f', width=200, height=120/}
  {pattern:dots size=24, colors='#fdfbf7, #e0c9a6, #b08968', width=200, height=120/}
  {pattern:isometric size=18, colors='#000, #f4e9d8, #d4a373, #6b4f3a', width=200, height=120/}
</p>
Resultopen alone

The pattern type, named

pattern: names the kind itself; title= makes the picture an image with that name.

Templateexamples/pattern/3-type.pad 
{pattern:voronoi seed=3, size=60, width=640, height=160, title='Shards of colour'/}
Resultopen alone

A background

as='url' writes a CSS url() for a style attribute - the circles behind the text, covering the box.

Templateexamples/pattern/4-background.pad 
<div style="background-image:{pattern:circles seed=4, size=30, width=640, height=160, as='url'/}; background-size:cover; padding:48px 24px; border-radius:8px">
  <p style="margin:0; padding:10px 14px; background:#fffd; color:#222; display:inline-block">Text over a pattern</p>
</div>
