Tags/Patterns/pattern:flowfield

{pattern:flowfield}

Streamlines of a current the seed makes up.

Patterns Single tags

Syntax 

{pattern:flowfield seed=7/}

Description

One of the 26 patterns of pattern:*, written {pattern:flowfield seed=7/}.

Streamlines of a current the seed makes up: lines that follow the flow, the size apart and never closer than half of it, so the picture is evenly full of them, its swirls some twenty sizes across. Each line is an ink the seed picks.

The flow's direction at a place is a sum of three sine waves, each running a direction, at a length and a phase of the seed's, taken as an angle around one main direction. The lines are laid as Jobard and Lefer lay evenly spaced streamlines: a line is traced both ways from its start, a third of the size a step (the midpoint method), until it leaves the picture, comes within half the size of another line - or of itself further back - or has gone two hundred steps; the next starts are tried the size away on either side of every second point of the lines laid, and when they run out at a square of the size not yet reached, in an order the seed shuffles - a start taken only where no line passes within the size. A line of fewer than six points is dropped. The points laid are kept in a grid of squares half the size, so a test looks at the squares around it alone; a line is written by every second point, each from the one before.

A flowfield pattern

Template
