Agenda

The programme of a conference or a meeting: the sessions by their time, those at the same time side by side, each with its title, its time, its speaker, its track and a line about it; the days under a heading each when it has more than one. The sessions are the rows of data= - their fields title, from, to, day, track, speaker, text, link and pause (a break - coffee, lunch - drawn quieter) - or {section:item} pairs or single tags, the item's first value the title and its content the text. A time is written 09:30, on the clock of 24 hours, or with its day as 2026-11-05T09:30, an offset after it when given; no other way, so every time is read the same. The sessions are put in order by their day, their time, then their track - the tracks in the order tracks= gives them, else as they first come - and as written; a day written 2026-11-05 orders the days by the calendar, any other day stands where it first comes. timezone= says what clock the times are on - CET, UTC+1, Europe/Amsterdam - under the heading; tracks= names every track, and a session in a track it does not name is reported. A session without a time, a time written otherwise, an end before its start, and a session without a day where the others have one are reported too.

Blog

The latest articles, a card each: a picture over it, its title as the link to it - the whole card a target for the pointer, one link for the keyboard and a screen reader - its date, its tag, a few lines from it and who wrote it. The articles are the rows of data= - their fields title, link, date, author, tag, image, alt and text (the lines from it) - or {section:item} pairs or single tags, the item's first value the title and its content the lines. A date written 2026-10-30 is a <time> a machine reads too, any other date stands as it is. The picture decorates - the title says what the card is - so its alt text is empty unless alt= gives one. In the page the title comes first, then the date, the lines and the author, the picture drawn above them all. columns= as for features (3).

Changelog

The releases, in the order they are written - newest first, as a changelog keeps them; never sorted by their version, whose order a sort cannot know - each with its version, its date and its changes in the groups of Keep a Changelog: Added, Changed, Deprecated, Removed, Fixed and Security, each a list. The releases are the rows of data= - their fields version, date, title, text, the six groups (a list, or a text with a line or a ; between them), link and badge - or {section:item} pairs or single tags, the item's first value the version and its content the text. A release's date is written 2026-10-30, as Keep a Changelog writes it; its title is a name beside the version, link= where the version leads (a release page, a comparison), badge= a word beside it - Latest, Yanked. Every release is an anchor named by its version alone, so a link to it stays when releases are added: #v2.1.0, #Unreleased - the section's id= before it when given; a version given twice is reported.

Comparison

The plans or products side by side in a table, a row per feature and a column per plan, each cell what the plan has of it: a value as it is written, a check for yes, a dash for no and a question mark for a value nobody gave - each read out as its word (included=, excluded=, unknown=; Included, Not included, Unknown), so a missing value is never a blank cell. The plans are plans= (a list, a comma between them) or else the fields of the first row of data= after feature, category and text. The features are the rows of data= - their fields feature, category, text and a field per plan - or {section:item} pairs or single tags, the item's first value the feature, values= its values in the order of the plans (a ; or a line between them, or a list) or the lines of its content, a line a plan; an option of a plan's name gives that plan's value. category= gathers the features under a heading row, in the order the categories first come; text= is a line under the feature's name. featured= names the plan drawn in the accent, badge= a word over its name. The table scrolls sideways in a section too narrow for it, the features' column standing still, so a value is never parted from its feature and its plan.

Downloads

The files to download, a row each: its name, what it is for - the platform, the version, the size, the date - a line about it, its checksum and its button. The files are the rows of data= - their fields name, link, platform, version, size, date, checksum, text, button and featured - or {section:item} pairs or single tags, the item's first value the name and its content the text. A size in bytes - 2457600 - is written as one a person reads, 2.3 MB; another stands as it is. A checksum is the hex digits the publisher gave, the algorithm before them - sha256:9f86... - and shown under a summary of its name to unfold and copy: the section shows what it was given and checks only that the digits are as many as the algorithm makes; it never says a file was verified, which only the one who downloads it can do. One file can be featured - the one most people want, its button in the accent and the others quieter; without one every button is. A file's button is button= ('Download'), named by the file for a screen reader, and shows when it has a link.

Faq

The questions people ask, each a <details> whose <summary> is the question: the browser opens and closes them, no script. The questions are the rows of data= - their fields question and answer - or {section:item} pairs, the item's first value the question and its content the answer. open= opens one at first, by its number from 1, or every one, written bare; single lets one stand open at a time (the details share a name, which a browser that knows exclusive details honours).
